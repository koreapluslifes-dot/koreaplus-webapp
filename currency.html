Korea Does NOT Have a Tipping Culture
Unlike the US, tipping is NOT expected or required in Korea — not at restaurants, hotels, taxis, or salons. Leaving a tip can sometimes cause confusion. The price you see is the price you pay. Tax is always included in menu prices too.
