👜 Lost Items

Lost on public transit: Contact Lost & Found Center: +82-2-6110-1122 (Seoul Metro). Items kept for 30 days.

Lost passport: Report to police (112) first → Get police report → Contact your embassy.

Lost in a taxi: The taxi number is shown on receipts. Call 1330 tourist hotline who can contact the driver.

Lost wallet: Korean society has very low theft. Check the nearest convenience store or station lost & found first — it may have been handed in.

Lost phone: Report to police + call your carrier to block SIM. Korea Police website has English lost property filing.
