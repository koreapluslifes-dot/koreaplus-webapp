Our Mission

Korea is one of the world's most remarkable destinations — a country where 5,000 years of history coexists with cutting-edge technology, where ancient Buddhist temples stand beside K-pop arenas, where the world's most innovative street food culture thrives alongside Michelin-starred restaurants.

KoreaPlus was created to make all of this accessible to every traveler — regardless of language, budget, or prior knowledge. We combine real-time data from Korea's official tourism APIs with AI-powered itinerary generation to give you the most up-to-date, personalised Korea travel experience available anywhere online.

We believe travel should be for everyone. That's why KoreaPlus is, and always will be, completely free.
