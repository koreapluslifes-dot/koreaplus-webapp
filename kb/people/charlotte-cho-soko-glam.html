Charlotte Cho and the K-Beauty Boom in the West

Charlotte Cho is a Korean American esthetician, author and entrepreneur best known as a co-founder of the Korean beauty retailer Soko Glam and the author of a popular guide to Korean skincare.

Her public work

Charlotte Cho co-founded Soko Glam, a U.S.-based online retailer that curates and sells Korean beauty products for the English-speaking market. She is publicly known for writing 'The Little Book of Skin Care: Korean Beauty Secrets for Healthy, Glowing Skin,' published in 2015, which walked readers through the multi-step Korean routine. She is also publicly described as a licensed esthetician, lending hands-on credibility to her educational content, and she later launched her own skincare line.

Why she matters

In the mid-2010s, as interest in Korean skincare surged in the United States, Cho became one of the most visible English-language explainers of K-beauty concepts such as double cleansing, essences and layering. Her retail platform and book helped translate Korean routines and terminology for newcomers, making her a frequently cited figure when people trace how K-beauty crossed over into mainstream Western awareness.

Key facts
  • Co-founder of the Korean beauty retailer Soko Glam
  • Author of 'The Little Book of Skin Care' (2015)
  • Publicly described as a licensed esthetician
  • Helped popularize the multi-step Korean routine in English
  • A leading English-language voice during the mid-2010s K-beauty boom
