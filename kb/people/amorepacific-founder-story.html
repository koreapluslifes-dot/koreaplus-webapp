How Amorepacific Began

Amorepacific is one of the oldest and largest cosmetics companies in South Korea, and its founding story is closely tied to the early commercialization of Korean beauty products.

Company-level origins

Amorepacific's corporate history is generally traced to a business founded by Suh Sung-whan in 1945, which grew out of an earlier family enterprise associated with hand-pressed camellia-oil products. Over the following decades the company professionalized cosmetics manufacturing in Korea and built a research and distribution base. Much of the brand storytelling emphasizes Korean botanical ingredients, including ginseng and green tea, which later became signatures of its prestige lines. We describe these origins broadly because the precise early details are part of company-told history.

From local maker to global group

By the late 20th and early 21st centuries the company had restructured into a modern group with multiple brands and an international footprint. Amorepacific is often credited, alongside its main domestic rival, with building the manufacturing and research infrastructure that allowed Korean cosmetics to compete globally. We describe this at the company level rather than attributing specific milestones to individuals, since corporate timelines are the most reliably documented part of the story.

Key facts
  • Amorepacific traces its corporate roots to 1945
  • Founded by Suh Sung-whan, growing from an earlier family business
  • Early products were associated with camellia-oil hair and skin care
  • Korean botanicals like ginseng and green tea became brand signatures
  • Now one of Korea's two largest cosmetics groups
