Suh Kyung-bae and the Modern Amorepacific

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Who is Suh Kyung-bae? The public chairman role behind Amorepacific, Korea's largest cosmetics group and home to brands like Sulwhasoo, Laneige and Innisfree.

Suh Kyung-bae is the chairman and chief executive of Amorepacific Group, the largest cosmetics company in South Korea and one of the most influential forces in the global spread of K-beauty.

His public role

Suh Kyung-bae leads Amorepacific Group as chairman and CEO. The company traces its roots to a business founded by his father, Suh Sung-whan, in 1945, and Suh Kyung-bae took the helm of the modern group in the late 1990s, becoming chief executive around 1997. Under his leadership the company expanded from a domestic Korean manufacturer into a multinational beauty house with a portfolio that includes prestige and mass-market brands. He is widely cited in business media as one of South Korea's most prominent business leaders, and in 2017 he appeared on Harvard Business Review's ranking of the world's best-performing CEOs, which weighs long-run financial and other measures over a leader's tenure.

Why he matters to K-beauty

Amorepacific is frequently described as one of the companies that helped take Korean cosmetics global. Its house of brands spans luxury herbal skincare, hydration-focused lines, and accessible naturals, several of which became gateway products for consumers outside Korea discovering Korean routines. Suh's tenure coincided with K-beauty's international breakout in the 2010s, making his stewardship a useful lens for understanding how a national industry scaled worldwide. We focus on his documented public and professional role rather than private biographical details.

Key facts
  • Chairman and CEO of Amorepacific Group, South Korea's largest cosmetics company
  • Amorepacific was founded by his father, Suh Sung-whan, in 1945
  • He became chief executive of the modern group in the late 1990s (around 1997)
  • Amorepacific's brand portfolio includes Sulwhasoo, Laneige and Innisfree
  • Appeared on Harvard Business Review's 2017 list of best-performing CEOs
