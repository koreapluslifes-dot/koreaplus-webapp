For reactive & easily-stung skin, a Korean sunscreen earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream SPF50+ PA++++, SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus SPF50+ PA++++, SKIN1004 Hyalu-Cica Water-Fit Sun Serum SPF50+ PA++++ and 4 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream SPF50+ PA++++ $18-26
Dry and normal skin, sensitive skin that tolerates organic filters, and anyone wearing SPF daily through a cold winter when most formulas feel drying. It layers under makeup without pilling in most hands. Oily skin will find it too comfortable by afternoon. Deeper skin tones should get no cast from it, but always check in daylight rather than bathroom lighting.
🌳 Birch Sap🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)
2
SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus SPF50+ PA++++ $18-28
Combination and oily skin, and sensitive skin that gets on with organic filters and wants centella in the same layer. It is a reasonable option for skin that flushes in heat. Very dry skin will want something more emollient underneath it. As with any cica sunscreen, the centella is a supporting act — the filters are the part that protects you.
🌿 Centella (Cica)
3
SKIN1004 Hyalu-Cica Water-Fit Sun Serum SPF50+ PA++++ $18-28
Oily and combination skin, humid climates, and anyone who has abandoned sunscreen over texture alone. It sits well under makeup and reapplies over it more graciously than a cream does. Dry skin will need a moisturiser underneath, since there is very little emollience here. Sensitive skin generally tolerates it, though the fluid texture can travel into the eyes.
💧 Hyaluronic Acid🌿 Centella (Cica)
4
Purito SEOUL Daily Go-To Sunscreen SPF50+ PA++++ $15-22
Fragrance-sensitive and reactive skin, and combination or normal skin that wants a straightforward daily SPF with no skincare story bolted on. It is a sensible option for anyone building a first sunscreen habit. Dry skin will want a moisturiser underneath it. If you specifically need mineral filters for post-procedure skin, follow your clinician's advice rather than a review.
🌿 Centella (Cica)
5
Axis-Y Complete No-Stress Physical Sunscreen SPF50+ PA++++ $18-26
Sensitive and reactive skin, skin that stings under organic filters, and anyone wanting a mineral option without ordering from a pharmacy abroad. It can be a reasonable choice after a gentle in-clinic treatment if your practitioner agrees. Deeper skin tones should expect a cast — that is inherent to inorganic filters, not a fault of this formula. Oily skin often likes the matte finish.
💧 Glycerin
6
COSRX Aloe Soothing Sun Cream SPF50 PA+++ $12-20
Dry and normal skin, sensitive skin after a plain organic-filter option, and anyone who wants one cheap sunscreen for face and body. It is comfortable under makeup on drier skin. Oily skin will find it slightly greasy by afternoon. If you specifically want the highest UVA rating available, choose a PA++++ formula instead of this one.
🪴 Aloe Vera
7
Dr.G Green Mild Up Sun+
Sensitive and normal skin that wants daily protection it will not resent applying. It suits redness-prone faces well and sits reasonably under makeup. Very oily skin may want something drier-finishing; very dry skin may want a cream underneath. Anyone who has reacted to a specific UV filter before should read the current filter list on the pack rather than trusting the word "mild".

Why is Korean sunscreen considered better?

Korean sunscreens are often praised for elegant, lightweight textures that feel pleasant enough to wear daily, which encourages consistent use. They frequently use newer filters approved in Korea but not the US. Preferences are partly cosmetic; effectiveness depends on applying enough and reapplying regardless of brand.

Why is Korean sunscreen better?

Because Korea approves modern UV filters — Uvinul A Plus, Tinosorb S and M among them — that give strong UVA protection in light, non-greasy textures. Some markets, notably the US, have not approved them.
