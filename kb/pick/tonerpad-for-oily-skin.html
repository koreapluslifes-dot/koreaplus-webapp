Best Korean toner pad for oily skin

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
COSRX One Step Original Clear Pad $18-26
Oily and blemish-prone skin that wants a low-effort exfoliating step, and anyone who travels enough that a bottle is a nuisance. Good for the T-zone specifically. Dry and sensitive skin will usually find an acid plus a physical sweep too much across the whole face — treating just the nose and chin is the safer approach.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)
2
Abib Heartleaf Spot Pad Calming Touch $18-26
Sensitive, flushed and blemish-prone skin that wants a calming step without acids, and anyone whose skin is currently unhappy from over-exfoliating. It is one of the few pads safe to use daily. If your goal is blackheads or rough texture, this is the wrong product — it contains nothing that exfoliates and is not trying to.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)
3
Medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0
Oily and combination skin with a genuinely congested T-zone that tolerates acids well. It is a strong pad by the standards of the format, so it suits people who have already used a leave-on BHA without trouble. Dry, sensitive and reactive skin should not start here — a single-acid liquid at a low frequency is a far more controllable place to begin.
🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic)🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone)
4
Anua Heartleaf 77% Clear Pad $20-28
Combination and oily skin that flushes easily and wants a gentle daily clarifying step rather than acids. It suits blemish-prone skin that finds exfoliating pads too provoking. Very dry skin has little to gain here. If your main complaint is blackheads or rough texture, an acid liquid does more than any heartleaf pad will.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)

What is a Korean toner pad?

A pre-soaked cotton or embossed pad delivering a measured dose of toner, or of a mild exfoliating or soothing treatment. It removes the guesswork about how much product to use.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.
