Best Korean water cleanser (gel / foam): the ones worth knowing

Choosing a Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) comes down to what it is formulated around and whether the texture suits your skin. The picks below are COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser, COSRX Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser, Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser and 14 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser $10-15
Oily and combination skin that wants a morning cleanse which does not strip, and anyone whose skin has been irritated by high-pH foaming washes. It is a reasonable second cleanse in the evening too. Dry and very sensitive skin may still find it slightly brisk and be happier with a milk or cream cleanser, particularly in winter.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🌱 Tea Tree
2
COSRX Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser $10-15
Oily, congested and blemish-prone skin that wants something more substantial than a low-foam gel without going back to a stripping bar. Works as an evening second cleanse and, for very oily skin, a morning wash. Dry, sensitive or currently over-exfoliated skin should skip it — there is nothing here it needs, and the foaming will not help.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)
3
Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser $12-18
Combination and normal skin looking for an everyday second cleanse that does not strip, and people building a first routine who want the low-pH box ticked without overthinking it. Oily skin will find it adequate but not bracing. If you have dry or reactive skin, a milk or cream cleanser is a more comfortable choice than any gel, including this one.
4
Round Lab 1025 Dokdo Cleanser $12-18
Combination, normal and mildly sensitive skin that wants something dependable twice a day. It is a particularly good default for people whose skin has been irritated by stronger cleansers and who need a reset. Very dry skin may still want a cream or milk cleanser in winter, and skin with heavy congestion will not find any treatment benefit here.
5
ETUDE SoonJung pH 6.5 Whip Cleanser $10-16
Sensitive, easily flushed and over-exfoliated skin that reacts to almost everything else. It is one of the safest cleansers to recommend blind, and a sensible morning wash for dry skin. Oily and congested skin can use it but will likely find it too mild to feel like it has done anything, which is a perception problem more than a performance one.
🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside
6
Pyunkang Yul Low pH Pore Deep Cleansing Foam $10-16
Combination and oily skin that wants the satisfaction of a real lather without the tightness that usually comes with it. Works as an evening second cleanse for most skin types. Dry and sensitive skin will probably find it more than they need — a low-foam gel or a milk cleanser is kinder, particularly in cold weather.
7
Isntree Yam Root Vegan Milk Cleanser $16-22
Dry, dehydrated and sensitive skin, and anyone whose barrier is currently unhappy from too much exfoliation. A good morning cleanser for skin that does not want to foam before breakfast. Oily and congested skin usually finds milk cleansers unsatisfying and will get on better with a low-pH gel or a light foam.
8
Some By Mi AHA-BHA-PHA 30 Days Miracle Acne Clear Foam $12-18
Oily and blemish-prone skin that wants a decisive evening wash and is already using proper leave-on treatment elsewhere. It is a reasonable second cleanse after an oil or balm. Dry, sensitive or currently irritated skin should stay away — three acid types plus tea tree in a foaming base is more provocation than benefit for reactive skin.
🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic)🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone)🌱 Tea Tree
9
Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Cleansing Foam $12-18
Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and anyone using the rest of the DIVE-IN range who wants consistency across the routine. It is gentle enough for most sensitive skin. Oily and congested skin will find it perfectly usable but underwhelming — there is no oil-control angle here, and nothing addressing blackheads.
💧 Hyaluronic Acid
10
Anua Heartleaf Quercetinol Pore Deep Cleansing Foam $14-20
Oily and combination skin that wants a proper wash after an oil cleanse, especially anyone already using the Heartleaf oil and toner and wanting the range to hang together. It suits blemish-prone skin reasonably well. Dry and sensitive skin will do better with a milk or cream cleanser — the foam is mild, but it is still a foam.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)
11
Dear, Klairs Rich Moist Foaming Cleanser $16-22
Dry and sensitive skin that still wants the ritual of a foaming wash without the tight, stripped after-feel. A sound morning cleanser and a reliable second cleanse at night. Oily and heavily congested skin will find it too soft to feel like it has done the job and will be better served by a brisker low-pH foam.
12
Axis-Y Sunday Morning Refreshing Cleansing Foam $14-20
Combination and normal skin looking for an unfussy twice-daily cleanser, and existing Axis-Y users who want the routine to come from one place. Oily skin will find it adequate as a second cleanse. Dry and sensitive skin should look at a milk or cream cleanser instead — this is mild but it is still a foaming surfactant wash.
13
Sulwhasoo Gentle Cleansing Foam
Dry and mature skin that treats cleansing as part of the pleasure of a routine and wants the scent and texture to match the rest of a Sulwhasoo shelf. It is genuinely comfortable and does not strip. If you are choosing between spending on this or on a treatment step, the treatment step is where the money does more.
14
Innisfree Volcanic Pore Cleansing Foam $10-16
Genuinely oily skin — the sort that is shining by lunchtime — and combination skin that needs a firmer hand on the T-zone in summer. It suits congestion-prone skin as an evening second cleanse. Dry, sensitive or dehydrated skin should avoid it outright; the matte finish that oily skin enjoys is the same thing dry skin experiences as tightness.
15
Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Foam
Reactive, easily-flushed skin that finds standard foaming cleansers leave a tight, shiny feeling afterwards. It works for combination and acne-prone skin too. Very dry skin may prefer a cream or milk cleanser. If your skin feels fine with whatever you already use, there is no argument for switching — cleansers are the step where upgrading changes the least.
🌿 Centella (Cica)
16
Pyunkang Yul ATO Wash & Shampoo Blue Label
Dry, tight, itchy-feeling skin that gets worse after showering, and households that want one bottle for everyone. It suits children's skin, again with a clinician's input if there is a diagnosis. People with oily scalps or heavy styling-product use will find it does not clean thoroughly enough, and anyone with a diagnosed scalp condition needs a medicated shampoo, not this.
17
Aromatica Rosemary Scalp Scaling Shampoo $18-26
Oily or buildup-prone scalps, people who use a lot of dry shampoo or styling product, and anyone whose scalp feels heavy by the second day. It suits colour-treated hair better than sulfate shampoos do. Dry, coarse or curly hair will find the length left thirsty. If your scalp itches, flakes persistently or is sore, see a dermatologist rather than shopping.
