Best Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) for pores & texture

⏱ 3 min read
⚡ Quick answer: For pores & texture, a Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser, COSRX Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser, Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser and 4 more.

For pores & texture, a Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser, COSRX Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser, Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser and 4 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser $10-15
Oily and combination skin that wants a morning cleanse which does not strip, and anyone whose skin has been irritated by high-pH foaming washes. It is a reasonable second cleanse in the evening too. Dry and very sensitive skin may still find it slightly brisk and be happier with a milk or cream cleanser, particularly in winter.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🌱 Tea Tree
2
COSRX Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser $10-15
Oily, congested and blemish-prone skin that wants something more substantial than a low-foam gel without going back to a stripping bar. Works as an evening second cleanse and, for very oily skin, a morning wash. Dry, sensitive or currently over-exfoliated skin should skip it — there is nothing here it needs, and the foaming will not help.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)
3
Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser $12-18
Combination and normal skin looking for an everyday second cleanse that does not strip, and people building a first routine who want the low-pH box ticked without overthinking it. Oily skin will find it adequate but not bracing. If you have dry or reactive skin, a milk or cream cleanser is a more comfortable choice than any gel, including this one.
4
Pyunkang Yul Low pH Pore Deep Cleansing Foam $10-16
Combination and oily skin that wants the satisfaction of a real lather without the tightness that usually comes with it. Works as an evening second cleanse for most skin types. Dry and sensitive skin will probably find it more than they need — a low-foam gel or a milk cleanser is kinder, particularly in cold weather.
5
Anua Heartleaf Quercetinol Pore Deep Cleansing Foam $14-20
Oily and combination skin that wants a proper wash after an oil cleanse, especially anyone already using the Heartleaf oil and toner and wanting the range to hang together. It suits blemish-prone skin reasonably well. Dry and sensitive skin will do better with a milk or cream cleanser — the foam is mild, but it is still a foam.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)
6
Axis-Y Sunday Morning Refreshing Cleansing Foam $14-20
Combination and normal skin looking for an unfussy twice-daily cleanser, and existing Axis-Y users who want the routine to come from one place. Oily skin will find it adequate as a second cleanse. Dry and sensitive skin should look at a milk or cream cleanser instead — this is mild but it is still a foaming surfactant wash.
7
Innisfree Volcanic Pore Cleansing Foam $10-16
Genuinely oily skin — the sort that is shining by lunchtime — and combination skin that needs a firmer hand on the T-zone in summer. It suits congestion-prone skin as an evening second cleanse. Dry, sensitive or dehydrated skin should avoid it outright; the matte finish that oily skin enjoys is the same thing dry skin experiences as tightness.
