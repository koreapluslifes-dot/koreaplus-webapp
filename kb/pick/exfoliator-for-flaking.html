Best Korean exfoliator for flaking & peeling

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: For flaking & peeling, a Korean exfoliator earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are AMOREPACIFIC Treatment Enzyme Peel, Dr.G Brightening Peeling Gel, Dear, Klairs Gentle Black Sugar Facial Polish.

For flaking & peeling, a Korean exfoliator earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are AMOREPACIFIC Treatment Enzyme Peel, Dr.G Brightening Peeling Gel, Dear, Klairs Gentle Black Sugar Facial Polish.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
AMOREPACIFIC Treatment Enzyme Peel
Normal, combination and dry skin dealing with a rough or dull surface that finds acids too provoking. Enzyme exfoliation is milder than acid exfoliation and easier to fit alongside retinoids, so it suits people already running an active routine. Very oily or heavily congested skin will get more from a BHA. It is usable on sensitive skin, but start once a week.
2
Dr.G Brightening Peeling Gel $18-25
Normal, combination and dry skin that finds acid exfoliants stinging and wants something less demanding once or twice a week. It is a reasonable first exfoliant for a nervous beginner. Blemish-prone skin should be cautious — rubbing over active spots is not helpful. Anyone using retinoids or acids already does not need this on top.
3
Dear, Klairs Gentle Black Sugar Facial Polish $18-25
Dry and flaky skin that wants a physical smoothing before makeup, and people who simply prefer a scrub and will not be talked into acids. The dissolving granules make it one of the safer options in the format. It is not for blemish-prone or sensitive skin, and not for anyone using retinoids, acids or a prescription treatment.
