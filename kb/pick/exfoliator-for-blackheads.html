Best Korean exfoliator for blackheads & sebaceous filaments

For blackheads & sebaceous filaments, a Korean exfoliator earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are Dear, Klairs Gentle Black Sugar Facial Polish, Isntree Chestnut BHA 2% Clear Liquid, COSRX BHA Blackhead Power Liquid.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Dear, Klairs Gentle Black Sugar Facial Polish $18-25
Dry and flaky skin that wants a physical smoothing before makeup, and people who simply prefer a scrub and will not be talked into acids. The dissolving granules make it one of the safer options in the format. It is not for blemish-prone or sensitive skin, and not for anyone using retinoids, acids or a prescription treatment.
2
Isntree Chestnut BHA 2% Clear Liquid $18-25
Oily, congested and blackhead-prone skin, particularly around the nose, chin and forehead. It also helps the rough, bumpy texture that comes with persistent congestion. Dry and sensitive skin should approach carefully or choose a PHA instead. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a doctor before adding any salicylic acid product to a routine.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)
3
COSRX BHA Blackhead Power Liquid $18-25
Oily and combination skin with persistent blackheads and a bumpy surface texture. Because betaine salicylate is milder than straight salicylic acid, it is a sensible first leave-on exfoliant for people who have reacted badly to stronger formulas. Dry and sensitive skin should still start slowly. Ask a doctor before using any BHA in pregnancy.
🧽 BHA (Salicylic)
