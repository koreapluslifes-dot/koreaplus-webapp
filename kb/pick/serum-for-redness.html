How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Anua Azelaic Acid 10 Hyaluron Redness Soothing Serum
Sensitive, combination and blemish-prone skin dealing with the look of persistent redness and lingering marks. It is a reasonable option for people who cannot tolerate acids or retinoids. Anyone with diagnosed rosacea should be having this conversation with a clinician, who can prescribe the higher strengths that the evidence actually rests on. Very dry skin may need extra moisturiser over the top.

Some By Mi Snail Truecica Miracle Repair Serum
Dry, normal and combination skin recovering the look of comfort after a period of over-exfoliation, and sensitive skin that gets on with snail mucin. Good alongside, not instead of, sun protection when you are watching post-blemish marks fade. It is mollusc-derived, so not for vegans. Very oily skin often finds the film heavier than it wants in summer.
🐌 Snail Mucin🍀 Madecassoside🌿 Centella (Cica)
3
Medicube PDRN Pink Peptide Serum
Normal, combination, dry and mature skin curious about the ingredient and comfortable paying for the novelty. It is a decent light hydrating serum with a comfortable finish and no obvious irritants. It is salmon-derived, so not for vegans or anyone avoiding fish-derived ingredients. If your budget is finite, a retinoid and a sunscreen have far more evidence behind them for the look of ageing.
🐟 PDRN (Salmon DNA)🔗 Peptides
4
Dr.Jart+ Cicapair Serum
Sensitive and combination skin that flushes easily, and normal or dry skin that wants a calming layer without weight. It sits well in a routine that uses stronger actives on alternate nights. Anyone already using an inexpensive centella ampoule is unlikely to see a difference worth the price gap. Fragrance-avoidant skin should read the current pack carefully.
🌿 Centella (Cica)🍀 Madecassoside
5
Purito SEOUL Wonder Releaf Centella Serum Unscented
Sensitive, dry and normal skin looking for a soothing layer with slightly more substance than a watery centella ampoule. It suits people building a minimal routine after over-treating their skin. Combination skin can use it year-round. Anyone whose main complaint is congestion or texture will find it beside the point — nothing here exfoliates or resurfaces.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
