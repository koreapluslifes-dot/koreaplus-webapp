Best Korean emulsion / lotion for dryness & dehydration

For dryness & dehydration, a Korean emulsion / lotion earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are ETUDE SoonJung 10-Free Moist Emulsion, Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Lotion, COSRX Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion with Birch Sap and 2 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
ETUDE SoonJung 10-Free Moist Emulsion $14-20
Reactive or barrier-stressed skin, and anyone who finds creams too heavy but a serum not quite enough. It suits oily-but-sensitive skin particularly well and works year-round in humid climates. Very dry skin in winter will want a cream on top. There are no actives in it, which is the point rather than an oversight.
🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside
2
Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Lotion $12-20
Dry, flaky or easily irritated skin that goes through moisturiser quickly, and households wanting one bottle for several people. It works on the face for normal to dry skin. Oily skin may find it unnecessary. If you have diagnosed eczema or atopic dermatitis, this is a supportive daily moisturiser and not a treatment — that is a conversation for your doctor.
🧱 Ceramides🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)
3
COSRX Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion with Birch Sap $15-22
Oily and breakout-prone skin that keeps abandoning moisturiser because everything feels greasy, and combination skin in humid weather. It is also a sensible summer swap for people who use something heavier the rest of the year. Dry skin will find it thin — it hydrates but does not cushion, and in winter you will want a lipid-rich layer sitting on top of it.
🌳 Birch Sap
4
Aestura Atobarrier 365 Lotion
Combination and normal skin that runs dry in patches, and dry skin during humid months. It also suits anyone building a deliberately short routine, since it can be the only moisturising step. Very dry or visibly flaking skin will get more out of the cream. Reactive skin generally tolerates it well, but patch-test on the jaw for a few days like anything else.
🧱 Ceramides
5
Dr.Jart+ Ceramidin Body Lotion
Dry, rough or flaky body skin, especially anyone who showers daily in hard water or spends winter in heated rooms. It suits sensitive skin, though check the current pack if you avoid fragrance entirely. Anyone treating body skin on a budget should compare it honestly with the large-format Korean ceramide lotions, which cost far less per application.
🧱 Ceramides
