Best Korean toner / skin for acne-prone skin

Acne-prone skin narrows a Korean toner / skin down mostly by texture and by what the formula leaves behind. The picks below are Anua Heartleaf 77% Soothing Toner, Isntree Green Tea Fresh Toner, ETUDE SoonJung pH 5.5 Relief Toner and 1 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Anua Heartleaf 77% Soothing Toner $18-25
Best for reactive, flushed or lightly congested skin that wants a hydration step which does not also introduce an active. Oily and combination skin will like how little it leaves behind. Very dry skin will find it thin — treat it as the first of several layers, not the whole job. It is not a spot treatment, and people expecting it to clear breakouts are usually disappointed.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)
2
Isntree Green Tea Fresh Toner $18-25
Oily and combination skin, particularly in summer or a humid climate, and breakout-prone skin already using an exfoliant elsewhere that does not want a second one. Dry or mature skin will find it too slight. Anyone hoping a green tea toner will hold sebum in check across a whole day should temper expectations considerably.
🍃 Green Tea🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)
3
ETUDE SoonJung pH 5.5 Relief Toner $12-18
Reactive, flushed or barrier-compromised skin, and anyone recovering from over-exfoliation. It is also a sound beginner toner for teenage skin that is oily but easily irritated. Very dry skin will find it thin on its own. There is nothing in it for pigmentation, lines or pores, and it makes no pretence otherwise.
🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside
4
SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Toning Toner $15-22
Skin that flushes easily, or breakout-prone skin that cannot tolerate the acids usually aimed at it. Oily and combination skin will like the weightlessness. Dry skin needs something layered over it before long. It is a maintenance product: useful as a daily calm-and-hydrate step, unremarkable if you are shopping for visible change.
🌿 Centella (Cica)

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.

What is a Korean toner pad?

A pre-soaked cotton or embossed pad delivering a measured dose of toner, or of a mild exfoliating or soothing treatment. It removes the guesswork about how much product to use.
