Best Korean serum for combination skin

⏱ 7 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Combination skin narrows a Korean serum down mostly by texture and by what the formula leaves behind. The picks below are COSRX The Vitamin C 23 Serum, Beauty of Joseon Glow Serum: Propolis + Niacinamide, Beauty of Joseon Revive Serum: Ginseng + Snail Mucin and 14 more.

Combination skin narrows a Korean serum down mostly by texture and by what the formula leaves behind. The picks below are COSRX The Vitamin C 23 Serum, Beauty of Joseon Glow Serum: Propolis + Niacinamide, Beauty of Joseon Revive Serum: Ginseng + Snail Mucin and 14 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
COSRX The Vitamin C 23 Serum
For normal, oily or combination skin that already tolerates actives and wants the strongest well-documented form of vitamin C rather than a comfortable derivative. Good for the look of uneven tone, dullness and lingering post-blemish marks. Sensitive, rosacea-prone or barrier-compromised skin should not start here — the lower-strength version of the same serum exists for exactly that reason.
🍊 Vitamin C
2
Beauty of Joseon Glow Serum: Propolis + Niacinamide $15-20
Dry and normal skin wanting dewiness, and combination skin that finds most brightening serums drying. Sensitive skin generally tolerates it, with the caveat that niacinamide makes some people flush. Anyone with a bee-product allergy should skip it entirely. Very oily skin often finds the propolis tack unpleasant, and there are lighter niacinamide serums for that.
🍯 Propolis🧫 Niacinamide (B3)
3
Beauty of Joseon Revive Serum: Ginseng + Snail Mucin $15-22
Dry, normal and mature skin looking for hydration with a bit of bounce, and anyone drawn to the herbal end of Korean skincare. Combination skin can use it at night. Snail mucin is mollusc-derived, so vegans and anyone with a shellfish or mollusc sensitivity should avoid it. Oily skin prone to congestion sometimes finds the film heavy under sunscreen.
🫚 Ginseng🐌 Snail Mucin
4
Beauty of Joseon Glow Deep Serum: Rice + Alpha Arbutin $15-20
Normal, dry and combination skin working on the appearance of post-blemish marks and general unevenness, and a reasonable first step for anyone who found vitamin C too irritating. Oily skin will like the finish. It is not a fast fix for stubborn melasma, and anyone dealing with that should be talking to a clinician rather than shopping — topical brighteners alone rarely settle it.
🍚 Rice Extract🫐 Alpha-Arbutin
5
Anua Peach 70 Niacin Serum
Normal, combination and mildly dry skin that wants a comfortable everyday niacinamide rather than a clinical one. Good for the look of dullness and rough texture. People who flush from niacinamide will flush from this too. If you dislike scented skincare, the peach note is noticeable enough to be a dealbreaker, and fragrance-free niacinamide serums are easy to find.
🧫 Niacinamide (B3)
6
Anua Niacinamide 10% + TXA 4% Serum
Normal, combination and oily skin working on the appearance of post-blemish marks and diffuse unevenness. It suits people who found vitamin C too unstable or too irritating. Very sensitive skin should be careful — ten per cent niacinamide is enough to make some faces warm and pink. Anyone with diagnosed melasma should treat this as a supporting player and see a clinician about the rest.
🧫 Niacinamide (B3)⚪ Tranexamic Acid
7
Anua Azelaic Acid 10 Hyaluron Redness Soothing Serum
Sensitive, combination and blemish-prone skin dealing with the look of persistent redness and lingering marks. It is a reasonable option for people who cannot tolerate acids or retinoids. Anyone with diagnosed rosacea should be having this conversation with a clinician, who can prescribe the higher strengths that the evidence actually rests on. Very dry skin may need extra moisturiser over the top.
🌾 Azelaic Acid💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Some By Mi AHA BHA PHA 30 Days Miracle Serum
Oily, combination and congested skin that wants a mild everyday exfoliating step rather than a weekly strong one. Reasonable for the look of rough texture and visible pores. Dry, sensitive or barrier-compromised skin should stay away, and so should anyone already using a nightly retinoid. If you have inflamed cystic breakouts, this is not the product and a clinician is the better call.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
