Best Korean oil cleanser / cleansing balm for combination skin

Combination skin narrows a Korean oil cleanser / cleansing balm down mostly by texture and by what the formula leaves behind. The picks below are Anua Heartleaf Pore Control Cleansing Oil, Banila Co Clean It Zero Cleansing Balm Original, Banila Co Clean It Zero Cleansing Balm Purifying and 3 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Anua Heartleaf Pore Control Cleansing Oil $18-26
Oily and combination skin that wants makeup and SPF off without a greasy after-feel. It suits congestion-prone skin better than most balms because the texture is light and rinses fully rather than sitting in the folds around the nose. Very dry skin may find it leaves nothing behind and prefer a richer balm. Check the current ingredient list for fragrance before buying if you react easily.
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)
2
Banila Co Clean It Zero Cleansing Balm Original $18-25
Almost everyone, which is exactly why it sells. Dry and normal skin get a comfortable first cleanse that does not strip; anyone wearing base makeup or a proper layer of sunscreen gets a thoroughness gel cleansers cannot match. Oily and blemish-prone skin often prefers the Purifying version. If you wear waterproof mascara daily, a balm like this is the most useful single addition to a routine.
3
Banila Co Clean It Zero Cleansing Balm Purifying $18-25
Oily and combination skin that likes the convenience of a balm but finds the Original leaves things a little too cushioned. Also a sensible pick if you wear sunscreen daily and want it properly off without a foaming cleanser doing all the work. Dry or reactive skin will usually be happier with the Original, which is more comfortable and less brisk on the rinse.
4
Beauty of Joseon Radiance Cleansing Balm $15-20
Normal to dry skin that wants a comfortable first cleanse without a heavy after-feel, and anyone already using the brand's Relief Sun sunscreen who wants something that reliably removes it. Combination skin gets on with it well. If your main concern is oiliness or active congestion, a lighter oil rinses more completely and will serve you better as a nightly first step.
🍚 Rice Extract
5
Hanskin Cleansing Oil & Blackhead PHA $18-25
Oily and combination skin dealing with visible blackheads around the nose and chin who want their first cleanse to feel light. It works well as a daily makeup-and-SPF remover for most skin types that are not actively dry. If your skin is dry or reactive, the acid content is not a problem in itself, but you will likely prefer a balm for comfort.
🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone)
6
SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Light Cleansing Oil $12-18
Sensitive and blemish-prone skin that wants the double-cleanse step without a rich texture sitting on the face. Good for oily and combination skin, and a reasonable choice for people new to oil cleansing who are nervous about congestion. If you wear heavy or waterproof makeup, a proper balm removes it more completely in one pass.
🌿 Centella (Cica)
