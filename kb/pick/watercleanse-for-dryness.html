Best Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) for dryness & dehydration

For dryness & dehydration, a Korean water cleanser (gel / foam) earns its place through what it is built around rather than through the claim on the box. The picks below are Isntree Yam Root Vegan Milk Cleanser, Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Cleansing Foam, Dear, Klairs Rich Moist Foaming Cleanser and 2 more.

How this list is built: products are grouped by what they are formulated around and who they suit, drawn from public information. Order is not a ranking of quality and nothing here is paid placement. Formulas change — read the current ingredient list before you buy.

1
Isntree Yam Root Vegan Milk Cleanser $16-22
Dry, dehydrated and sensitive skin, and anyone whose barrier is currently unhappy from too much exfoliation. A good morning cleanser for skin that does not want to foam before breakfast. Oily and congested skin usually finds milk cleansers unsatisfying and will get on better with a low-pH gel or a light foam.
2
Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Cleansing Foam $12-18
Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and anyone using the rest of the DIVE-IN range who wants consistency across the routine. It is gentle enough for most sensitive skin. Oily and congested skin will find it perfectly usable but underwhelming — there is no oil-control angle here, and nothing addressing blackheads.
💧 Hyaluronic Acid
3
Dear, Klairs Rich Moist Foaming Cleanser $16-22
Dry and sensitive skin that still wants the ritual of a foaming wash without the tight, stripped after-feel. A sound morning cleanser and a reliable second cleanse at night. Oily and heavily congested skin will find it too soft to feel like it has done the job and will be better served by a brisker low-pH foam.
4
Sulwhasoo Gentle Cleansing Foam
Dry and mature skin that treats cleansing as part of the pleasure of a routine and wants the scent and texture to match the rest of a Sulwhasoo shelf. It is genuinely comfortable and does not strip. If you are choosing between spending on this or on a treatment step, the treatment step is where the money does more.
5
Pyunkang Yul ATO Wash & Shampoo Blue Label
Dry, tight, itchy-feeling skin that gets worse after showering, and households that want one bottle for everyone. It suits children's skin, again with a clinician's input if there is a diagnosis. People with oily scalps or heavy styling-product use will find it does not clean thoroughly enough, and anyone with a diagnosed scalp condition needs a medicated shampoo, not this.
