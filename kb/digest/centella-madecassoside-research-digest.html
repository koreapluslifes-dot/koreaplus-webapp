Where the evidence points (as of 2026)

Centella asiatica and its purified components such as madecassoside and madecassic acid are long-standing soothing actives. The general direction of cosmetic evidence suggests they support the appearance of calm, comfortable skin and a resilient-looking barrier. We summarize this established direction without inventing trials or figures. This is education on ingredient trends, not a medical or treatment claim.

Why cica defines Korean soothing care

Cica is practically synonymous with K-beauty's barrier-first philosophy. Brands like COSRX, SKIN1004 (Madagascar Centella), and Dr.Jart popularized it for reactive, post-active, or stressed-looking skin. It is commonly paired with panthenol, ceramides, and heartleaf (Houttuynia) to support a soothed, even glass-skin look, and it appears across toners, ampoules, and barrier creams.

How to read new claims sensibly

Because cica is marketed as 'calming,' look for cosmetic wording rather than medical claims about treating conditions. Extract quality and the specific actives (madecassoside vs. crude extract) vary by product. Cica pairs well as a buffer alongside stronger actives like retinal or acids, supporting comfort in a layered Korean routine; patch-test if highly reactive.
