Morning shed: viral claim vs the evidence

💨 More hype than proof

Viral claim: "Pile on overnight layers, then peel off a “transformed” face at dawn."

Mostly theatrical — overnight masks and patches do hydrate, but the dramatic reveal is a look, not a lasting skin upgrade. Heavy layering can clog or irritate some skin.
