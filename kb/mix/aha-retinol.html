Can I use Retinol and AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) together?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Can you use Retinol and AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) together in a Korean routine? Verdict: usable with care. Both increase irritation & sensitivity. Alternate on different nights rather than layering.

Can I use vitamin C and retinol together?

Not in the same layer, but easily in the same routine — vitamin C in the morning, retinol at night. That split suits both: vitamin C partners with sunscreen, and retinoids are degraded by light.

What is the difference between AHA and BHA?

AHAs like glycolic and lactic acid are water-soluble and work on the skin's surface to brighten and smooth, while BHA like salicylic acid is oil-soluble and gets into pores. In Korean skincare both are used sparingly, a few times weekly, always with daily sun protection.

Can you use vitamin C and niacinamide together?

Yes, current understanding is that vitamin C and niacinamide can be used together safely, and many Korean formulas combine them. The old warning that they cancel out is largely outdated. If your skin is sensitive, you can still apply them at different times of day to be cautious.

Can I mix Korean and Western skincare?

Yes. Products do not care where they were made — what matters is the ingredients and the order you apply them. Layer thinnest to thickest, avoid stacking two exfoliating actives, and introduce anything new one at a time.
