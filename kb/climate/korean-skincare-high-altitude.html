Combat dryness with deep hydration layering

At high altitude, the air is thinner and drier, pulling moisture from skin and leaving it tight and dehydrated. Korean hydration layering is the perfect counter. Press in several thin layers of a watery hydrating toner, then build essences like snail mucin and hyaluronic acid to draw and hold moisture. Rice and propolis essences from Beauty of Joseon add nourishment that keeps elevation-stressed skin plump and supple.

Soothe a stressed barrier

Lower oxygen, dry air, and temperature swings can leave skin reactive and red-looking at altitude. Korea's calming botanicals — centella (cica), heartleaf, and panthenol — help skin feel comforted and balanced. Ceramides reinforce the barrier against the heightened moisture loss elevation causes. Antioxidant-rich essences like green tea and vitamin C support defense against environmental stress, keeping skin resilient in demanding mountain conditions.

Prioritize strong sun protection

UV intensity rises significantly with altitude — roughly increasing the higher you climb — making sunscreen absolutely critical. Apply a broad-spectrum, high-SPF Korean sunscreen generously every morning and reapply often, especially on snow or open terrain where glare reflects more UV. Pair it with a hat and seek shade. Diligent protection plus deep hydration is the formula for keeping high-altitude skin healthy-looking, calm, and glowy.
