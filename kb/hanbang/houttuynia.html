Houttuynia (Eoseongcho): Korea's hanbang heritage ingredient 어성초

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Houttuynia (Eoseongcho) (어성초, eoseongcho) is a hanbang botanical Korea has used in traditional skincare for generations. One of the biggest K-beauty ingredient stories of recent years, driven by brands building entire lines around high-percentage heartleaf extract for blemish-prone and reactive skin.

Named for its distinctive fishy smell — 어성 means fish smell — houttuynia has a long folk-medicine history in Korea and across East Asia, traditionally used in preparations for skin complaints.

🗣️ Houttuynia (Eoseongcho) — Hangul 어성초, romanized eoseongcho, say "uh-SUNG-choh".
