Angelica gigas (Korean Angelica): Korea's hanbang heritage ingredient 당귀

⚡ Quick answer: Angelica gigas (Korean Angelica) (당귀, danggui) is a hanbang botanical Korea has used in traditional skincare for generations. K-beauty uses angelica-root extract as a botanical antioxidant in nourishing and anti-ageing formulations, positioned to help skin look revitalised and supple. Cosmetic positioning emphasises a healthy, well-cared-for appearance.

Korean angelica (Angelica gigas, danggui) is a classic root in traditional Korean medicine documented in hanbang materia medica and the Donguibogam, where angelica roots were valued as nourishing 'blood-tonic' herbs in herbal formulas.

🗣️ Angelica gigas (Korean Angelica) — Hangul 당귀, romanized danggui, say "DAHNG-gwee".
