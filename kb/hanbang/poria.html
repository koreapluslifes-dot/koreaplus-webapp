Poria (Bokryeong): Korea's hanbang heritage ingredient 복령

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Poria (Bokryeong) (복령, bongnyeong) is a hanbang botanical Korea has used in traditional skincare for generations. Used in premium hanbang skincare as part of traditional herbal complexes, often in lines that name their formulation lineage explicitly.

A fungus that grows on pine roots, poria is a classic component of Korean hanbang formulations and appears in the Donguibogam among preparations associated with balance and calm.

🗣️ Poria (Bokryeong) — Hangul 복령, romanized bongnyeong, say "BONG-nyung".
