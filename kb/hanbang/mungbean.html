Mung Bean: Korea's hanbang heritage ingredient 녹두

⚡ Quick answer: Mung Bean (녹두, nokdu) is a hanbang botanical Korea has used in traditional skincare for generations. In contemporary K-beauty, mung-bean extract or powder features in cleansing and clarifying products positioned to help skin feel clean and look fresh and balanced. Claims focus on gentle cleansing and a clarified appearance.

Mung bean (nokdu) has a long place in Korean food culture and traditional grooming, where mung-bean powder was used as a gentle cleansing and clarifying agent for the skin in folk beauty practice.

🗣️ Mung Bean — Hangul 녹두, romanized nokdu, say "NOK-doo".
