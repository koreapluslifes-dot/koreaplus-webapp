Goji Berry: Korea's hanbang heritage ingredient 구기자

⚡ Quick answer: Goji Berry (구기자, gugija) is a hanbang botanical Korea has used in traditional skincare for generations. Goji-berry extract appears in K-beauty antioxidant and anti-ageing serums and creams, marketed to help skin look revitalised and protected against the appearance of dullness. Claims remain antioxidant and cosmetic.

Goji berry (gugija, the fruit of Lycium) is a classic tonic fruit in traditional Korean and East Asian medicine, documented in hanbang materia medica and valued as a nourishing, antioxidant-rich botanical in herbal tradition.

🗣️ Goji Berry — Hangul 구기자, romanized gugija, say "goo-GEE-jah".
