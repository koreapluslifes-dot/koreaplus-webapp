Kolmar Korea

Kolmar Korea is a leading South Korean ODM manufacturer of cosmetics and related products, supplying formulations and finished goods to a wide range of beauty brands.

Overview

Kolmar Korea is a South Korean company focused on the original development manufacturing (ODM) of cosmetics, and it is also active in pharmaceutical and health-related manufacturing. Like Cosmax, it operates primarily as a behind-the-scenes producer, developing and manufacturing products that client brands market under their own names. It is regarded as one of the major Korean contract manufacturers in the beauty sector.

ODM model

Kolmar Korea's ODM approach means it not only manufactures but also develops formulations and product concepts that brands can adopt. This lowers the barrier for companies wanting to launch cosmetics, contributing to the dynamism and variety of the Korean beauty market. The company is often mentioned alongside Cosmax as one of the dominant Korean cosmetics manufacturers.

Scope

Beyond cosmetics, Kolmar Korea's broader operations have included pharmaceutical and health-supplement manufacturing, giving it a diversified manufacturing base. The company is publicly traded in South Korea. Its name reflects historical ties to the international 'Kolmar' manufacturing tradition, though the Korean entity operates as its own business.

Key facts
  • South Korean ODM cosmetics manufacturer
  • Also active in pharmaceutical and health-product manufacturing
  • Commonly cited alongside Cosmax as a leading Korean contract manufacturer
  • Develops formulations as well as producing finished goods
  • Publicly listed in South Korea
