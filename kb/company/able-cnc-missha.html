Able C&C (Missha)

Able C&C is a South Korean cosmetics company best known as the operator of Missha, one of the brands that helped popularize affordable, accessible K-beauty.

Overview

Able C&C is a South Korean cosmetics company whose flagship brand is Missha. The company became prominent in the 2000s during the rise of Korean 'brand shop' retailing, where single-brand stores offered cosmetics directly to consumers, often emphasizing value and affordability. Able C&C is publicly traded in South Korea.

Missha

Missha is widely associated with bringing affordable, trend-driven skincare and makeup to a broad audience. The brand became internationally known in connection with the popularity of BB creams, a product category that was popularized and refined in Korea before spreading globally. Missha's positioning has long emphasized offering effective products at accessible price points.

Other brands

Beyond Missha, Able C&C has operated additional beauty brands over time, expanding its portfolio in the value and mid-market segments. The company is often cited as part of the wave of Korean brand-shop companies that, alongside others, helped make K-beauty broadly accessible to consumers both in Korea and abroad.

Key facts
  • South Korean cosmetics company behind the Missha brand
  • Rose to prominence with Korea's single-brand 'brand shop' retail model
  • Missha is associated with the popularization of BB creams
  • Positioned around affordable, accessible cosmetics
  • Publicly listed in South Korea
