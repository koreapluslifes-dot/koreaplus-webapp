Olive Young (CJ Olive Young)

⚡ Quick answer: Olive Young is South Korea's leading health-and-beauty retail chain, operated by CJ Olive Young, part of the CJ Group.

Olive Young is South Korea's leading health-and-beauty store chain, operated by CJ Olive Young, a company within the CJ Group, and a key gateway for K-beauty brands.

Overview

Olive Young is the dominant health-and-beauty (drugstore-style) retail chain in South Korea, operated by CJ Olive Young, part of the large South Korean CJ Group. Unlike the cosmetics manufacturers and brands, Olive Young's role is retail: it curates and sells a wide assortment of beauty, skincare and personal-care products from many brands.

Role in K-beauty

Olive Young is widely regarded as a major launchpad and barometer for K-beauty brands. Securing placement and strong sales at Olive Young can significantly raise a brand's profile, and the chain is often cited as a key channel through which both established and indie Korean brands reach consumers. Its store network spans Korea extensively.

Reach beyond stores

In addition to physical stores, Olive Young has expanded online and into global e-commerce, helping international shoppers access Korean beauty products. This combination of broad domestic retail dominance and growing cross-border sales makes it a structurally important player in the K-beauty ecosystem.

Key facts
  • South Korea's leading health-and-beauty retail chain
  • Operated by CJ Olive Young, part of the CJ Group
  • A retailer, not a manufacturer or single brand
  • Considered a major launchpad for K-beauty brands
  • Expanded into online and global e-commerce
