Cosmax

Cosmax is one of the world's largest cosmetics ODM/OEM companies, a South Korean manufacturer that develops and produces products for many beauty brands rather than selling chiefly under its own name.

Overview

Cosmax is a South Korean company specializing in original development manufacturing (ODM) and original equipment manufacturing (OEM) of cosmetics. Rather than being a consumer-facing brand, it operates largely behind the scenes, formulating and producing skincare and color cosmetics that other companies sell under their own labels. It is widely described as one of the largest cosmetics manufacturers of this type globally.

Role in the industry

Cosmax is a cornerstone of the K-beauty supply chain. Because many beauty brands, both Korean and international, do not own their own factories, contract manufacturers like Cosmax handle research, formulation and large-scale production. This model has helped enable the rapid proliferation of K-beauty brands and indie labels, since a new brand can launch products without building manufacturing capacity from scratch.

Reach

Cosmax operates manufacturing and research operations in multiple countries and serves a broad international client base. Its scale and formulation expertise make it an influential, if often less publicly visible, force in shaping cosmetic trends and bringing new product types to market. The company is publicly traded in South Korea.

Key facts
  • South Korean cosmetics ODM/OEM (contract) manufacturer
  • One of the largest cosmetics manufacturers of its kind globally
  • Produces products sold under many other brands' labels
  • Key enabler of the K-beauty supply chain and indie brands
  • Publicly listed in South Korea
