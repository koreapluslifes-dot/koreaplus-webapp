Innisfree

Innisfree is a South Korean cosmetics brand owned by Amorepacific, built around a nature-inspired identity and ingredients associated with Korea's Jeju Island.

Overview

Innisfree is a popular South Korean beauty brand under the Amorepacific group. It is positioned around a natural, eco-friendly image and is well known for marketing ingredients linked to Jeju Island, such as green tea and volcanic clay. The brand operates in the accessible, mass-to-mid price segment.

Brand concept

Innisfree's identity emphasizes naturally derived ingredients and a clean, green aesthetic in both its products and store design. It became one of the more internationally recognized single brands of the K-beauty era, with standalone stores in Korea and various overseas markets. Green-tea-based skincare has been among its signature offerings.

Role in K-beauty

As part of Amorepacific's multi-brand strategy, Innisfree helped carry the nature-focused, ingredient-driven storytelling that characterizes much of K-beauty to global consumers. It is frequently cited as an example of how Korean brands package traditional and local ingredients into modern, widely marketed product lines.

Key facts
  • South Korean cosmetics brand owned by Amorepacific
  • Built around a natural, eco-friendly brand identity
  • Markets ingredients associated with Jeju Island, like green tea
  • Operated standalone stores in Korea and abroad
  • Part of Amorepacific's multi-brand portfolio
