Dr.Jart+ (Have & Be)

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Dr.Jart+ is a South Korean dermatological-style skincare brand; its parent company Have & Be is owned by Estée Lauder.

Dr.Jart+ is a South Korean skincare brand with a dermatological, science-oriented image, operated by the company Have & Be, which became part of the Estée Lauder Companies.

Overview

Dr.Jart+ is a South Korean cosmetics brand known for a dermatological, problem-solving approach to skincare. Its parent company is Have & Be Co. The brand's name and positioning emphasize a blend of skincare science and accessible products, fitting within the 'derma' or cosmeceutical-leaning corner of K-beauty.

BB cream association

Dr.Jart+ is often associated with the international popularization of BB creams. BB (blemish balm) cream as a concept originated outside Korea, in Germany, but was popularized and refined within the Korean beauty industry, and Dr.Jart+ was among the brands that helped introduce the category to Western consumers.

Estée Lauder ownership

Have & Be, the company behind Dr.Jart+, became part of the Estée Lauder Companies, a major global beauty group, making Dr.Jart+ a notable example of a Korean brand acquired by a large Western conglomerate. This reflects the growing global interest in K-beauty brands as strategic assets.

Key facts
  • South Korean skincare brand with a dermatological image
  • Operated by parent company Have & Be Co.
  • Helped popularize BB creams in Western markets
  • BB cream originated in Germany but was popularized in Korea
  • Became part of the Estée Lauder Companies
