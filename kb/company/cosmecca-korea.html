Cosmecca Korea

⚡ Quick answer: Cosmecca Korea is a South Korean ODM/OEM cosmetics manufacturer supplying products to domestic and global brands.

Cosmecca Korea is a South Korean cosmetics ODM/OEM manufacturer that develops and produces beauty products for a range of domestic and international brands.

Overview

Cosmecca Korea is a South Korean company that operates in the cosmetics contract-manufacturing space, providing ODM and OEM services. As with other Korean manufacturers of this kind, its core business is formulating and producing cosmetics that client brands sell under their own labels rather than building a large consumer-facing brand of its own.

Role in K-beauty

Cosmecca Korea is part of the cluster of Korean contract manufacturers, alongside larger peers such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, that underpin the K-beauty industry's ability to launch products quickly and at scale. It is commonly cited as a specialist in base-makeup formats, including BB and cushion-type products — categories closely associated with the rise of K-beauty — and supplies both Korean and international brands.

Position

While smaller and less internationally recognized by consumers than the biggest manufacturers, Cosmecca Korea is an established name within the industry supply chain. It is publicly traded in South Korea. Its presence illustrates how a competitive ecosystem of Korean manufacturers helps sustain the steady output of new beauty products characteristic of the K-beauty market.

Key facts
  • South Korean ODM/OEM cosmetics manufacturer
  • Known as a specialist in base makeup such as BB and cushion products
  • Produces products for domestic and international brands
  • Part of the Korean contract-manufacturing ecosystem behind K-beauty
  • Publicly listed in South Korea
