Laneige

⚡ Quick answer: Laneige is a South Korean skincare brand owned by Amorepacific, internationally known for hydration products.

Laneige is a South Korean cosmetics brand under Amorepacific, internationally recognized for hydration-focused skincare such as sleeping masks and lip-care products.

Overview

Laneige is a popular South Korean skincare brand owned by the Amorepacific group. Its name derives from the French word for snow, reflecting a brand identity centered on hydration and moisture. It sits in the accessible-premium segment and has achieved strong international recognition.

Signature products

Laneige is especially well known for hydration-focused items, including overnight sleeping masks and lip-care products that have become widely popular internationally. These hero products have helped the brand gain visibility in Western markets and through global beauty retailers.

Role in K-beauty

Laneige is frequently highlighted as one of the K-beauty brands that successfully crossed over to mainstream international audiences. As part of Amorepacific's portfolio, it represents the group's effort to build globally appealing, concept-driven skincare. Its emphasis on hydration aligns with the moisture-centric ethos common in Korean skincare.

Key facts
  • South Korean skincare brand owned by Amorepacific
  • Brand identity centered on hydration and moisture
  • Known internationally for sleeping masks and lip care
  • One of the more globally successful K-beauty brands
  • Name derives from the French word for snow
