COSRX

COSRX is a South Korean skincare brand that built an international following through minimalist, ingredient-focused formulations aimed at common skin concerns.

Overview

COSRX is a South Korean skincare brand that gained popularity, particularly online, for straightforward products centered on well-known functional ingredients. Its no-frills, ingredient-led approach resonated strongly with skincare enthusiasts and communities discussing K-beauty routines.

Product approach

COSRX is associated with targeted products addressing concerns such as blemishes and hydration, often featuring recognizable ingredients in its naming and marketing. This transparency-oriented, function-first style helped distinguish it from more heavily marketed or cosmetically elaborate brands and made it a favorite for routine-building.

Market position

COSRX became one of the K-beauty brands with a notable international footprint, especially via e-commerce and global beauty retailers. In the early 2020s the Amorepacific group built up a stake in COSRX and subsequently made it a majority-owned subsidiary, reflecting how larger Korean cosmetics companies have engaged with successful indie-style brands. Key facts
  • South Korean skincare brand with a strong online following
  • Known for minimalist, ingredient-focused products
  • Popular for targeted blemish and hydration items
  • Built an international footprint largely via e-commerce
  • Became a majority-owned subsidiary of the Amorepacific group in the early 2020s
