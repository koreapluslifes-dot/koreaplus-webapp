Etude House

⚡ Quick answer: Etude House is a South Korean makeup and skincare brand owned by Amorepacific, known for a youthful, playful image.

Etude House is a South Korean cosmetics brand under Amorepacific, recognized for its youthful, playful branding and affordable color makeup and skincare.

Overview

Etude House is a South Korean beauty brand owned by the Amorepacific group. It is widely associated with a sweet, princess-like aesthetic and targets younger consumers with affordable makeup and skincare. The brand became a familiar fixture in Korea's single-brand store retail landscape.

Product focus

Etude House offers a broad range of color cosmetics, such as eyeshadows, lip products and nail items, alongside skincare. Its playful packaging and accessible pricing made it especially popular with teens and young adults. The brand frequently releases trend-driven and collaboration products in line with its fun, youthful positioning.

Role in K-beauty

As part of Amorepacific's stable of brands, Etude House complemented the group's prestige lines by appealing to a younger, value-conscious demographic. It is often cited as one of the brands that helped spread approachable, fashion-forward Korean makeup culture internationally.

Key facts
  • South Korean cosmetics brand owned by Amorepacific
  • Known for youthful, playful branding and packaging
  • Offers affordable color makeup and skincare
  • Popular in Korea's single-brand store retail scene
  • Targets teens and young adults
