Amorepacific Group

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Amorepacific is one of South Korea's largest cosmetics companies, home to Sulwhasoo, Laneige, Innisfree and more.

Amorepacific is one of South Korea's largest and most influential cosmetics and personal-care companies, with a portfolio of brands that helped define the global K-beauty movement.

Overview

Amorepacific is a major South Korean cosmetics conglomerate headquartered in Seoul. It is widely regarded as one of the two dominant players in the Korean beauty industry alongside LG Household & Health Care. The company traces its roots back to the first half of the 20th century, growing from an early Korean cosmetics enterprise into a large multinational group. Over the decades it built a reputation for combining traditional Korean botanical ingredients, such as green tea and ginseng, with modern cosmetic science.

Brand portfolio

Amorepacific owns or operates a broad stable of well-known brands across price tiers. Its prestige lines include Sulwhasoo, known for hanbang (traditional Korean herbal) skincare, and Laneige, recognized internationally for hydration-focused products. The group has also been associated with brands such as Innisfree, Etude House, Hera, Mamonde and IOPE, spanning luxury, mass and lifestyle segments. This multi-brand structure is a hallmark of the company's strategy.

Industry role

Amorepacific is frequently credited as a key force in popularizing K-beauty concepts and rituals, including multi-step skincare routines, internationally. The company operates research facilities and has expanded into markets across Asia, North America and beyond. It is a publicly traded company in South Korea and is often cited in discussions of the global beauty market as a leading representative of the Korean cosmetics sector.

Key facts
  • One of South Korea's largest cosmetics companies, based in Seoul
  • Parent of prestige brands Sulwhasoo and Laneige, among others
  • Strongly associated with traditional Korean botanical ingredients like green tea and ginseng
  • A central player in the international spread of K-beauty
  • Publicly listed in South Korea
