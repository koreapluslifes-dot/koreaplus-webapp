Autumn warm (가을 웜) is Korea's deep, golden warm season for people with rich warm undertones and muted coloring. Autumn warms glow in brick, terracotta, olive, mustard and warm brown. Korean brick-red lips and bronzy eye looks support a warm, sophisticated finish.

How autumn warm looks

Autumn-warm coloring is deep, warm and muted. Skin carries a golden or olive undertone, hair and eyes lean rich warm-brown, and the overall feel is soft and earthy rather than icy or stark. Autumn warms generally look most luxurious in warm, low-saturation shades reminiscent of fall foliage, and can look dulled by cool pastels or icy tones.

Best K-beauty shades

Brick, terracotta, rust, camel, olive, mustard and deep warm browns flatter autumn warms beautifully. A brick or MLBB-brown gradient lip supports the warm, refined Korean look favored by this season. Cheeks suit warm peach-brown and brick blush, and eyes glow in bronze, copper, warm khaki and chocolate shadows. Korean brands like rom&nd ('Better Than series') offer many muted warm tones popular with autumn warms.

Base and finish tips

Pick a foundation with a clear warm or golden undertone to harmonize with rich skin; cool pink bases can look mismatched and ashy. A satin to soft-dewy finish supports the warm glow this season carries naturally. Warm-brown brows and a soft bronzy contour complete a cohesive, sophisticated autumn-warm look.
