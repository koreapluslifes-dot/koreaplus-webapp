Winter cool (겨울 쿨) is Korea's boldest cool season for people with clear, high-contrast coloring and cool undertones. Winter cools shine in true red, fuchsia, cobalt, emerald and pure white. Korean vivid cool-red lips and crisp eye looks support a striking, clear complexion.

How winter cool looks

Winter-cool coloring is clear, cool and high-contrast. Skin often appears cool-fair or cool-deep, hair and eyes are dark and crisp against the skin, and the overall impression is sharp and vivid. Winter cools generally look most striking in saturated, cool jewel tones and pure shades, and can look washed out in muted, dusty or warm-earth tones.

Best K-beauty shades

True red, cherry, fuchsia, plum, cobalt, emerald and icy cool pinks flatter winter cools. A bold cool-red lip — applied full or as a vivid gradient — supports the clear, confident Korean look this season carries so well. Cheeks suit cool pink and rose blush used lightly, and eyes look sharp in cool gray, navy, plum and silver tones. Korean brands such as 3CE and rom&nd carry vivid cool reds beloved by winter cools.

Base and finish tips

Choose a foundation with a neutral to cool undertone for a clean canvas; warm yellow bases can muddy this clear coloring. Either a fresh dewy or a clean semi-matte finish works, since winter cools carry strong contrast well. Keep brows cool-toned and defined, and let one bold feature — usually the lip — lead the look for maximum impact.
