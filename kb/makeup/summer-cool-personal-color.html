Summer Cool Personal Color (여름 쿨) 여름 쿨

⚡ Quick answer: Summer cool (여름 쿨) is Korea's soft, cool-toned season for people with pink or rosy undertones and low contrast coloring. Summer cools glow in rose pink, lavender, soft mauve and powder blue. Korean MLBB rosy lips and pink-toned blush support a gentle, fresh complexion.

Summer cool (여름 쿨) is Korea's soft, cool-toned season for people with pink or rosy undertones and low contrast coloring. Summer cools glow in rose pink, lavender, soft mauve and powder blue. Korean MLBB rosy lips and pink-toned blush support a gentle, fresh complexion.

How summer cool looks

Summer-cool coloring is soft and cool with a pink or rosy undertone. Skin may have a fair, slightly pinkish cast, hair and eyes lean toward ashy or soft cool-brown, and the contrast between features is gentle rather than sharp. Summer cools tend to look most harmonious in muted, cool pastel shades and can look harsh in bright orange or heavy warm-earth tones.

Best K-beauty shades

Rose pink, mauve, lavender, soft berry and cool-toned MLBB ('my lips but better') shades flatter summer cools. A rosy gradient lip supports the soft, fresh Korean aesthetic this season is known for. Cheeks suit cool pink and rose blush, and eyes look elegant in soft mauve, taupe and rose-brown. Korean brands such as Romand, rom&nd and 3CE carry plenty of cool rosy tones well-loved by summer cools.

Base and finish tips

A foundation with a neutral to slightly cool undertone keeps summer-cool skin looking clean and bright; overly yellow bases can look sallow. A soft, semi-dewy finish flatters this gentle coloring better than a heavy matte. Keep brows soft (gray-brown rather than warm chocolate) and contour with a cool-toned shade for the most natural, harmonious result.
