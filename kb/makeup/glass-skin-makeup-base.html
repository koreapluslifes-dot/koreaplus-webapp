What glass skin means

Glass skin (유리피부) describes a complexion so smooth, hydrated and luminous it looks like glass. As a makeup goal, it's less about heavy coverage and more about light layering that lets natural radiance show through. The base is built on well-hydrated skin, so skincare and makeup blur together — this is why K-beauty treats prep as the most important step toward the look.

Skincare prep that sets the stage

Start with a hydrating routine: a watery toner, an essence (snail mucin and propolis essences are Korean favorites), and a lightweight moisturizer let the skin look plump and dewy. Niacinamide and centella (cica) steps support a smooth, even-looking surface, while a few minutes of patting helps products absorb. The aim is skin that already glows before any makeup, so the base looks like skin rather than a mask.

Layering the luminous base

Apply a hydrating or glow primer, then a thin layer of a dewy cushion or radiant foundation, building coverage only where needed. Use a damp sponge to press product in for a seamless finish. Add cream highlight on the high points — tops of cheeks, nose bridge, cupid's bow — and set only oily zones lightly so the dewy effect survives. The result is a fresh, see-through radiance that supports the signature glass-skin look.

What is glass skin?

Glass skin is a Korean beauty ideal describing a complexion so smooth, even and hydrated it looks translucent and reflective, like a pane of glass. It isn't a single product but the result of diligent hydration, gentle exfoliation and sun protection layered over a healthy skin barrier.

What is the 10-step Korean skincare routine?

The 10-step Korean skincare routine is a layered framework popularized in Korea: oil cleanser, water cleanser, exfoliant, toner, essence, serum or ampoule, sheet mask, eye cream, moisturizer and sunscreen. It's a customizable menu, not a daily mandate, and most people use only the steps their skin needs.

What is a cushion foundation?

A cushion foundation is a Korean makeup innovation: liquid foundation soaked into a sponge inside a compact, applied with a puff for a dewy, buildable, skincare-infused finish. Many include sun protection and hydrating ingredients, blurring the line between makeup and skincare.

What is a good Korean skincare routine for beginners?

A simple beginner Korean routine is a gentle cleanser, a hydrating toner, a lightweight moisturizer and daily sunscreen, adding one targeted serum once your skin adjusts. Introduce actives slowly, one at a time, and patch-test, following the Korean principle of gentle, barrier-friendly care over many steps at once.
