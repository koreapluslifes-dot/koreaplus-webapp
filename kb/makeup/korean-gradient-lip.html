What the gradient lip is

The gradient lip, or 그라데이션 립, is one of the most recognizable K-makeup signatures. Instead of a sharp, fully filled-in lip, color is densest in the middle of the mouth and softly diffused toward the outer edges, mimicking a natural flush or a 'just ate a popsicle' tint. The effect is youthful and soft, and it supports the gentle, fresh aesthetic central to Korean beauty.

Step-by-step

Start with a smooth base — many Koreans dab a little foundation or concealer over the lips to blur the natural lip line. Apply a lip tint or creamy lipstick to the center of the upper and lower lips. Then press and blend outward using a fingertip or a cotton swab so the color melts into the skin with no hard border. Build in thin layers for a believable flush. Lip tints work especially well because they grip color while staying lightweight.

Best products and shade picks

Korean lip tints and 'water' or 'velvet' formulas from brands like Peripera (Ink series), rom&nd (Juicy Lasting Tint) and Etude are made for this technique. Choose a shade that suits your personal color — coral or peach for warm seasons, rose or berry for cool seasons. Top with a clear or tinted gloss for a juicy finish, or leave it as a soft stain. The gradient lip pairs beautifully with a luminous, glass-skin base.
