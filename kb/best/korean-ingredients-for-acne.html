Best Korean ingredients for acne & breakouts

⚡ Quick answer: For acne & breakouts, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Niacinamide (B3), Centella (Cica), Propolis — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for acne & breakouts.

For acne & breakouts, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Niacinamide (B3), Centella (Cica), Propolis — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for acne & breakouts.

Ranking criteria (transparent): hero-ingredient status, breadth of documented skin benefits, and how broadly Korean routines use it for acne & breakouts. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🧫 Niacinamide (B3) 나이아신아마이드
The ultimate multitasker — 2–5% suits most skin. The old “don’t mix with vitamin C” rule is a debunked myth.
2
🌿 Centella (Cica) 센텔라/시카
“Cica” = Centella asiatica (tiger grass). The go-to soother for sensitive, stressed or breakout-prone skin.
3
🍯 Propolis 프로폴리스
Bee-derived honey-like extract; a glow-giving favourite (skip if you have a bee-product allergy).
4
🐌 Snail Mucin 달팽이 점액
Snail secretion filtrate is a K-beauty icon — a glossy hydrator famed for that plump, dewy finish.
5
🧽 BHA (Salicylic) BHA
Salicylic acid dives into oily pores to decongest. Ideal for blackheads & breakouts.
6
🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia) 어성초
Houttuynia cordata — a trending calming extract for oily, breakout-prone skin.
7
🌙 Retinol 레티놀
PM only, start low & slow (1–2×/week), always SPF next day. Avoid during pregnancy.
8
🍵 Mugwort (Artemisia) 쑥
A traditional Korean botanical (“ssuk”) prized for soothing sensitive, troubled skin.
9
🌾 Azelaic Acid 아젤라산
A multitasker beloved for redness, bumps and post-acne marks — usually well tolerated.
10
🍃 Green Tea 녹차
Camellia sinensis from Jeju — antioxidant-rich and beloved for oily, sensitive skin.
