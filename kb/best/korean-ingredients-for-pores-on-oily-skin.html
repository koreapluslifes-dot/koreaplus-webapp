Best Korean ingredients for pores & texture on oily skin

⏱ 3 min read
⚡ Quick answer: For pores & texture on oily skin, the Korean ingredients worth prioritising are Niacinamide (B3), BHA (Salicylic), Retinol — chosen for what oily skin needs (oil-control, lightweight, pores) as well as for the concern itself.

For pores & texture on oily skin, the Korean ingredients worth prioritising are Niacinamide (B3), BHA (Salicylic), Retinol — chosen for what oily skin needs (oil-control, lightweight, pores) as well as for the concern itself.

Shiny by midday, enlarged pores, prone to congestion. Likes lightweight, balancing care.

Ranked by hero status, breadth of documented benefit, and fit with oily skin. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🧫 Niacinamide (B3) 나이아신아마이드
The ultimate multitasker — 2–5% suits most skin. The old “don’t mix with vitamin C” rule is a debunked myth.
2
🧽 BHA (Salicylic) BHA
Salicylic acid dives into oily pores to decongest. Ideal for blackheads & breakouts.
3
🌙 Retinol 레티놀
PM only, start low & slow (1–2×/week), always SPF next day. Avoid during pregnancy.
4
⬛ Activated Charcoal 활성탄
Charcoal binds things to its surface, which is why it works in water filters and why it looks convincing in a black clay mask. On skin it picks up surface oil during the couple of minutes it is there. The detox framing is nonsense — nothing is being drawn out of a pore, and a peel-off charcoal mask mostly removes your vellus hair.
5
🌋 Jeju Volcanic Ash (Songi) 화산송이
화산송이 (hwasan songi) is the reddish porous scoria that covers parts of Jeju, and it became a Korean beauty signature the way volcanic mud did in Iceland — through provenance as much as chemistry. As an oil absorber it works. As proof of anything about Jeju's air or water, it is a story sold with a mask.
6
🏺 Kaolin Clay 카올린
The gentlest of the common cosmetic clays, and the one Korean wash-off masks lean on when they want oil control without a stripped face. Kaolin soaks up sebum sitting on the surface. It does not reach into a pore, dissolve a blackhead or shrink anything — those are jobs for a BHA, if they are jobs at all.
7
🔮 Polymethylsilsesquioxane 폴리메틸실세스퀴옥산
The 'pore filter' effect in a jar, and the reason a Korean primer can make your nose look airbrushed in about four seconds. These are tiny hard spheres that sit on the surface and bounce light in all directions. Nothing is being treated. Wash it off and the pores are exactly as they were.
8
⚪ Silica 실리카
The workhorse of Korean primers, sun sticks and mattifying creams. Silica is porous mineral powder: it soaks up oil and diffuses light, so shine takes longer to break through and pores look shallower than they are. The effect is optical and temporary, which is exactly what a primer should be honest about.
