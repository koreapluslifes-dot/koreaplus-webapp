Best Korean ingredients for pores & texture

⚡ Quick answer: For pores & texture, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Niacinamide (B3), BHA (Salicylic), Retinol — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for pores & texture.

For pores & texture, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Niacinamide (B3), BHA (Salicylic), Retinol — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for pores & texture.

Ranking criteria (transparent): hero-ingredient status, breadth of documented skin benefits, and how broadly Korean routines use it for pores & texture. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🧫 Niacinamide (B3) 나이아신아마이드
The ultimate multitasker — 2–5% suits most skin. The old “don’t mix with vitamin C” rule is a debunked myth.
2
🧽 BHA (Salicylic) BHA
Salicylic acid dives into oily pores to decongest. Ideal for blackheads & breakouts.
3
🌙 Retinol 레티놀
PM only, start low & slow (1–2×/week), always SPF next day. Avoid during pregnancy.
4
🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) AHA
Water-loving acids that resurface the top layer. Lactic is gentler; glycolic is stronger. SPF a must.
5
🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone) PHA
Large-molecule acid that exfoliates very gently — great for sensitive skin new to acids.
6
🍶 Galactomyces Ferment 갈락토미세스
A fermented essence star (think “first essence”) for radiance and refined texture.
