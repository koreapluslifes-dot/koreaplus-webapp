Best Korean ingredients for post-acne marks (pie & pih) on acne-prone skin

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: For post-acne marks (pie & pih) on acne-prone skin, the Korean ingredients worth prioritising are Retinaldehyde (Retinal), Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu), Exosome-type Ingredients — chosen for what acne-prone skin needs (oil-control, non-comedogenic, barrier, gentle-actives) as well as for the concern itself.

For post-acne marks (pie & pih) on acne-prone skin, the Korean ingredients worth prioritising are Retinaldehyde (Retinal), Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu), Exosome-type Ingredients — chosen for what acne-prone skin needs (oil-control, non-comedogenic, barrier, gentle-actives) as well as for the concern itself.

Skin that reliably congests and breaks out — often oily, but just as often normal or dry skin that clogs easily.

Ranked by hero status, breadth of documented benefit, and fit with acne-prone skin. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🌙 Retinaldehyde (Retinal) 레티날
The middle rung of the retinoid ladder. Because it needs fewer conversion steps than retinol, it is generally considered more potent at the same percentage — start low, go slow, and always pair it with sunscreen the next morning. Retinoids are also the one category conventionally avoided altogether in pregnancy and breastfeeding: here, 'caution' means skip it until after.
2
🔷 Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu) 구리펩타이드
The distinctive blue serum. Copper peptides are widely used but formulation-sensitive — direct acids and pure vitamin C in the same layer can destabilise them, which is why they are usually kept to their own step.
3
🫧 Exosome-type Ingredients 엑소좀
Genuinely interesting science, genuinely overstated marketing. Cosmetic 'exosome' products are usually plant- or ferment-derived vesicle preparations, and they are not the same as the exosome procedures offered in clinics. Independent evidence for topical, over-the-counter versions is still early.
4
🌹 Rosehip Oil 로즈힙 오일
It oxidises faster than most oils, so buy small bottles, keep it dark and cool, and stop using it once it smells sharp.
