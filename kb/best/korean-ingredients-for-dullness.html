Best Korean ingredients for dullness & glow

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: For dullness & glow, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, Bifida Ferment Lysate, Rice Extract — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for dullness & glow.

For dullness & glow, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, Bifida Ferment Lysate, Rice Extract — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for dullness & glow.

Ranking criteria (transparent): hero-ingredient status, breadth of documented skin benefits, and how broadly Korean routines use it for dullness & glow. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🍋 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid 에칠아스코빌에텔
The vitamin C derivative Korean brands use when they want a serum that does not turn orange by March. Korea's MFDS lists it among the notified 미백 (mibaek, brightening) functional actives, which means a product carrying that mark on its strength contains a regulated amount. Gentler than L-ascorbic acid, and slower.
2
🧫 Bifida Ferment Lysate 비피다 발효용해물
The ferment that made 'microbiome skincare' a shelf category. Bifida ferment lysate is a processed bacterial culture — nothing is alive by the time it reaches the bottle — used as a conditioning ingredient in essences and barrier creams.
3
🍚 Rice Extract 쌀
A heritage Korean ingredient for that soft, luminous “mochi skin” finish.
4
🍶 Saccharomyces Ferment Filtrate 사카로미세스 발효여과물
Close cousin of galactomyces and the reason first essences exist at all. Yeast ferment filtrates are conditioning ingredients — read the marketing about 'cell renewal' as appearance language, not a proven biological claim.
5
🍊 Vitamin C 비타민C
Best in the AM under sunscreen. Pure L-ascorbic acid is potent; derivatives are gentler for sensitive skin.
6
💧 Hyaluronic Acid 히알루론산
A humectant magnet for moisture. Apply to damp skin and seal with cream so it pulls water IN, not out.
7
🐟 PDRN (Salmon DNA) 연어 DNA
Polydeoxyribonucleotide — the 2026 “salmon DNA” buzz active. Note: topical evidence is emerging; benefits are cosmetic, not the same as clinical injectables.
8
🫚 Black Ginseng 흑삼
Heuksam is made by steaming and drying ginseng through repeated cycles — a genuinely different preparation, not just a marketing colour. It is the ingredient Korean luxury houses reach for when they want heritage on the box.
9
🟡 Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone) 코엔자임Q10
One of the older anti-ageing actives and still a sensible one. It is oil-soluble and yellow, so it lives in creams and oils rather than watery essences.
10
🌱 Danggwi (Angelica gigas) 당귀
당귀 (danggwi) is the Korean angelica that appears in almost every hanbang blend, usually alongside peony and rehmannia. Topically it is an antioxidant botanical with reasonable laboratory backing and very little human skin data. It is also a herb with real internal pharmacology, so if you are pregnant, ask your doctor rather than assuming.
