Best Korean ingredients for dullness & glow

⚡ Quick answer: For dullness & glow, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Rice Extract, Vitamin C, Hyaluronic Acid — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for dullness & glow.

For dullness & glow, the most-recommended Korean skincare ingredients are Rice Extract, Vitamin C, Hyaluronic Acid — ranked by hero status, breadth of benefit, and how broadly Korean routines use them for dullness & glow.

Ranking criteria (transparent): hero-ingredient status, breadth of documented skin benefits, and how broadly Korean routines use it for dullness & glow. We rank ingredients, never specific products.

1
🍚 Rice Extract 쌀
A heritage Korean ingredient for that soft, luminous “mochi skin” finish.
2
🍊 Vitamin C 비타민C
Best in the AM under sunscreen. Pure L-ascorbic acid is potent; derivatives are gentler for sensitive skin.
3
💧 Hyaluronic Acid 히알루론산
A humectant magnet for moisture. Apply to damp skin and seal with cream so it pulls water IN, not out.
4
🐟 PDRN (Salmon DNA) 연어 DNA
Polydeoxyribonucleotide — the 2026 “salmon DNA” buzz active. Note: topical evidence is emerging; benefits are cosmetic, not the same as clinical injectables.
5
🫚 Ginseng 인삼
A premium hanbang (Korean herbal) hero, central to luxury anti-ageing lines.
6
🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) AHA
Water-loving acids that resurface the top layer. Lactic is gentler; glycolic is stronger. SPF a must.
7
🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone) PHA
Large-molecule acid that exfoliates very gently — great for sensitive skin new to acids.
8
🫐 Alpha-Arbutin 알부틴
A gentle, stable brightener — a softer route to fading dark spots.
9
🍶 Galactomyces Ferment 갈락토미세스
A fermented essence star (think “first essence”) for radiance and refined texture.
10
⚪ Tranexamic Acid 트라넥삼산
A favourite for melasma and stubborn pigmentation, often paired with niacinamide.
