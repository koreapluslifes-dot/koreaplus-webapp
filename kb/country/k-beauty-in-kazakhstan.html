K-Beauty in Kazakhstan: A Practical Guide

Kazakhstan combines a strong K-pop following with a long-established Korean diaspora community, giving Korean beauty a notable foothold in Central Asia.

How K-beauty arrived in Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan's interest in Korean beauty grew with the global Korean Wave, with K-pop and K-drama building a following through the 2010s. The country is also home to a long-established ethnic Korean community (often referred to as Koryo-saram), part of a broader Korean diaspora in the post-Soviet region, which has helped sustain a connection to Korean culture and products.

Where Kazakhs shop for it

Shoppers use local and regional e-commerce marketplaces, specialist online stores, and some physical beauty retailers. Certain shops and pharmacies have carried Korean or Korean-inspired products, though assortments vary over time and by store — checking current listings directly is the dependable approach. Almaty and Astana generally offer the most in-person options.

Customs and shipping notes

Kazakhstan is part of the Eurasian Economic Union customs framework, and imported parcels can attract duties, taxes, and fees depending on value and category, with thresholds that change over time. Because rules are revised periodically and regional frameworks apply, confirm the current details with Kazakhstan's state revenue authority before relying on direct international orders. Shipping times vary by origin and courier.

Key facts
  • Kazakhstan has both a K-pop following and a Korean diaspora community.
  • Interest grew with the global Hallyu wave through the 2010s.
  • Regional and local marketplaces serve buyers.
  • Imported parcels can attract duties, taxes, and fees by value.
  • Almaty and Astana offer the most in-person options.
