How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in the Philippines

The Philippines is among Southeast Asia's most enthusiastic Hallyu markets, and Korean beauty grew rapidly alongside the popularity of K-pop and Korean dramas.

Deep Hallyu fandom

Korean dramas and K-pop have long enjoyed large, devoted Filipino audiences, and this strong fandom fed directly into demand for Korean skincare and makeup. As Hallyu — the international spread of Korean pop culture — deepened, celebrity and idol endorsements became influential, and Korean brands established a visible mall presence. Social media and live-selling formats, popular in the Philippines, became important channels for product discovery.

What resonated locally

The country's hot, humid climate made lightweight, hydration-focused and oil-control products appealing, fitting the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines. The Korean emphasis on sun protection and on brightening or even-tone products drew interest, though preferences vary among consumers. Cushion compacts, sheet masks and sunscreens were popular, and ingredients like centella asiatica became widely familiar.

Where shoppers in the Philippines buy it today

Korean beauty is widely available through mall-based beauty retailers, brand boutiques, general online marketplaces, regional e-commerce platforms and live-shopping channels. Brand availability changes over time, and counterfeits can be a concern online, so buying from reputable sellers is advisable. Shoppers should check ingredient and batch information and follow standard customs and shipping guidance for overseas purchases.

Key facts
  • The Philippines is among Southeast Asia's most enthusiastic Hallyu markets.
  • Live-selling and social media are key discovery channels.
  • Humid climate suited lightweight, oil-control products.
  • Buying from reputable sellers helps avoid counterfeits.
  • K-beauty is sold via mall retailers, online marketplaces and live shopping.
