How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in Taiwan

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Korean beauty in Taiwan grew early with strong Hallyu fandom and a humid climate, now widely available via drugstores, beauty retailers and online marketplaces.

Taiwan was an early and enthusiastic Asian market for Korean beauty, with strong Hallyu fandom and a vibrant existing beauty culture, including a well-established tradition of sheet-mask use.

Early adoption and mask culture

Taiwan embraced Korean pop culture relatively early, and Korean dramas and K-pop built devoted local audiences. As Hallyu — the international spread of Korean pop culture — grew, demand for Korean skincare and makeup followed. Taiwan also has its own well-established sheet-mask culture, which made Korean masks and hydration-focused products a natural fit and helped Korean beauty integrate smoothly into existing routines.

What resonated locally

Taiwan's hot, humid climate made lightweight, hydration-focused and oil-control products appealing, suiting the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines. The Korean emphasis on sun protection and brightening products drew interest among many consumers. Cushion compacts, sheet masks and sunscreens were popular, and ingredients like centella asiatica became widely familiar through reviews and beauty media.

Where shoppers in Taiwan buy it today

Korean beauty is widely available in Taiwan through drugstore and beauty retail chains, brand boutiques, general online marketplaces and regional e-commerce platforms. Brand availability changes over time. As with any cosmetics, shoppers should check ingredient and batch information and follow standard customs and shipping guidance for overseas purchases.

Key facts
  • Taiwan was an early Asian adopter of Korean beauty.
  • Taiwan has its own well-established sheet-mask culture.
  • Humid climate suited lightweight, hydration-focused products.
  • Hallyu drove demand alongside a vibrant local beauty culture.
  • K-beauty is sold via drugstores, beauty chains and online marketplaces.
