How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in Italy

Italy, with its strong tradition of personal care and style, embraced Korean beauty as part of the broader Korean Wave, with younger consumers leading much of the early interest.

Style culture meets Korean skincare

Italy has a long-standing culture of personal grooming and aesthetics, and Korean beauty entered this market offering novel formats and an ingredient-led, hydration-focused philosophy. As Hallyu — the international spread of Korean pop culture — gained Italian audiences, particularly younger ones, interest in Korean skincare and makeup grew. Social media, beauty influencers and online communities were key channels for introducing routines and hero products.

What appealed to Italian consumers

The gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines resonated with consumers interested in skincare as both ritual and result. Sun protection, a hallmark of Korean skincare culture, also drew attention in a sunny Mediterranean climate. Ingredients commonly linked to K-beauty, including centella asiatica and hyaluronic acid, became familiar to enthusiasts through reviews.

Where Italians buy it today

Korean beauty is available in Italy through beauty retail chains, pharmacies, general online marketplaces and specialty K-beauty online shops. Specific brand line-ups shift over time, so shoppers often compare options. As with imported cosmetics generally, it is sensible to check ingredient lists and batch or expiry details and be aware of standard customs considerations when ordering from outside the EU.

Key facts
  • Italy's strong grooming and style culture welcomed novel K-beauty formats.
  • Younger consumers and social media led early interest.
  • Korea's sun-protection focus suited the Mediterranean climate.
  • Hallyu broadened Italian interest in Korean skincare.
  • K-beauty is sold via beauty chains, pharmacies and online shops.
