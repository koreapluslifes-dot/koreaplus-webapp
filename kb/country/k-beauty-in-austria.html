K-Beauty in Austria: A Practical Guide

Austria's beauty market has long looked to neighboring Germany, and that proximity helped Korean skincare reach Austrian shelves and inboxes as the trend spread across Europe.

How K-beauty arrived in Austria

Korean beauty entered Austrian awareness through the broader global Korean Wave and the rising popularity of K-pop and Korean entertainment in the 2010s. Because Austria shares a language and many retail relationships with Germany — one of Europe's comparatively earlier K-beauty markets — Austrian consumers often discovered Korean products through German-language media, reviews, and retailers.

Where Austrians shop for it

Many Austrian shoppers order from German and pan-European online beauty retailers that ship within the EU. Some Austrian pharmacies (Apotheke), drugstores, and beauty chains have carried Korean or Korean-inspired products, though the exact assortment shifts over time and varies by store — confirm current stock directly. Vienna, as the largest city, generally has the broadest in-person selection, with Graz, Linz, and Salzburg offering more limited options.

Shipping and import notes

Austria's EU membership means intra-EU orders move without border customs and typically include VAT at checkout. Parcels shipped directly from Korea may incur EU import VAT and handling fees under current EU rules, which have tightened in recent years. Before a large non-EU order, check current thresholds and procedures with Austrian customs.

Key facts
  • Austria's ties to Germany helped Korean beauty arrive via German retailers.
  • Interest grew with the global Hallyu wave through the 2010s.
  • Intra-EU orders avoid border customs and usually include VAT.
  • Direct imports from Korea may face EU import VAT and fees.
  • Vienna offers the widest in-person selection.
