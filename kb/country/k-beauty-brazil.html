How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in Brazil

Brazil, one of the world's largest beauty markets, developed strong interest in Korean beauty driven by passionate K-pop fandom and a vibrant existing cosmetics culture.

Passionate fandom drives interest

Brazil has one of the most passionate K-pop fan communities in Latin America, and this enthusiasm has been a major driver of interest in Korean beauty. As Hallyu — the global spread of Korean pop culture — grew, fans sought out Korean skincare and makeup, often through online discovery. Social media and fan communities played an outsized role in product awareness, given that local retail presence developed more gradually.

What resonated locally

Brazil already has a large, sophisticated beauty market and warm, often humid climates, which made lightweight, hydration-focused products appealing alongside the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines. The Korean emphasis on sun protection drew interest, though Brazilian consumers also have strong local sun-care traditions. Sheet masks, sunscreens and hydration-focused products were common entry points, and ingredients like centella asiatica became familiar to enthusiasts.

Where shoppers in Brazil buy it today

Korean beauty is increasingly available in Brazil through online beauty platforms, general e-commerce marketplaces and specialty online shops, with growing presence in major cities. Brand availability is expanding but varies, and counterfeits can be a concern online, so reputable sellers are advisable. Because imports can involve customs duties and longer shipping, shoppers should review costs, check ingredient and batch information, and follow standard import guidance.

Key facts
  • Brazil has one of Latin America's most passionate K-pop fan communities.
  • Fan communities and social media drove product awareness.
  • Brazil is one of the world's largest beauty markets.
  • Imports can involve customs duties and longer shipping.
  • K-beauty is sold via online beauty platforms and e-commerce marketplaces.
