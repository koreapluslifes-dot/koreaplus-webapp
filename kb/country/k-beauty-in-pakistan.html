K-Beauty in Pakistan: A Practical Guide

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: How Korean beauty reached Pakistan, where to shop it in Karachi and Lahore, and what to know about customs. A hedged, practical guide.

Pakistan's large, youthful population and a growing K-drama following have driven steady interest in Korean beauty, led by online shopping.

How K-beauty arrived in Pakistan

Korean beauty's growing presence in Pakistan reflects the global Korean Wave, with K-drama and K-pop building a following among younger audiences through the 2010s and 2020s. Social media, YouTube reviews, and beauty influencers have been central to introducing multi-step routines and Korean staples to Pakistani shoppers.

Where Pakistanis shop for it

Online channels lead, including local e-commerce marketplaces and specialist online stores focused on imported beauty, alongside international retailers that ship to Pakistan. Some beauty shops in larger cities have begun stocking Korean or Korean-inspired products, though availability varies and is still developing — checking current listings is the dependable approach. Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad generally offer the most options.

Customs and shipping notes

Pakistan maintains its own customs regime through the Federal Board of Revenue and Pakistan Customs, and imported parcels can attract import duties, taxes, and handling fees depending on value and category, with rules that change over time. Because policies are revised periodically, confirm the current details before relying on direct international orders. International shipping times can run from one to several weeks.

Key facts
  • A large, youthful population and K-drama following drive interest.
  • Interest grew with the global Hallyu wave through the 2010s-2020s.
  • Online channels lead; local stock is still developing.
  • Imported parcels can attract duties, taxes, and handling fees.
  • Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad offer the most options.
