K-Beauty in Peru: A Practical Guide

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: How Korean beauty reached Peru, where to shop it in Lima, and what to know about customs. A hedged, practical guide for Peruvian shoppers.

Peru's growing K-pop fandom and a visible Korean cultural presence in Lima have fueled rising interest in Korean beauty.

How K-beauty arrived in Peru

Peruvian interest in Korean beauty grew with the global Korean Wave, as K-pop and K-drama built a dedicated following through the 2010s and 2020s. Lima has seen Korean cultural events and a community presence that helped sustain interest beyond online channels, and social media has been central to spreading awareness of Korean skincare routines.

Where Peruvians shop for it

Shoppers rely on local e-commerce marketplaces, specialist online stores, and a number of physical shops, including some associated with Korean community areas in Lima. Certain beauty retailers and pharmacies have introduced Korean or Korean-inspired products, though assortments vary — checking current stock directly is the reliable approach. Lima offers the most options, with other cities more limited.

Customs and shipping notes

Peru maintains its own customs regime through SUNAT, and imported parcels can attract import duties, taxes, and handling fees depending on value and category, with low-value thresholds that change over time. Because policies are revised periodically, confirm the current details before relying on direct international orders. International shipping can take one to several weeks depending on the courier and clearance.

Key facts
  • Growing K-pop fandom and a Lima community presence drive interest.
  • Interest grew with the global Hallyu wave through the 2010s-2020s.
  • Local marketplaces and specialist e-shops serve buyers.
  • Imported parcels can attract duties, taxes, and handling fees.
  • Lima offers the most options for shoppers.
