How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in Vietnam

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Korean beauty in Vietnam grew with strong Hallyu fandom and close Korea ties, now widely available via beauty retailers, malls, online marketplaces and live shopping.

Vietnam has become one of Southeast Asia's fast-growing markets for Korean beauty, supported by strong Hallyu fandom and close economic and cultural ties with South Korea.

Close ties and strong fandom

South Korea has substantial economic and cultural ties with Vietnam, and Korean dramas and K-pop have enjoyed large Vietnamese audiences. As Hallyu — the international spread of Korean pop culture — grew, interest in Korean skincare and makeup rose alongside it. Korean brands and multi-brand beauty retailers established a presence in Vietnam's cities, while online and social platforms broadened access.

What resonated locally

Vietnam's hot, humid climate made lightweight, hydration-focused and oil-control products appealing, fitting the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines. The Korean emphasis on sun protection and brightening or even-tone products drew interest among many consumers, though preferences vary. Cushion compacts, sheet masks and sunscreens were popular, and ingredients like centella asiatica became widely familiar.

Where shoppers in Vietnam buy it today

Korean beauty is available in Vietnam through beauty retail chains, brand boutiques, general online marketplaces, regional e-commerce platforms and live-shopping channels. Brand availability changes over time, and counterfeits can be a concern online, so reputable sellers are advisable. Shoppers should check ingredient and batch information and follow standard customs and shipping guidance for overseas purchases.

Key facts
  • South Korea has close economic and cultural ties with Vietnam.
  • K-dramas and K-pop built large Vietnamese audiences.
  • Humid climate suited lightweight, oil-control products.
  • Live-selling and online platforms broadened access.
  • K-beauty is sold via beauty chains, online marketplaces and live shopping.
