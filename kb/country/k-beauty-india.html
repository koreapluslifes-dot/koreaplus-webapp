How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in India

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Korean beauty in India grew with rising Hallyu interest and online retail, now increasingly available via e-commerce marketplaces, beauty platforms and specialty shops.

India's interest in Korean beauty has grown more recently than in some Asian markets, accelerating as Hallyu reached wider audiences and as online beauty retail expanded.

A more recent, online-led rise

Korean pop culture's reach in India broadened notably in recent years, with K-pop and Korean dramas gaining substantial followings, particularly among younger urban consumers and in the country's northeastern regions, which have long-standing cultural affinities with East and Southeast Asia. As Hallyu — the international spread of Korean pop culture — grew, interest in Korean skincare and makeup followed, largely driven by online discovery and e-commerce rather than traditional retail.

What resonated locally

India's diverse climates range from hot and humid to dry, but lightweight, hydration-focused products and the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines found an audience among skincare enthusiasts. The Korean emphasis on sun protection drew interest, though product preferences vary widely across a large and diverse country. Sheet masks, sunscreens and hydration-focused products were common entry points, and ingredients like centella asiatica became familiar to enthusiasts.

Where shoppers in India buy it today

Korean beauty is increasingly available in India through online beauty platforms, general e-commerce marketplaces and specialty online shops, with growing physical retail in major cities. Brand availability is expanding but varies, and counterfeits can be a concern online, so reputable sellers are advisable. Shoppers should check ingredient and batch information and follow standard customs and shipping guidance for imported purchases.

Key facts
  • India's K-beauty interest rose more recently and is largely online-led.
  • Northeastern India has long-standing cultural affinities with East and Southeast Asia.
  • Hallyu broadened among younger urban consumers.
  • Sheet masks and sunscreens were common entry points.
  • K-beauty is sold via online beauty platforms and e-commerce marketplaces.
