How K-Beauty Arrived and Grew in South Africa

South Africa, one of the larger and more developed beauty markets in sub-Saharan Africa, has seen growing interest in Korean beauty as Hallyu reached local audiences and online retail expanded.

An emerging, online-led market

Korean pop culture's reach in South Africa grew in recent years, with K-pop and Korean dramas gaining followings, especially among younger, urban and online-active consumers. As Hallyu — the global spread of Korean pop culture — expanded, curiosity about Korean skincare and makeup followed. Discovery has been largely driven by social media and e-commerce, with specialty importers and online shops helping to fill gaps where mainstream retail presence is still developing.

What resonated locally

South Africa's sunny climate and diverse population created interest in sun protection and in hydration-focused products, fitting the gentle, layered approach associated with Korean routines. Because consumers span a wide range of skin tones and needs, product suitability varies, and shoppers often look for items that work for their individual skin. Sheet masks, sunscreens and hydration-focused products were common entry points, and ingredients like centella asiatica became familiar to enthusiasts.

Where shoppers in South Africa buy it today

Korean beauty is increasingly available in South Africa through online beauty platforms, general e-commerce marketplaces and specialty importers' online shops, with growing presence in major cities. Brand availability is expanding but varies, and counterfeits can be a concern online, so reputable sellers are advisable. Because imports can involve customs duties and longer shipping, shoppers should review costs, check ingredient and batch information, and follow standard import guidance.

Key facts
  • South Africa is among sub-Saharan Africa's larger, more developed beauty markets.
  • Discovery has been largely social-media and e-commerce led.
  • Sunny climate spurred interest in sun protection.
  • Diverse skin tones mean product suitability varies.
  • K-beauty is sold via online platforms, marketplaces and specialty importers.
