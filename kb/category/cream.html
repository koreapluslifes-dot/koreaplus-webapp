Korean Moisturizer / Cream: what it is & do you need it 크림/수분크림

⚡ Quick answer: The seal. Ceramides and humectants lock everything underneath in and reinforce the barrier — the last skincare step before SPF.

The seal. Ceramides and humectants lock everything underneath in and reinforce the barrier — the last skincare step before SPF.

At a glance

Texture: Rich, cushiony, occlusive ≈ Moisturizer / face cream When: AM & PM, last skincare step (before sunscreen in the AM)

Do you need it?

essential Everyone needs something to seal in moisture. Whether it's a gel-cream or a rich balm depends on your skin and season.

⏭️ You can skip it if: Never fully skip the seal — but very oily skin can use a light gel-cream or an emulsion instead of a heavy cream.
💡 Tip: This is where the thinnest-to-thickest rule ends. Layer humectants underneath on damp skin, then seal with cream.
