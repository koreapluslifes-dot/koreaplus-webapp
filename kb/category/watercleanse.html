Korean Water cleanser (gel / foam): what it is & do you need it 폼/젤 클렌저

⚡ Quick answer: Step 2 of the double cleanse — lifts away sweat, dirt and any leftover residue without stripping the barrier.

Step 2 of the double cleanse — lifts away sweat, dirt and any leftover residue without stripping the barrier.

At a glance

Texture: Low-pH gel or gentle foam ≈ Face wash / cleanser When: AM & PM, after the oil cleanser at night

Do you need it?

essential The one cleanser everyone needs, morning and night. Low-pH (around 5.5) keeps the barrier calm.

⏭️ You can skip it if: Never fully skip — but in the morning a quick water rinse can replace it for very dry skin.
💡 Tip: Lukewarm water only. Hot water and squeaky-clean tightness are signs you're over-stripping.
