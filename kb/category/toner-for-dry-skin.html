Best Korean toner / skin for dry skin

Choosing a Korean toner / skin for dry skin.

Tight, flaky or rough; little visible oil. Craves hydration + barrier support.

The texture fit

On its own this may not be enough for very dry skin — layer it under a richer cream to seal in the hydration.

What this step does: First hydration + prep step. Korean toners rebalance and soften skin so the next layers absorb better. They hydrate — they do not strip.

💡 Forget Western alcohol astringents. A K-beauty toner should feel like watery hydration; pat 1-3 layers into damp skin.
