Korean Oil cleanser / Cleansing balm: what it is & do you need it 클렌징 오일/밤

⚡ Quick answer: Step 1 of the double cleanse — dissolves oil-based grime (sunscreen, makeup, sebum) that water alone can't lift.

Step 1 of the double cleanse — dissolves oil-based grime (sunscreen, makeup, sebum) that water alone can't lift.

At a glance

Texture: Slippery oil or melting balm ≈ Makeup-removing oil / cleansing balm When: PM, very first step on dry skin

Do you need it?

essential Non-negotiable at night if you wear sunscreen or makeup — water cleansers can't break down water-resistant SPF.

⏭️ You can skip it if: AM only, or nights with bare, SPF-free skin (then a single water cleanse is plenty).
💡 Tip: Massage onto DRY hands and DRY face for 30-60s, then add a splash of water to emulsify (it turns milky) before rinsing.
