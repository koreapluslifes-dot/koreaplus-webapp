⚡ Quick answer: What a Korean oil cleanser / cleansing balm actually is and whether you need one. Step 1 of the double cleanse — dissolves oil-based grime (sunscreen, makeup, sebum) that water alone can't lift — a slippery oil or melting balm texture, used pm, very first step on dry skin.

At a glance

Texture: Slippery oil or melting balm ≈ Makeup-removing oil / cleansing balm When: PM, very first step on dry skin

Do you need it?

essential Non-negotiable at night if you wear sunscreen or makeup — water cleansers can't break down water-resistant SPF.

⏭️ You can skip it if: AM only, or nights with bare, SPF-free skin (then a single water cleanse is plenty).

🙋 People also ask

What is double cleansing?

Double cleansing is a two-step Korean method: first an oil-based cleanser dissolves makeup, sunscreen and sebum, then a water-based cleanser removes sweat and water-soluble debris. The pairing aims to clean thoroughly without the tightness or stripping that one harsh wash can cause.

Do you really need all 10 steps of Korean skincare?

No. The 10-step routine is a menu, not a requirement. Many Koreans use a simple core of cleanser, hydrating toner, moisturizer and sunscreen, adding serums, essences or masks only as their skin needs. The point is consistency and customization, not maximum steps.

Do I really need to double cleanse?

At night, if you wear sunscreen or makeup — yes. Water-based cleansers cannot break down water-resistant sunscreen, so a single cleanse leaves a film behind. In the morning, one gentle cleanse or just water is plenty.

What is a first essence and do I need one?

A first essence is a watery, high-percentage ferment applied straight after cleansing, before anything else. It is a hydration and conditioning step. Pleasant and popular, but genuinely optional.
