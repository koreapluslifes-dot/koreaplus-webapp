Korean Sleeping mask: what it is & do you need it 수면팩

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: What a Korean sleeping mask actually is and whether you need one. A sealing final PM layer that locks the rest of the routine in overnight — a gel, gel-cream or thick balm texture, used pm, the very last step.

A sealing final PM layer that locks the rest of the routine in overnight.

At a glance

Texture: Gel, gel-cream or thick balm ≈ Overnight mask / night treatment When: PM, the very last step

Do you need it?

optional Most useful in winter, in dry climates, or on skin that wakes up tight no matter what it went to bed in.

⏭️ You can skip it if: Your night cream already leaves skin comfortable until morning.
💡 Tip: For most of them it goes over your moisturiser rather than instead of it — the job is to seal the routine in overnight. Some are formulated as treatments in their own right, with acids or retinoids in them, so read what is actually in the jar before deciding which slot it takes.

What is a sheet mask and how often to use it?

A sheet mask is a serum-soaked fabric or hydrogel mask, a Korean skincare staple, used to flood skin with hydration and active ingredients for 15 to 20 minutes. Most people use them a few times a week or as an occasional boost, applied after essence and before moisturizer.

Do you really need all 10 steps of Korean skincare?

No. The 10-step routine is a menu, not a requirement. Many Koreans use a simple core of cleanser, hydrating toner, moisturizer and sunscreen, adding serums, essences or masks only as their skin needs. The point is consistency and customization, not maximum steps.

Do I really need to double cleanse?

At night, if you wear sunscreen or makeup — yes. Water-based cleansers cannot break down water-resistant sunscreen, so a single cleanse leaves a film behind. In the morning, one gentle cleanse or just water is plenty.

What is a first essence and do I need one?

A first essence is a watery, high-percentage ferment applied straight after cleansing, before anything else. It is a hydration and conditioning step. Pleasant and popular, but genuinely optional.
