Dry skin + dryness & dehydration: Korean routine

⚡ Quick answer: K-beauty routine for dry skin and dryness & dehydration.

A Korean skincare routine for dry skin dealing with dryness & dehydration.

Tight, flaky or rough; little visible oil. Craves hydration + barrier support.

Key ingredients

💧 Hyaluronic Acid🧱 Ceramides🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🫒 Squalane

Routine shape

  • AM: gentle cleanse → hydrating toner → targeted serum → moisturizer → SPF
  • PM: (oil +) gentle cleanse → toner → treatment → moisturizer
