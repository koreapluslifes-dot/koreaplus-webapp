자외선차단: how Korea regulates sunscreen claims

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Sunscreen is a functional cosmetic in Korea, so SPF and PA figures on a Korean product come from prescribed testing and review rather than from the brand's own judgement. That regulatory position is a large part of why Korean sunscreen labelling is comparatively trustworthy.

Sunscreen is a functional cosmetic in Korea, so SPF and PA figures on a Korean product come from prescribed testing and review rather than from the brand's own judgement. That regulatory position is a large part of why Korean sunscreen labelling is comparatively trustworthy.

Sunscreen sits inside the functional category

Rather than treating sunscreen as an over-the-counter drug, as the United States does, Korea places it in the functional cosmetics tier. Claims require testing and review, but the route to market for a new filter or a new formula is far shorter than the American one.

What gets tested

SPF is determined by prescribed methodology for UVB protection, and the PA rating by the persistent pigment darkening method for UVA. Water resistance, where claimed, has its own protocol.

Why this produces better sunscreen

A workable approval path means formulators can adopt newer filters as they become available. That is the mechanism behind the texture advantage — not superior chemistry, but a regulatory system that lets good chemistry reach shelves.

The caveat worth knowing

There have been documented cases of Korean sunscreens whose tested SPF did not match the label, which led to tightened oversight. No system is perfect. Buying from established brands and reapplying properly remains sound advice anywhere.
