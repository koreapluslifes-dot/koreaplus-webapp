Rosehip Oil in autumn: how to adjust

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Using Rosehip Oil in autumn: the season brings falling humidity and barrier recovery, so what changes is texture, frequency and what you layer around it — not usually whether you use it at all.

Using Rosehip Oil in autumn: the season brings falling humidity and barrier recovery, so what changes is texture, frequency and what you layer around it — not usually whether you use it at all.

It oxidises faster than most oils, so buy small bottles, keep it dark and cool, and stop using it once it smells sharp.

What autumn does to your skin

Dropping humidity is the classic time to repair the moisture barrier and slowly transition to richer textures.

How that changes Rosehip Oil

Autumn is the classic Korean repair season — humidity drops, summer sun damage surfaces, and richer textures start to feel right again. Rosehip Oil works on dryness & dehydration, post-acne marks (pie & pih), dullness & glow, and it is normally applied PM.

What to layer it with

🫒 Squalane🌻 Vitamin E (Tocopherol)🧫 Niacinamide (B3)🌰 Linoleic Acid
