Winter K-beauty for damaged moisture barrier

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Managing damaged moisture barrier through winter the Korean way — how the season shifts your skin (Cold air and indoor heating strip moisture, so layer hydrating toners and essences under a richer cream or occlusive) and which ingredients to lean on (Ceramides, Cholesterol, Panthenol (Pro-B5)).

Managing damaged moisture barrier in winter with a Korean approach.

Cold air and indoor heating strip moisture, so layer hydrating toners and essences under a richer cream or occlusive.

Stinging with products that used to be fine, new sensitivity, patchy flaking — the signature of an over-exfoliated or over-worked barrier.

Ingredients to lean on

🧱 Ceramides🧱 Cholesterol🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🌿 Centella (Cica)
💡 Barrier repair is mostly subtraction. Strip the routine back to cleanser, a simple moisturiser and sunscreen for two to four weeks — adding another product is usually the wrong instinct.
