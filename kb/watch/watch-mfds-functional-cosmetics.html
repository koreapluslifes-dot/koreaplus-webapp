Regulatory & Safety Watch: MFDS Functional-Cosmetic Categories 기능성화장품 (식약처 MFDS)

The concept

MFDS, Korea's food-and-drug regulator, operates a 'functional cosmetics' category that sits between everyday cosmetics and pharmaceuticals. It broadly covers areas such as UV protection, helping the look of brightness, supporting the appearance of wrinkles, and certain hair and barrier claims, with associated review expectations. We summarize this well-known framework at a high level without quoting specific statutes; treat it as education, not legal guidance.

Why it matters for K-beauty

The functional-cosmetic system is part of why Korean labels can confidently use structured benefit language while staying in the cosmetic lane. It encourages standardized claim categories rather than open-ended medical promises, which aligns with responsible cosmetic wording ('supports the look of'). It also explains why some benefits common on Korean packs are framed differently when the product is exported.

How to read it as a shopper

When a Korean product references a functional or '기능성' designation, understand it as a regulated cosmetic claim category, not a medical drug approval. Benefit wording may be adapted to fit local rules in other markets. For authoritative detail on what each category requires, consult MFDS or qualified local regulatory professionals rather than relying on this overview.
