The concept

Some preservatives work by slowly releasing small amounts of formaldehyde. Cosmetic regulators in markets such as the EU and the US restrict their use and have moved toward clearer disclosure when products may release formaldehyde above set thresholds. We describe this established direction generally, cite no specific limit values, and present it as education. Preservatives are essential for product safety; the topic is about which systems are used, not fear.

Why it intersects with Korean skincare

K-beauty's gentle, barrier-first positioning has encouraged many Korean formulators toward modern preservative systems and away from older formaldehyde-releasers, especially in 'sensitive' or minimalist lines. That said, preservation choices vary by brand and product, so the presence or absence of any system should be read from the actual ingredient list, not assumed from marketing language.

How to read it as a shopper

If you prefer to avoid these preservatives, learn the common INCI names of formaldehyde-releasers and scan ingredient lists. Remember that 'preservative-free' is not automatically safer, since adequate preservation prevents microbial spoilage. Requirements and disclosures differ by market, so confirm current rules with official regulatory sources rather than treating this summary as definitive.
