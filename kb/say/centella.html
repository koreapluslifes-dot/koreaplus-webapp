How to pronounce Centella (Cica) 병풀

⚡ Quick answer: Centella (Cica) (병풀) is pronounced "byung-pool", romanized byeongpul. Meaning: Korean common name for Centella asiatica ("byeongpul"); "cica" is short for Latin Centella.

How do you pronounce Centella (Cica)? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 병풀
  • Romanization: byeongpul
  • Say it like: byung-pool
