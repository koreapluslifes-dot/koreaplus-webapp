How to pronounce Snail Mucin 달팽이 점액

⚡ Quick answer: Snail Mucin (달팽이 점액) is pronounced "dal-peng-ee jum-aek", romanized dalpaengi jeomaek. Meaning: "Snail mucus/secretion" (dalpaengi = snail, jeomaek = mucus).

How do you pronounce Snail Mucin? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 달팽이 점액
  • Romanization: dalpaengi jeomaek
  • Say it like: dal-peng-ee jum-aek

What Snail Mucin means

"Snail mucus/secretion" (dalpaengi = snail, jeomaek = mucus).
