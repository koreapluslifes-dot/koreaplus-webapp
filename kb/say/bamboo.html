How to pronounce Bamboo Sap 대나무 수액

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Bamboo Sap (대나무 수액) is pronounced "DEH-nah-moo SOO-ek", romanized daenamu suaek. Meaning: 대나무 is bamboo.

How do you pronounce Bamboo Sap? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 대나무 수액
  • Romanization: daenamu suaek
  • Say it like: DEH-nah-moo SOO-ek
