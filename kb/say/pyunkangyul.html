How to pronounce Pyunkang Yul 편강율

⚡ Quick answer: Pyunkang Yul (편강율) is pronounced "pyun-gang-yool", romanized Pyeongang-yul. Meaning: From "pyeongang" (peaceful/healthy lungs, an oriental-medicine concept) — name of the affiliated Korean clinic.
