How to pronounce Hanyul 한율

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Hanyul (한율) is pronounced "HAHN-yool", romanized hanyul. Meaning: 한 (han, Korean/one) + 율 (yul, rhythm or law) — roughly 'the Korean rhythm'.

How do you pronounce Hanyul? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 한율
  • Romanization: hanyul
  • Say it like: HAHN-yool

What Hanyul means

한 (han, Korean/one) + 율 (yul, rhythm or law) — roughly 'the Korean rhythm'.
