How to pronounce Dr.Jart+ 닥터자르트

⚡ Quick answer: Dr.Jart+ (닥터자르트) is pronounced "dok-tur zhart", romanized Dakteo Jareuteu. Meaning: "Dr." + a coinage of "Jart" said to blend "join" and "art" (doctor joins art).

How do you pronounce Dr.Jart+? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 닥터자르트
  • Romanization: Dakteo Jareuteu
  • Say it like: dok-tur zhart
