How to pronounce Innisfree 이니스프리

⚡ Quick answer: Innisfree (이니스프리) is pronounced "in-iss-free", romanized Iniseupeuri. Meaning: From "The Lake Isle of Innisfree," a W. B. Yeats poem; evokes a pure natural island.

How do you pronounce Innisfree? Here's the Korean — Hangul, romanization and an easy English respelling.

🗣️ Say it right
  • Hangul: 이니스프리
  • Romanization: Iniseupeuri
  • Say it like: in-iss-free
