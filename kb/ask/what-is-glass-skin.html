What is glass skin?

⚡ Quick answer: Glass skin is a Korean beauty ideal describing a complexion so smooth, even and hydrated it looks translucent and reflective, like a pane of glass. It isn't a single product but the result of diligent hydration, gentle exfoliation and sun protection layered over a healthy skin barrier.

Glass skin is a Korean beauty ideal describing a complexion so smooth, even and hydrated it looks translucent and reflective, like a pane of glass. It isn't a single product but the result of diligent hydration, gentle exfoliation and sun protection layered over a healthy skin barrier.

More detail

The look emphasizes a poreless-appearing, dewy finish rather than matte coverage, achieved through consistent layering of hydrating toners, essences and moisturizers.

🇰🇷 The term originated in Korean skincare culture and went global as 'yuri-pibu' (유리 피부), Korean for glass skin.
