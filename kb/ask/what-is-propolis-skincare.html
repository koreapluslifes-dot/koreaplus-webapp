What is propolis in skincare?

⚡ Quick answer: Propolis is a bee-derived, honey-like extract used in Korean skincare for a comforting, nourishing glow and antioxidant support. It's popular in soothing essences and ampoules for dry or stressed skin. Anyone with a bee-product allergy should avoid it and patch-test before regular use.
