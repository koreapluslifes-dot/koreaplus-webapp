What is skin cycling?

⚡ Quick answer: Skin cycling is a rotation method that spaces out active ingredients across nights, alternating exfoliation, treatment and recovery, to avoid overloading skin. While the trend name is recent, the gentle, barrier-protective spirit aligns closely with Korean skincare's caution against using too many actives at once.
