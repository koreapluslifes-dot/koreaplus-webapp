What is the 10-step Korean skincare routine?

⚡ Quick answer: The 10-step Korean skincare routine is a layered framework popularized in Korea: oil cleanser, water cleanser, exfoliant, toner, essence, serum or ampoule, sheet mask, eye cream, moisturizer and sunscreen. It's a customizable menu, not a daily mandate, and most people use only the steps their skin needs.

More detail

Steps are spread across morning and evening, with exfoliation and masks used only a few times weekly rather than daily.

🇰🇷 The sequence reflects the Korean philosophy of gentle, gradual layering over harsh, one-step treatments.
