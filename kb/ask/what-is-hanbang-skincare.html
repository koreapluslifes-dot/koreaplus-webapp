What is hanbang Korean skincare?

⚡ Quick answer: Hanbang refers to Korean skincare rooted in traditional herbal medicine, using botanicals like ginseng, mugwort and licorice. These ingredients appear in premium and heritage Korean lines, especially anti-ageing products, blending old herbal traditions with modern cosmetic formulation.
