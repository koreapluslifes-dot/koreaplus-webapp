What is skip-care in Korean skincare?

⚡ Quick answer: Skip-care is a Korean minimalist trend that streamlines routines down to a few effective, multitasking products instead of many layers. It emerged as a reaction to the elaborate 10-step routine, prioritizing skin health, efficiency and avoiding product overload while keeping core steps like hydration and sun protection.
