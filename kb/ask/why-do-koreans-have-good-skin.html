Why do Koreans have good skin?

⚡ Quick answer: Clear skin reflects many factors, including genetics, diet and climate, not skincare alone. That said, Korean culture strongly emphasizes consistent daily routines, gentle hydration-focused care and diligent sun protection from a young age, habits that support healthy-looking skin over time. Individual results always vary.
