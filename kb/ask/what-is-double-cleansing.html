What is double cleansing?

⚡ Quick answer: Double cleansing is a two-step Korean method: first an oil-based cleanser dissolves makeup, sunscreen and sebum, then a water-based cleanser removes sweat and water-soluble debris. The pairing aims to clean thoroughly without the tightness or stripping that one harsh wash can cause.

More detail

It's especially recommended at night to remove long-wear sunscreen and makeup that water alone struggles to break down.

🇰🇷 Double cleansing is a foundational first step in Korean routines, where sun protection is worn daily and needs careful removal.
