Is Korean skincare good for acne?

⚡ Quick answer: Korean skincare can suit blemish-prone skin through gentle, barrier-friendly care using ingredients like centella, heartleaf, niacinamide and BHA. The approach favors soothing over stripping, since over-cleansing can worsen oil and breakouts. Persistent or severe acne should be assessed by a dermatologist rather than self-treated.
