What is PDRN or salmon DNA in skincare?

⚡ Quick answer: PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide), sometimes called salmon DNA, is an ingredient trending in Korean skincare and aesthetic treatments, marketed for a smoother, more revitalized look. In cosmetics its effects are described in appearance terms; stronger claims belong to clinical procedures, not over-the-counter products.
