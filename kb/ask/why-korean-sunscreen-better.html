Why is Korean sunscreen better?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Because Korea approves modern UV filters — Uvinul A Plus, Tinosorb S and M among them — that give strong UVA protection in light, non-greasy textures. Some markets, notably the US, have not approved them.

Because Korea approves modern UV filters — Uvinul A Plus, Tinosorb S and M among them — that give strong UVA protection in light, non-greasy textures. Some markets, notably the US, have not approved them.

More detail

It is a regulatory difference, not a chemistry secret. Korea regulates sunscreen as a functional cosmetic with a workable approval path; the US regulates it as an over-the-counter drug, which has made new filters extremely slow to arrive.

🇰🇷 Korean sunscreens show both SPF for UVB and a PA rating for UVA.
