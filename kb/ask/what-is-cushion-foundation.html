What is a cushion foundation?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A cushion foundation is a Korean makeup innovation: liquid foundation soaked into a sponge inside a compact, applied with a puff for a dewy, buildable, skincare-infused finish. Many include sun protection and hydrating ingredients, blurring the line between makeup and skincare.

More detail

🇰🇷 The cushion compact was invented in Korea and became one of K-beauty's biggest global exports.
