What is the skin barrier and why does K-beauty focus on it?

⚡ Quick answer: The skin barrier is the outermost layer that locks in moisture and keeps out irritants. Korean skincare emphasizes protecting and supporting it because a healthy barrier looks calmer, hydrated and more resilient. The philosophy favors gentle, hydrating care over aggressive actives that can compromise it.
