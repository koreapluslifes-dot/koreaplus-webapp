Home ultrasound and radiofrequency devices: managing expectations

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Home radiofrequency and ultrasound devices work by warming the deeper layers of skin, and Korean brands have made them a mainstream category. They are meaningfully weaker than the clinic equipment they are modelled on, so treat them as gradual maintenance rather than as a substitute for a procedure.

Home radiofrequency and ultrasound devices work by warming the deeper layers of skin, and Korean brands have made them a mainstream category. They are meaningfully weaker than the clinic equipment they are modelled on, so treat them as gradual maintenance rather than as a substitute for a procedure.

The mechanism

Radiofrequency delivers controlled heat below the skin surface, which is associated with the appearance of firmer skin over time. Home "ultrasound" devices are mostly low-intensity sonic or ultrasound heads that warm the surface and help spread product — not the focused ultrasound (HIFU) used in clinics, which is a different and far more powerful thing. The principle is borrowed from the clinic treatments; the energy is a small fraction of it. This is the same principle behind the clinic treatments, at a fraction of the energy.

Why Korea leads the category

Korea's aesthetic-clinic culture is unusually mainstream, and home devices grew directly out of it — a way to extend between-appointment maintenance at home. Korean brands have become some of the most visible in the category worldwide as a result, even though the largest players by revenue remain Japanese, American and European.

The power gap is the point

Home devices are deliberately limited for safety — Korean rules cap the skin-contact temperature of these devices at 43°C. That limitation is why they are safer to use unsupervised and why their results are gentler; both facts are true at once. Safer is not the same as harmless: a 2025 Korea Consumer Agency review of handheld home skin devices warned that exceeding the stated time or intensity can cause burns and skin damage. The limits printed on the box are the product, not a suggestion.

Use them safely

Follow the recommended session frequency rather than exceeding it, use the conductive gel specified, and avoid the eye area unless the manufacturer explicitly permits it. If you have had injectables or have an implanted device, check with your clinician first.
