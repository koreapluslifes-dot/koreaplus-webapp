Propolis: what the evidence says

⚡ Quick answer: Propolis gives a nourishing glow with antioxidant support for blemish-prone skin. — Evidence grade: Emerging. Propolis is a bee-derived resin extract used in K-beauty for a comforting, nourishing, glowy finish and antioxidant support. Lab studies show antioxidant and antimicrobial activity, but standardized topical cosmetic concentrations and robust independent clinical skin data are limited, and composition varies widely by source. Benefits are best framed as conditioning and appearance-related rather than therapeutic. Important caveat: propolis is a notable contact-allergy and bee-product sensitizer, so anyone with a bee or honey allergy should avoid it and others should patch-test.
