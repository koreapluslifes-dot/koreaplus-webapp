Heartleaf (Houttuynia cordata): what the evidence says

⚡ Quick answer: Heartleaf calms and balances oily, breakout-prone skin. — Evidence grade: Emerging. Heartleaf (Houttuynia cordata, 'eoseongcho') is a trending Korean calming botanical used to support a soothed, balanced look in oily and blemish-prone skin. It contains flavonoids and other compounds with antioxidant and soothing activity in laboratory studies, but high-quality clinical evidence for topical cosmetic use is limited and there is no standard use percentage. Frame its value as comfort and the appearance of calmer, less reactive skin rather than as a treatment for breakouts. Generally well tolerated; patch-test reactive skin.
