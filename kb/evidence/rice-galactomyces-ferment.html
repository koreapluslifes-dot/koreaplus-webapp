Rice & Galactomyces Ferment: what the evidence says

⚡ Quick answer: Rice and fermented-ferment essences brighten dull skin for a glassy mochi-skin glow. — Evidence grade: Emerging. Rice extracts and fermented ferment filtrates such as galactomyces are heritage and modern K-beauty ingredients prized for a soft, luminous, brightened-looking finish. They act largely as humectants and conditioning agents rich in amino acids, vitamins and antioxidants, which can make skin look more radiant and feel smoother. Independent clinical evidence is limited, fermentation composition varies by batch and brand, and there is no standardized cosmetic percentage. Benefits are best described as glow and softness of appearance rather than proven brightening of pigment.
