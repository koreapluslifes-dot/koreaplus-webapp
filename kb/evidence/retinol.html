Retinol: what the evidence says

⚡ Quick answer: Retinol is the gold standard for softening lines and refining texture over time. — Evidence grade: Strong. Retinol is among the best-evidenced cosmetic actives for improving the look of fine lines, uneven texture and tone with consistent long-term use, typically in the 0.1-1% range. It works gradually, so start low and infrequent (1-2 times weekly), build up, use it only at night, and always pair with daytime SPF since it can increase sun sensitivity and initial irritation. Avoid during pregnancy and breastfeeding as a precaution. Effects are real but require patience and tolerance-building.
