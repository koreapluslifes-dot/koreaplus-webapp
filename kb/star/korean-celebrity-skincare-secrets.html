Korean celebrity skincare secrets (the science)

Consistency beats intensity

Doing a simple routine every morning and night outperforms occasional aggressive treatments — a core Korean philosophy.

Barrier-first

Protecting the skin barrier (ceramides, centella, avoiding over-exfoliation) keeps skin resilient and glowing.

Professional support

Many also rely on dermatologist guidance and in-clinic care; at home, the routine stays gentle and hydration-led.
