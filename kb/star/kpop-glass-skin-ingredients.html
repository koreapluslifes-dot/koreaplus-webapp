The Korean ingredients behind K-pop glass skin

⚡ Quick answer: The Korean ingredients most linked to the K-pop "glass skin" look are hydrators (hyaluronic acid, snail mucin, beta-glucan), soothers (centella/cica, heartleaf) and gentle brighteners (niacinamide, rice extract) — all layered over diligent SPF.

The Korean ingredients most linked to the K-pop "glass skin" look are hydrators (hyaluronic acid, snail mucin, beta-glucan), soothers (centella/cica, heartleaf) and gentle brighteners (niacinamide, rice extract) — all layered over diligent SPF.

Hydrators for the dewy base

Hyaluronic acid, snail mucin and beta-glucan draw in and hold water for the plump, reflective finish.

Soothers for an even tone

Centella (cica) and heartleaf help calm redness so skin looks uniform — key to the glass effect.

Brighteners for glow

Niacinamide and traditional rice extract support a brighter, more luminous appearance over time.
