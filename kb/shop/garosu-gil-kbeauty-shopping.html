Garosu-gil: Tree-Lined Boutique Beauty Shopping

Garosu-gil, a tree-lined street in the Sinsa-dong area of Gangnam, is known for stylish boutiques, cafés, and design-led concept stores — a pleasant setting for curated, aesthetic-forward beauty shopping.

What Garosu-gil is

Garosu-gil (literally 'tree-lined street') is a well-known shopping street in Sinsa-dong, Gangnam District, south of the Han River. It became popular for its ginkgo-lined walkway, fashion boutiques, cafés, and a generally design-conscious atmosphere that contrasts with the high-volume bustle of districts like Myeongdong.

What to expect for beauty

The street and its side alleys (sometimes called 'Serosu-gil') host concept stores and flagship-style boutiques, including beauty and cosmetics brands that use the area to showcase brand experiences. The focus tends to be on aesthetics, photography-friendly interiors, and curated ranges rather than deep discounting, so it suits shoppers who enjoy browsing and brand storytelling.

Tips for visitors

The area is walkable from Sinsa Station. It's especially enjoyable in autumn when the ginkgo trees turn yellow. As trendy retail spaces open and close frequently here, check whether a specific brand's concept store is still operating before making a dedicated trip, and confirm current hours.

Key facts
  • Garosu-gil is a tree-lined shopping street in Sinsa-dong, Gangnam
  • Known for boutiques, cafés, and design-led concept stores
  • Hosts brand experience and flagship-style beauty shops
  • Atmosphere is curated and aesthetic-focused, not discount-driven
  • Walkable from Sinsa Station
  • Concept stores open and close often — verify before visiting
