Olive Young: Korea's Leading Health & Beauty Chain Explained

Olive Young is widely regarded as Korea's leading health-and-beauty store chain, functioning as a one-stop multi-brand retailer where you can compare a huge range of K-beauty products in person.

What Olive Young is

Olive Young is a Korean health-and-beauty (drugstore-style) retail chain operating a large network of stores across the country, alongside online channels. It carries skincare, makeup, haircare, body care, and wellness items spanning major brands, indie labels, and trending products, which is why it's often the first stop for both locals and visitors wanting variety in one place.

How to shop it

Stores are organised by category and brand, with frequent promotions, testers, and curated displays highlighting popular or new items. Larger flagship branches (such as those in Myeongdong) carry broader ranges and more indie brands. Membership and app-based discounts exist primarily for local shoppers; foreign visitors can often access tax refunds on qualifying purchases — bring your passport.

Tips and caveats

Because Olive Young curates by trend, it's an efficient way to find viral products, but in-store selection and promotions vary by branch and over time. Online catalogues (domestic and the separate global/US sites) differ from in-store stock and from each other, so don't assume an item seen online is on a given shelf. Verify current store hours, since these change.

Key facts
  • Olive Young is Korea's leading health-and-beauty store chain
  • Operates a large nationwide store network plus online channels
  • Carries major brands, indie labels, and trending products
  • Flagship branches stock broader ranges and more indie brands
  • Tax refunds often available for eligible foreign shoppers
  • Online and in-store selection differ; stock varies by branch
