Myeongdong: The Heart of K-Beauty Shopping in Seoul

Myeongdong is widely regarded as the most concentrated K-beauty shopping district in Seoul, packing brand flagships, multi-brand stores, and street food into a compact, walkable grid.

What makes Myeongdong special

Myeongdong, in central Seoul near Myeongdong Station (Seoul Metro Line 4), has long been one of the city's premier shopping and tourist districts. For beauty shoppers it functions almost like an open-air department store: within a roughly one-square-kilometre area you'll find flagship boutiques for major Korean brands alongside large multi-brand retailers. It is especially popular with international visitors, so signage and staff often accommodate English, Chinese, and Japanese speakers.

What you'll find

Expect standalone flagship stores from well-known Korean brands such as Innisfree, Etude House, and Laneige, plus one of the larger branches of the Olive Young health-and-beauty chain. Single-brand stores let you explore a full range in one place, while multi-brand shops are better for comparing across brands. Many shops cluster along the main pedestrian corridor and the alleys branching off it.

Practical tips

Mornings are generally quieter than evenings, when the street-food stalls and crowds appear. Many stores offer tax-refund processing for eligible foreign visitors on qualifying purchases — bring your passport. Because tenants and store layouts change frequently in such a high-turnover district, verify current store locations and opening hours online before a special trip.

Key facts
  • Myeongdong is one of Seoul's most concentrated K-beauty shopping districts
  • Home to brand flagships (e.g., Innisfree, Laneige) and large Olive Young branches
  • Near Myeongdong Station on Seoul Metro Line 4
  • Popular with international tourists; multilingual signage is common
  • Tax-refund processing is widely available for eligible foreign shoppers
  • Store tenants change often — verify current hours and locations
