Korean skincare during pregnancy: what usually stays and what usually goes

Why a Korean routine adapts well

The core of a Korean routine is hydration and barrier support — humectants, ceramides, panthenol, centella, gentle cleansing and daily sunscreen. Very little of that is on anyone's caution list, which means the shape of the routine usually survives pregnancy intact even when a few products come out of it.

The ingredients most commonly paused

Retinoids are the clearest case, and topical retinoids are widely advised against during pregnancy. High-strength exfoliating acids and certain brightening agents are also commonly paused, more out of caution than because strong evidence exists against typical cosmetic use. Some essential oils and botanical extracts carry their own advisories.

What frequently becomes more relevant

Melasma often appears or worsens during pregnancy, driven partly by hormones and partly by sun and heat. Since the usual brightening actives may be paused, sun protection carries far more of the load than usual — a hat, shade and reapplied sunscreen genuinely matter here.

The honest disclaimer

This is general educational information, not medical advice, and cosmetic ingredient guidance during pregnancy varies between countries and clinicians. Bring your actual products to your appointment and ask — it takes two minutes and replaces a lot of internet guessing.
