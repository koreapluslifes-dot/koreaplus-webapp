Olive Young: Korea's Beauty Retail Hub and Haul Culture

Olive Young, a major South Korean health-and-beauty retail chain, has become a hub for discovering products and a focal point of K-beauty 'haul' culture.

A dominant beauty retailer

Olive Young is widely recognized as South Korea's leading multi-brand health-and-beauty store chain, with a large presence across the country. Its stores stock a broad mix of skincare, makeup, and personal-care brands, making it a popular one-stop destination.

Haul culture

The term 'haul' refers to buying and then showcasing many products at once, often shared online. Olive Young is frequently featured in K-beauty haul videos and tourist shopping guides, reflecting its status as a go-to spot for trying trending items.

A tourist and trend barometer

For many visitors, an Olive Young trip is a popular part of experiencing K-beauty. The chain is also seen as a barometer of trending products. Specific market-share or revenue figures vary by source and year, so they are best omitted here.

Key facts

  • It stocks many skincare, makeup, and personal-care brands
