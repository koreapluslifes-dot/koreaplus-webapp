Skin-Fasting: The Minimalist Counter-Trend in K-Beauty

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Skin-fasting is the practice of temporarily simplifying or pausing a skincare routine to 'let skin rest.' Here is what it is and how it is discussed.

Skin-fasting is a minimalist practice, discussed within and beyond Korean beauty, of deliberately scaling back products to give skin a break from a heavy routine.

What skin-fasting involves

Skin-fasting generally means temporarily reducing a skincare routine to its essentials, or occasionally pausing most products, on the theory that skin can benefit from a rest. Approaches range from a single product-free night to a longer simplified period. Definitions vary across sources.

Why it appeals

The practice is often framed as a counterbalance to elaborate multi-step routines, appealing to those who suspect over-layering may irritate their skin. It reflects a broader minimalist 'skip-care' current that gained attention in the late 2010s.

An evidence caveat

Skin-fasting is a popular concept rather than a clinically established protocol, and dermatological opinion is mixed. Sun protection in particular is widely advised even when simplifying, and anyone using prescribed treatments should follow professional guidance rather than pausing on their own.

Key facts
  • Skin-fasting means simplifying or pausing a routine to 'rest' skin
  • It is a minimalist counter-trend to multi-step routines
  • Definitions and durations vary widely
  • It is a popular concept, not a clinically established protocol
  • Sun protection is generally still advised
