Isopropyl Myristate: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A fast-absorbing ester that adds slip and a non-greasy softness to lotions and serums.

A fast-absorbing ester that adds slip and a non-greasy softness to lotions and serums.

What it does

Function: emollient (ester). Emollients soften and smooth the skin surface; occlusives form a light seal that slows water loss — the "lock it in" step after watery layers.

Heads-up flags
  • comedogenic-risk

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Isopropyl Myristate. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all emollients & occlusives →
