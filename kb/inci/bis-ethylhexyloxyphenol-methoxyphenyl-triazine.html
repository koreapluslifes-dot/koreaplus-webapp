Tinosorb S: what it is in skincare

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A modern broad-spectrum filter that helps absorb both UVA and UVB rays for stable sun protection.

A modern broad-spectrum filter that helps absorb both UVA and UVB rays for stable sun protection.

What it does

Function: UV filter (chemical, broad-spectrum). UV filters absorb or reflect UV. Korea approves several elegant next-generation filters not yet available in the US, a key reason Korean sunscreens feel so light.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Tinosorb S. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all uv filters (sunscreen) →
