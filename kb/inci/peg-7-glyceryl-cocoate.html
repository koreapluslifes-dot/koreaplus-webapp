PEG-7 Glyceryl Cocoate: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A mild cleansing helper that supports softer foam and helps leave a conditioned feel on skin.

A mild cleansing helper that supports softer foam and helps leave a conditioned feel on skin.

What it does

Function: surfactant / emollient. Emollients soften and smooth the skin surface; occlusives form a light seal that slows water loss — the "lock it in" step after watery layers.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for PEG-7 Glyceryl Cocoate. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all emollients & occlusives →
