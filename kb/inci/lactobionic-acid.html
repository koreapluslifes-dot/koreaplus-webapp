Lactobionic Acid: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A large polyhydroxy acid that helps gently exfoliate and supports the skin's moisture barrier.

A large polyhydroxy acid that helps gently exfoliate and supports the skin's moisture barrier.

What it does

Function: exfoliant (PHA). Chemical exfoliants loosen dead surface cells for smoother, clearer-looking skin. K-beauty tends to favour low, gentle, gradual percentages.

Heads-up flags
  • sensitive-skin-caution

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Lactobionic Acid. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all exfoliants (aha/bha/pha) →
