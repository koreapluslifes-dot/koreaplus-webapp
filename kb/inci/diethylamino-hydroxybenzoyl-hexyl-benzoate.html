Uvinul A Plus: what it is in skincare

A photostable filter that helps absorb UVA rays to support defense against sun-induced aging.

What it does

Function: UV filter (chemical, UVA). UV filters absorb or reflect UV. Korea approves several elegant next-generation filters not yet available in the US, a key reason Korean sunscreens feel so light.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Uvinul A Plus. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all uv filters (sunscreen) →
