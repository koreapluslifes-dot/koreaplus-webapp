Octocrylene: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A filter that helps absorb UV light and supports the stability of other sunscreen ingredients.

A filter that helps absorb UV light and supports the stability of other sunscreen ingredients.

What it does

Function: UV filter (chemical). UV filters absorb or reflect UV. Korea approves several elegant next-generation filters not yet available in the US, a key reason Korean sunscreens feel so light.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Octocrylene. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all uv filters (sunscreen) →
