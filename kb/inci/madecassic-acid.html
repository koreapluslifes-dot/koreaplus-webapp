Madecassic Acid: what it is in skincare

One of centella's key actives that helps soothe and support skin looking tired or sensitized.

What it does

Function: soothing. Soothing actives help comfort the look of redness and sensitivity — a defining strength of Korean cica/centella and heartleaf formulas.

  • hero

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Madecassic Acid. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all soothing / calming →
