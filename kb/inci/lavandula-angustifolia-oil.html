Lavender Oil: what it is in skincare

A lavender essential oil that adds natural scent and aroma but can sensitize reactive skin.

What it does

Function: essential oil / fragrance. Fragrance and essential oils add scent but can be a sensitivity trigger — useful to recognise on a label if your skin is reactive.

Heads-up flags
  • fragrance
  • allergen
  • sensitive-skin-caution

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Lavender Oil. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all fragrance & essential oils →
