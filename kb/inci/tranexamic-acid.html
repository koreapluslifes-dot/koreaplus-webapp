Tranexamic Acid: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: An ingredient included to support a more even-looking tone and reduce the look of dark spots.

What it does

Function: brightening. Brightening actives target the look of dark spots and uneven tone for a more radiant, even finish.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Tranexamic Acid. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all brightening actives →
