Butylparaben: what it is in skincare

A preservative that helps guard a formula against bacteria and mold over its shelf life.

What it does

Function: preservative. Preservatives keep water-based formulas safe from microbial growth. Modern, well-tolerated systems are why your essence stays safe for months.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Butylparaben. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all preservatives →
