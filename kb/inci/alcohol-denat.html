Denatured Alcohol: what it is in skincare

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A fast-evaporating alcohol that helps products feel light but can be drying with frequent use.

A fast-evaporating alcohol that helps products feel light but can be drying with frequent use.

What it does

Function: solvent (drying alcohol). Solvents dissolve and carry other ingredients. Some lightweight alcohols also give a fast-absorbing finish (a sensitivity point for some skin).

Heads-up flags
  • alcohol-drying
  • sensitive-skin-caution

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Denatured Alcohol. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all solvents →
