Tromethamine: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A buffering agent that helps neutralize and stabilize the pH of a gel or lotion.

A buffering agent that helps neutralize and stabilize the pH of a gel or lotion.

What it does

Function: ph-adjuster. pH adjusters fine-tune a formula to skin-friendly acidity, which keeps both the product and your skin barrier happy.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Tromethamine. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all ph adjusters →
