1,2-Hexanediol: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A lightweight ingredient that supports skin hydration while helping keep the formula stable and fresh.

A lightweight ingredient that supports skin hydration while helping keep the formula stable and fresh.

What it does

Function: humectant / solvent. Humectants draw water into the upper layers of skin, the backbone of K-beauty "dewy" hydration. They work best sandwiched under an emollient or occlusive that locks the water in.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for 1,2-Hexanediol. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all humectants →
