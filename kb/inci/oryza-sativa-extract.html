Rice (Oryza Sativa) Extract: what it is in skincare

A rice-derived extract that helps brighten the look of dull skin and supports a smooth tone.

What it does

Function: brightening/antioxidant. Antioxidants help defend skin against the visible effects of daily environmental stress and support a brighter, more even-looking tone.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Rice (Oryza Sativa) Extract. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all antioxidants →
