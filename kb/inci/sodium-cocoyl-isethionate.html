Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate: what it is in skincare

A soft coconut-based cleanser that helps lift away dirt and oil while supporting a creamy, gentle lather.

What it does

Function: surfactant (mild). Surfactants lift away oil, sunscreen and grime. K-beauty favours mild amino-acid and amphoteric surfactants that clean without stripping.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all cleansing agents (surfactants) →
