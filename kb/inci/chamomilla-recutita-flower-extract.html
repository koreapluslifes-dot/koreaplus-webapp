Chamomile Extract: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A chamomile extract that helps calm the look of redness and soothe sensitive skin.

A chamomile extract that helps calm the look of redness and soothe sensitive skin.

What it does

Function: soothing. Soothing actives help comfort the look of redness and sensitivity — a defining strength of Korean cica/centella and heartleaf formulas.

Heads-up flags
  • allergen

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Chamomile Extract. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all soothing / calming →
