Dimethicone Crosspolymer: what it is in skincare

⚡ Quick answer: A bouncy silicone gel that blurs the look of pores and gives a soft, velvety finish.

A bouncy silicone gel that blurs the look of pores and gives a soft, velvety finish.

What it does

Function: emollient (silicone elastomer). Emollients soften and smooth the skin surface; occlusives form a light seal that slows water loss — the "lock it in" step after watery layers.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Dimethicone Crosspolymer. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all emollients & occlusives →
