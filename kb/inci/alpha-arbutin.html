Alpha-Arbutin: what it is in skincare

A gentle brightening ingredient that helps reduce the look of dark spots for a more even tone.

What it does

Function: brightening. Brightening actives target the look of dark spots and uneven tone for a more radiant, even finish.

Heads-up flags
  • pregnancy-caution

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Alpha-Arbutin. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all brightening actives →
