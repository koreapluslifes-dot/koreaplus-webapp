Trehalose: what it is in skincare

A protective sugar that holds water and helps skin stay hydrated and comfortable under dryness or stress.

What it does

Function: humectant. Humectants draw water into the upper layers of skin, the backbone of K-beauty "dewy" hydration. They work best sandwiched under an emollient or occlusive that locks the water in.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Trehalose. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all humectants →
