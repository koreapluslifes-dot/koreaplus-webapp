Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5: what it is in skincare

A peptide that supports a plumper, firmer-looking complexion and helps soften the appearance of lines.

What it does

Function: peptide (signal). Peptides are signal ingredients formulated to support a firmer, bouncier-looking complexion — a fast-growing K-beauty category.

How to spot it on a label

On a Korean product's INCI list, look for Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5. Ingredients are listed high-to-low by amount, so its position hints at how much is in the formula. See all peptides →
