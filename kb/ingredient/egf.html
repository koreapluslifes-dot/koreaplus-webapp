EGF-type Oligopeptide: K-beauty ingredient guide 이지에프(올리고펩타이드)

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Sold in Korea as sh-Oligopeptide-1 and heavily marketed. Read the claims carefully: cosmetics are regulated on appearance, and a topical peptide is not the same thing as a clinical growth-factor treatment. Treat it as a conditioning peptide whose independent evidence is thin — almost all the published support comes from the companies selling it.
