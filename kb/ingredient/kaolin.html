Kaolin Clay: K-beauty ingredient guide 카올린

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: The gentlest of the common cosmetic clays, and the one Korean wash-off masks lean on when they want oil control without a stripped face. Kaolin soaks up sebum sitting on the surface. It does not reach into a pore, dissolve a blackhead or shrink anything — those are jobs for a BHA, if they are jobs at all.

The gentlest of the common cosmetic clays, and the one Korean wash-off masks lean on when they want oil control without a stripped face. Kaolin soaks up sebum sitting on the surface. It does not reach into a pore, dissolve a blackhead or shrink anything — those are jobs for a BHA, if they are jobs at all.

What Kaolin Clay does for your skin

  • Absorbs surface oil without the tightness of stronger clays
  • Gives wash-off masks their body and mattifying finish

Best for

🛢️ Oil control🕳️ Pores & texture⚫ Blackheads & sebaceous filaments
