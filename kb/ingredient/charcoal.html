Activated Charcoal: K-beauty ingredient guide 활성탄

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Charcoal binds things to its surface, which is why it works in water filters and why it looks convincing in a black clay mask. On skin it picks up surface oil during the couple of minutes it is there. The detox framing is nonsense — nothing is being drawn out of a pore, and a peel-off charcoal mask mostly removes your vellus hair.
