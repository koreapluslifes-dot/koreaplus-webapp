Rosehip Oil for acne-prone skin

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Does Rosehip Oil suit acne-prone skin? Rich in linoleic acid and naturally occurring carotenoids. What it does for acne-prone skin, when to use it (PM), and how to work it into a Korean routine.

Is Rosehip Oil good for acne-prone skin?

It oxidises faster than most oils, so buy small bottles, keep it dark and cool, and stop using it once it smells sharp.

Why it suits acne-prone skin

  • Rich in linoleic acid and naturally occurring carotenoids
  • Popular in facial-oil steps for the look of even tone
💡 How to use: PM · patch-test and introduce gradually.
