Does Licorice Root (Glycyrrhiza) help with reactive & easily-stung skin? Here's the honest, structure-function read.

Gamcho is one of the most-used herbs in Korean traditional medicine and it carried straight over into hanbang skincare, where it does double duty as a soother and a gentle tone-evener.

Why it helps reactive & easily-stung skin

  • Traditionally used to calm the look of redness
  • Supports a more even-looking tone alongside other brighteners
💡 How to use: BOTH · introduce gradually and patch-test. Introduce one product at a time and wait two weeks. Reactive skin is where the minimal Korean routine genuinely outperforms the ten-step one.
