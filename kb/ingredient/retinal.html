Retinaldehyde (Retinal): K-beauty ingredient guide 레티날

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: The middle rung of the retinoid ladder. Because it needs fewer conversion steps than retinol, it is generally considered more potent at the same percentage — start low, go slow, and always pair it with sunscreen the next morning. Retinoids are also the one category conventionally avoided altogether in pregnancy and breastfeeding: here, 'caution' means skip it until after.

What Retinaldehyde (Retinal) does for your skin

  • One conversion step closer to retinoic acid than retinol
  • Often described as working faster than retinol at similar tolerance

Best for

⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness🔴 Acne & breakouts🪨 Rough & uneven texture🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)
