Centella (Cica) for oily skin

Is Centella (Cica) good for oily skin?

“Cica” = Centella asiatica (tiger grass). The go-to soother for sensitive, stressed or breakout-prone skin.

Why it suits oily skin

  • Helps calm visible redness & irritation
  • Supports a comfortable, resilient barrier
💡 How to use: BOTH · patch-test and introduce gradually.
