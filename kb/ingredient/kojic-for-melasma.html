Kojic Acid for melasma & stubborn patches

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Does Kojic Acid help melasma & stubborn patches? A fermentation-derived brightener used for uneven-looking tone. How it works, when to apply it (PM), and how to introduce it without irritating your skin.

Does Kojic Acid help with melasma & stubborn patches? Here's the honest, structure-function read.

A by-product of the same koji fermentation used to make sake and soy sauce. It can be sensitising for some people, so it is usually formulated at modest levels and in company with gentler brighteners.

Why it helps melasma & stubborn patches

  • A fermentation-derived brightener used for uneven-looking tone
  • Frequently combined with niacinamide or arbutin
💡 How to use: PM · introduce gradually and patch-test. Melasma is recurrent and genuinely difficult — it is worth seeing a dermatologist. Daily broad-spectrum SPF, reapplied, does more than any serum, and heat alone can trigger it even without direct sun.
