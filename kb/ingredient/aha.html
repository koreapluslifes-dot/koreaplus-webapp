AHA (Glycolic/Lactic): K-beauty ingredient guide AHA

Water-loving acids that resurface the top layer. Lactic is gentler; glycolic is stronger. SPF a must.

What AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) does for your skin

  • Surface exfoliation for glow & even tone

Best for

🌟 Dullness & glow🟤 Dark spots & tone🕳️ Pores & texture

Pairs well with

💧 Hyaluronic Acid🧫 Niacinamide (B3)
⚠️ Be careful pairing with: Retinol, BHA (Salicylic) — introduce gradually or alternate nights.
🤰 Pregnancy: Generally considered fine — confirm with your doctor. · ⏰ Use: PM
