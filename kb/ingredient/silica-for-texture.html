Silica for rough & uneven texture

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Does Silica help rough & uneven texture? Absorbs surface oil so skin looks matte for longer. How it works, when to apply it (AM), and how to introduce it without irritating your skin.

Does Silica help with rough & uneven texture? Here's the honest, structure-function read.

The workhorse of Korean primers, sun sticks and mattifying creams. Silica is porous mineral powder: it soaks up oil and diffuses light, so shine takes longer to break through and pores look shallower than they are. The effect is optical and temporary, which is exactly what a primer should be honest about.

Why it helps rough & uneven texture

  • Absorbs surface oil so skin looks matte for longer
  • Scatters light, which softens the look of pores and fine texture
💡 How to use: AM · introduce gradually and patch-test. Give any exfoliant a full eight weeks before judging it, and never stack two. Texture responds to consistency far better than to intensity.
