How much Hydrogenated Lecithin actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %0.5–3%
Optimal pH4.0–7.0
EvidenceModerate
Onsetnot applicable — a delivery and emulsifying ingredient

What the % on the label really means

Its presence does not prove a product is liposomal. That claim needs the brand to show its work, and most do not.

⚠️ Ceiling: High levels can smell faintly nutty and darken a pale formula over time.
