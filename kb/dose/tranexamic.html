How much Tranexamic Acid actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %2–5%
Optimal pH5.0–7.0
EvidenceModerate
Onset8–12 weeks

What the % on the label really means

2–5% tranexamic acid is the usual effective window for tone; a higher % isn't a proven upgrade, so pairing beats dose-chasing.

⚠️ Ceiling: Above ~5% topically shows little added benefit; often paired with niacinamide rather than pushed higher.
