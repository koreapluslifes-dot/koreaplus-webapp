What the % on the label really means

1–5% panthenol on a label is plenty; it's a gentle hydrator/soother where higher numbers don't translate to visibly better results.

⚠️ Ceiling: Above ~5% adds little and can feel slightly tacky; it's a supporting soother, not a dose-chasing active.
