What the % on the label really means

If a Korean cream carries the 기능성화장품 (functional cosmetic) mark for 주름개선 on the strength of adenosine, it contains exactly 0.04% — a regulated figure, not a marketing one. Adenosine on the ingredient list without that mark tells you nothing about the dose.

⚠️ Ceiling: This is a notified functional concentration rather than a dose-response curve — more is not the point.
