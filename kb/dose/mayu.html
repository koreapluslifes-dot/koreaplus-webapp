How much Mayu (Horse Oil) actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %not standardised — from a few percent in creams to the bulk of a balm
Optimal pHnot relevant; it is an oil phase ingredient
EvidenceLimited
Onsetimmediate (feel)

What the % on the label really means

Very good on chapped, windburnt-looking winter skin. Nothing about it is unique enough to justify a premium over ceramides.

⚠️ Ceiling: It is a heavy occlusive, so high levels on oily or acne-prone skin are a poor idea regardless of the folklore.
