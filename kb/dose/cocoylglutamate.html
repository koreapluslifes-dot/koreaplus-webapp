Sodium Cocoyl Glutamate: effective % & pH 소듐코코일글루타메이트

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Sodium Cocoyl Glutamate: effective concentration typically 5–20% of a cleanser's surfactant system, optimal pH 5.0–6.5, evidence Moderate. Spotting this near the top of a cleanser list is a good sign. Modest foam is a feature, not a defect.

How much Sodium Cocoyl Glutamate actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %typically 5–20% of a cleanser's surfactant system
Optimal pH5.0–6.5
EvidenceModerate
Onsetimmediate (feel)

What the % on the label really means

Spotting this near the top of a cleanser list is a good sign. Modest foam is a feature, not a defect.

⚠️ Ceiling: Amino acid surfactants foam less than sulfates by nature; pushing the level to chase lather undoes the gentleness that is the point.
