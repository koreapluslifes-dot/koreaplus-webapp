How much Galactomyces Ferment actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %varies (ferment filtrate — first-essences often listed 90–95%+)
Optimal pH4.0–5.5
EvidenceLimited
Onset2–4 weeks (radiance look)

What the % on the label really means

'95% galactomyces' means the essence is mostly ferment filtrate — read it as the product format, not a high-strength standardized active.

⚠️ Ceiling: A 90%+ listing is the essence base itself, not a concentrated active dose; more isn't proportionally brighter.
