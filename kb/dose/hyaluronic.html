Hyaluronic Acid: effective % & pH 히알루론산

⚡ Quick answer: Hyaluronic Acid: effective concentration 0.1–2% (as raw HA; finished serums often ~1–2%), optimal pH 5.0–7.0, evidence Strong. HA percentages look tiny because a little goes a long way; a '2% HA complex' is already high — apply to damp skin and seal to see the benefit.

How much Hyaluronic Acid actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %0.1–2% (as raw HA; finished serums often ~1–2%)
Optimal pH5.0–7.0
EvidenceStrong
OnsetImmediate (surface plumping)

What the % on the label really means

HA percentages look tiny because a little goes a long way; a '2% HA complex' is already high — apply to damp skin and seal to see the benefit.

⚠️ Ceiling: Past ~2% it gets sticky/tacky and can feel drying in low humidity — more isn't more hydration.
