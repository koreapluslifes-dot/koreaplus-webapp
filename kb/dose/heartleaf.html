How much Heartleaf (Houttuynia) actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %varies (extract; toners often listed up to ~70–90%+)

What the % on the label really means

A heartleaf/Houttuynia toner boasting a high % is mostly a diluted botanical water — a soothing base, not a dose you can compare to acids or niacinamide.

⚠️ Ceiling: High listed % is a base-feel cue; standardized heartleaf actives aren't established, so percentage ≠ potency.
