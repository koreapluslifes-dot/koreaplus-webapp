Mugwort (Artemisia): effective % & pH 쑥

⚡ Quick answer: Mugwort (Artemisia): effective concentration varies (extract; essences often listed very high, e.g. 90%+), optimal pH 5.0–6.5, evidence Limited. '100% mugwort' style claims mean the water phase is mugwort extract, not that it's a high-strength active — read it as a gentle soothing base.

How much Mugwort (Artemisia) actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

What the % on the label really means

'100% mugwort' style claims mean the water phase is mugwort extract, not that it's a high-strength active — read it as a gentle soothing base.

⚠️ Ceiling: A high mugwort % reflects a watery extract base, not concentrated potency; more doesn't mean stronger calming.
