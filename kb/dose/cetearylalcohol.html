⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Cetearyl Alcohol: effective concentration 1–5% in creams and emulsions, optimal pH tolerant across the usual range, evidence Strong. Not the drying alcohol you were warned about. If a cream breaks you out, look at the whole formula rather than blaming this line.

How much Cetearyl Alcohol actually works? Here's the dosage, pH and evidence — the formulator-level read behind the % on a Korean label.

Effective %1–5% in creams and emulsions
Optimal pHtolerant across the usual range
EvidenceStrong
Onsetimmediate (feel)

What the % on the label really means

Not the drying alcohol you were warned about. If a cream breaks you out, look at the whole formula rather than blaming this line.

⚠️ Ceiling: Heavy use makes a cream stiff and waxy, and fatty alcohols at high levels can feel occlusive on oily skin.
