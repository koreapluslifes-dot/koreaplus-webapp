The skin changes worth stopping everything for

Each of those warrants prompt medical contact, and the last is an emergency call. This page exists to prompt you to seek help — it cannot rule anything out, and it is not a diagnostic tool.

Moles and lesions that change

The features clinicians ask about are asymmetry, an irregular or blurred border, more than one colour within the same spot, increasing size, and — most important — change over time in something that used to be stable. Also worth showing someone: a lesion that itches, bleeds, crusts and reopens, or a sore that has not healed within a few weeks. A spot that looks different from all your others deserves a look. Skin cancers are generally very treatable when found early, which is the entire argument for going early. No app, photograph or serum substitutes for an examination.

Rapid, painful or systemic signs

Redness that is spreading outward and is warm and painful to touch, especially with fever or feeling unwell, needs same-day assessment — drawing a line around the edge with a pen and noting the time is genuinely useful information for the doctor. Blistering or peeling skin, rash involving the mouth, eyes or genitals, or a rash appearing shortly after starting a new medicine, all need urgent attention. Purple or dark spots that do not fade when pressed with a glass are an emergency, particularly alongside fever or drowsiness.

The allergic emergency

Swelling of the lips, tongue, mouth or throat, a hoarse or tight voice, wheezing, difficulty breathing or swallowing, sudden widespread hives with faintness or collapse: call emergency services immediately. Do not wait, do not drive yourself, and do not take an antihistamine and hope. Anyone carrying prescribed emergency medication for allergic reactions should use it exactly as they were instructed and still seek help. This can develop within minutes, and it is the one situation on this page where speed is the whole thing.

What to do while you wait

Photograph the area in good light with something for scale and note the date — a series of dated photographs is one of the most useful things you can bring. Write down any new medicines, supplements, foods or products from the preceding weeks. Stop all actives and stop anything you have been applying to the area. Do not attempt to treat a suspicious lesion with a cosmetic product or a wart remedy: obscuring or partially removing it delays diagnosis and makes assessment harder. Bring the list, keep the routine bland.

Do I really need to double cleanse?

At night, if you wear sunscreen or makeup — yes. Water-based cleansers cannot break down water-resistant sunscreen, so a single cleanse leaves a film behind. In the morning, one gentle cleanse or just water is plenty.
