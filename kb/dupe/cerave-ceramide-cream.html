Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream — a K-beauty alternative to CeraVe Moisturising Cream

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream is the Korean dupe for CeraVe Moisturising Cream — same hero ingredients (Ceramides in a fragrance-free, lipid-heavy base). Both are plain ceramide creams for dry, easily-irritated skin, with no fragrance and no glow story attached. Illiyoon is the tub Korean families buy for everyone in the house, and it sits in the same 저자극 (jeojagyeok, low-irritation) category. This is not a money-saver — the two cost about the same. The reason to swap is spread: the Illiyoon is a softer, more lotion-like texture if you find the reference heavy.
