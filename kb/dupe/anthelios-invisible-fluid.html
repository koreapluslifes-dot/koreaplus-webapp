Isntree Hyaluronic Acid Watery Sun Gel — a K-beauty alternative to La Roche-Posay Anthelios UVMune 400 Invisible Fluid

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Isntree Hyaluronic Acid Watery Sun Gel is the Korean dupe for La Roche-Posay Anthelios UVMune 400 Invisible Fluid — same hero ingredients (Light organic filters in a watery, humectant-rich base). Both are fluid facial sunscreens meant to layer under makeup without pilling, with humectants rather than oils carrying the texture. The filter lists differ — the reference's headline is a filter developed for long-wave UVA that Korean formulas do not use — so treat this as a texture-and-routine match rather than a protection-for-protection swap. Both carry PA++++ labelling, and how much you actually apply matters more than which bottle you buy. Save ~30–50%.
