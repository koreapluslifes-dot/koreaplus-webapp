I'm From Honey Mask — a K-beauty alternative to Farmacy Honey Potion Renewing Antioxidant Hydration Mask

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: I'm From Honey Mask is the Korean dupe for Farmacy Honey Potion Renewing Antioxidant Hydration Mask — same hero ingredients (Honey and propolis making up the bulk of the formula). Both are sticky wash-off masks with honey high in the ingredient list, used for a fifteen-minute comfort session rather than as a treatment step. I'm From's is built on Korean honey with propolis alongside; the reference warms on application, which the Korean one does not. Sticky masks feel far more dramatic than they are — the payoff is short-term surface hydration and a softer look, and that is fine to enjoy on its own terms. Save ~30–50%.
