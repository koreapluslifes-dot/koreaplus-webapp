Pyunkang Yul Low pH Cleansing Gel — a K-beauty alternative to Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Pyunkang Yul Low pH Cleansing Gel is the Korean dupe for Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser — same hero ingredients (Low-pH, fragrance-free, minimal-surfactant washing). Both are deliberately boring: no fragrance, no scrub particles, low lather, for skin that reacts to most things. Pyunkang Yul's house style is a very short list built around 황련 (hwangnyeon, coptis) extract in place of plain water. Prices land in the same range, so choose on rinse-feel — the Korean gel foams slightly, which some sensitive skin prefers and some does not.
