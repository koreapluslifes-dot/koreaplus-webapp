Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Cream — a K-beauty alternative to Kiehl's Centella Sensitive Facial Cream

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Cream is the Korean dupe for Kiehl's Centella Sensitive Facial Cream — same hero ingredients (Centella asiatica (병풀) as the lead soothing extract). Both are minimal, fragrance-free centella creams aimed at the look of redness on skin that has been over-worked. Dr.G's is the Olive Young default for that job and comes in a considerably bigger tub. Centella has more supporting evidence behind it than most botanical actives, though most of it concerns comfort and the appearance of irritation rather than anything structural — set expectations there. Save ~50–70%.
