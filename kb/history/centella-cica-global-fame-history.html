How Centella (Cica) Became K-Beauty's Soothing Superstar

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A history of centella asiatica (cica) in skincare — from a traditional medicinal herb to one of K-beauty's most famous soothing, barrier-repair ingredients.

Centella asiatica — widely marketed as "cica" — traveled from traditional herbal medicine to the front line of global K-beauty as a celebrated soothing and barrier-supporting ingredient. Its rise mirrors the industry's broader pivot toward gentle, calming skincare.

A traditional medicinal plant

Centella asiatica is a herb with a long history of use in various traditional medicine systems across Asia. Its skin-soothing reputation — sometimes linked in popular lore to wound-care and recovery — made it a natural candidate for modern skincare framed around calming and repair.

The 'cica' branding wave

Korean brands packaged centella into "cica" creams, balms, and serums marketed for sensitive, irritated, and compromised skin. The short, memorable "cica" name (derived from centella) became a category keyword, and centella-derived compounds were highlighted on labels as soothing actives.

Global mainstream adoption

Riding the barrier-care and gentle-skincare movement, centella/cica became one of the most recognized K-beauty ingredients worldwide and was adopted into many non-Korean brands' formulas as well. It exemplifies how K-beauty repackaged a traditional botanical into a globally fluent skincare concept.

Key facts
  • Centella asiatica is the botanical behind the "cica" name.
  • It has a long history in traditional Asian herbal medicine.
  • Korean brands popularized cica products for soothing and barrier support.
  • Centella/cica was adopted globally, including by non-Korean brands.
