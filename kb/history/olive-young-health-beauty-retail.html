Olive Young and the H&B Store Era

Much of K-beauty's late-2010s vitality flowed through Olive Young, Korea's leading health-and-beauty store chain.

A new retail format

Olive Young became Korea's dominant health-and-beauty (H&B) store chain, offering a wide, self-service assortment of skincare, makeup and personal-care products across many price points. This format, browsable, trend-driven and stocked with both established and emerging brands, became central to how Korean consumers discover and buy beauty products.

A launchpad for brands

For indie and emerging K-beauty labels, getting onto Olive Young shelves became a major path to visibility and credibility. The chain's curation and bestseller rankings could quickly turn a product into a hit, making it an important engine of the indie-brand boom and a barometer of Korean beauty trends.

Domestic and global relevance

Olive Young also grew into a destination for international visitors and, increasingly, online global shoppers seeking authentic K-beauty. While precise store counts and sales figures change over time and are best stated generally, the durable point is that Olive Young reshaped Korean beauty retail and helped power the late-2010s indie surge.

Key facts
  • Olive Young is Korea's leading health-and-beauty store chain
  • Its self-service format aids beauty discovery across price points
  • Shelf placement became a major launchpad for indie brands
  • It is also a destination for tourists and global online shoppers
