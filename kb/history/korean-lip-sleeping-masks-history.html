The Rise of Korean Sleeping Masks and Lip Masks

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A history of Korean sleeping masks and overnight lip masks — leave-on overnight treatments that became a signature K-beauty format and a global viral hit.

Overnight "sleeping masks" and overnight lip masks became a distinctive K-beauty format: leave-on treatments designed to work while you sleep. They reframed the mask from a rinse-off ritual into a passive overnight step, and several became global viral bestsellers.

The sleeping-mask concept

A sleeping mask (or sleeping pack) is a leave-on overnight treatment — typically a gel or cream applied as a final step to seal in hydration and actives while you sleep. The format fit K-beauty's layering philosophy while requiring no extra effort, making intensive overnight care feel effortless.

Overnight lip masks

The same overnight logic was applied to lips: rich, leave-on lip masks marketed to repair and hydrate chapped lips overnight. A Korean overnight lip-mask product in particular became a widely-shared global bestseller, popularizing the overnight-lip-care subcategory and inspiring many imitators.

A globally copied format

Both sleeping masks and overnight lip masks were widely adopted by international brands, becoming standard offerings far beyond K-beauty. The category demonstrated K-beauty's knack for popularizing convenient, demonstrable formats that translate easily into mainstream global routines.

Key facts
  • Sleeping masks are leave-on overnight gel/cream treatments.
  • They seal in hydration and actives during sleep with no rinse-off.
  • A Korean overnight lip mask became a viral global bestseller.
  • International brands widely adopted both formats.
