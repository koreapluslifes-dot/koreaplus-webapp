Stylenanda and 3CE: From Online Fashion to L'Oréal

⚡ Quick answer: The history of Stylenanda and its 3CE cosmetics line — a Korean online fashion brand that built a trendsetting makeup label later acquired by L'Oréal.

Stylenanda began as a Korean online fashion retailer and grew into a lifestyle brand whose 3CE (3 Concept Eyes) cosmetics line became a trendsetting force in Korean color makeup. Its trajectory — from e-commerce fashion to a major acquisition — illustrates the fashion-beauty crossover of the K-beauty boom.

From fashion e-commerce to beauty

Stylenanda started in online fashion in the mid-2000s and cultivated a distinctive, of-the-moment aesthetic that resonated with young Korean and Asian shoppers. Building on that brand identity, it launched its 3CE color-cosmetics line, extending its fashion-forward image into makeup with bold lip and eye products.

3CE's trendsetting role

3CE became known for on-trend shades, packaging, and a strong visual identity, helping shape popular Korean makeup looks during the 2010s. The brand's fashion DNA gave it a styling-led approach to cosmetics that differentiated it from traditional beauty houses and made it especially popular with younger consumers across Asia.

The L'Oréal acquisition

Stylenanda's success drew global attention, and French cosmetics giant L'Oréal announced the acquisition of its parent company, Nanda Co., Ltd., in 2018 — a notable example of a multinational buying into K-beauty's fashion-driven appeal. The deal underscored how Korean indie and fashion-led brands had become attractive global assets.

Key facts
  • Stylenanda began as a Korean online fashion retailer in the mid-2000s.
  • Its cosmetics line is named 3CE (3 Concept Eyes).
  • 3CE was known for fashion-forward, trendsetting color makeup.
  • L'Oréal announced the acquisition of Stylenanda's parent, Nanda Co., in 2018.
