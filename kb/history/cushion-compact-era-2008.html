The Cushion Compact Revolution

The cushion compact is one of K-beauty's most influential and widely copied inventions, popularized by Amorepacific around 2008.

A new makeup format

Around 2008, Amorepacific introduced the cushion compact, first associated with its IOPE brand. The product embeds liquid foundation or base makeup, typically with sun protection and skincare benefits, in a specially engineered sponge inside a compact, applied with a puff. This made glowing, buildable coverage portable and easy to reapply.

Why it succeeded

The cushion fit K-beauty's preferences well: multifunctional, a skin-like finish, sun protection and convenience in one step. It became extraordinarily popular among Korean consumers and was widely reported as a top-selling category. Its success drove significant sales for Amorepacific and inspired countless competitors, including global brands, to launch their own cushion products.

A genuine Korean innovation

Unlike BB cream, the modern cushion compact is broadly credited as a Korean-originated innovation, with Amorepacific recognized as the pioneer and the holder of related patents. The development is often described as requiring extensive research into the sponge technology. The 2008 launch is well documented, and the cushion remains a defining K-beauty contribution to global makeup.

Key facts
  • Amorepacific introduced the cushion compact around 2008
  • It was first associated with the IOPE brand
  • It embeds base makeup in an engineered sponge for one-step use
  • The cushion is credited as a Korean-originated innovation
