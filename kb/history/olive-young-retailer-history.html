How Olive Young Became Korea's Beauty Powerhouse

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: A history of Olive Young, the CJ-affiliated Korean health-and-beauty store chain that became the country's dominant beauty retailer and a key launchpad for indie brands.

Olive Young grew into Korea's dominant health-and-beauty retail chain, functioning as both a discovery engine for new brands and a barometer of domestic beauty trends. For many shoppers and tourists, an Olive Young store is the physical face of the K-beauty market.

The H&B store model

Olive Young operates in the "health and beauty" drugstore-style format, combining skincare, makeup, personal care, and wellness products under one roof. This model, with curated assortments and frequent newness, suited Korea's fast-moving beauty culture and made the chain a one-stop destination for trying products in person.

A launchpad for indie brands

Olive Young became an important gateway for emerging and indie Korean brands to reach mass retail shelves and gain credibility. Placement and visibility in its stores could meaningfully accelerate a young brand's growth, making the retailer a central node in the domestic beauty ecosystem.

Tourism and online expansion

The chain is affiliated with the CJ Group conglomerate and expanded its reach through flagship stores popular with international tourists and through online and global shipping channels. As inbound tourism and global K-beauty interest grew, Olive Young increasingly served overseas shoppers alongside its domestic base.

Key facts
  • Olive Young is Korea's leading health-and-beauty retail chain.
  • It is affiliated with the CJ Group conglomerate.
  • The chain is a major launchpad for indie and emerging Korean brands.
  • Its flagship stores are popular destinations for beauty tourists.
