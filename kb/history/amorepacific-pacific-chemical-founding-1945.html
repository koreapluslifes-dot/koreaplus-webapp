Amorepacific and the Founding of Pacific Chemical

⚡ Quick answer: Amorepacific traces its corporate founding to 1945, when Suh Sung-whan formalized a family camellia-oil trade into a cosmetics company.

Amorepacific, today one of Korea's largest cosmetics groups, traces its corporate founding to 1945, growing out of a family botanical-oil business.

Origins of the company

Amorepacific traces its corporate founding to 1945, when Suh Sung-whan formalized a family trade rooted in camellia oil into a cosmetics enterprise, the business often referred to in English as Pacific Chemical. The operation grew from an earlier family camellia-oil trade, and its early identity was tied to natural botanical ingredients.

Building a real industry

In the postwar decades the company invested in research, and it is widely credited with helping pioneer a formal cosmetics industry in Korea, including early dedicated research and the study of overseas cosmetic science. Over time the firm expanded from oils and basics into a full skincare and makeup portfolio, eventually adopting the Amorepacific name for the group.

Why this founding matters

Amorepacific became a structural backbone of K-beauty, later associated with milestones like the cushion compact and brands such as Laneige, Sulwhasoo, Innisfree and Etude. Its 1945 founding date is well documented; finer biographical details about the founder are best stated generally to avoid inventing specifics. The company is, durably, a major Korean cosmetics group with mid-20th-century roots.

Key facts
  • Amorepacific traces its corporate founding to 1945
  • It grew from a family camellia-oil trade
  • The company helped pioneer a formal Korean cosmetics industry
  • It later owned brands like Laneige, Sulwhasoo and Innisfree
