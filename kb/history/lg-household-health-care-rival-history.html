LG Household & Health Care and Korea's Beauty Duopoly

Korean cosmetics did not grow on one company alone; LG's consumer-goods arm became Amorepacific's major domestic competitor.

A second major player

LG Household & Health Care (now known as LG H&H), part of the larger LG conglomerate, became one of Korea's leading cosmetics and consumer-goods companies and the main domestic rival to Amorepacific. Together these two firms are often described as anchoring the Korean cosmetics market, owning numerous skincare and makeup brands between them.

Competition drove innovation

Rivalry between large, well-resourced Korean cosmetics companies helped fuel rapid product development, marketing investment and category creation. As both groups built broad brand portfolios spanning premium and mass tiers, Korean consumers gained access to a wide, fast-evolving range of products, which in turn raised expectations and kept the industry innovating.

Why this matters historically

The presence of more than one strong domestic champion is part of why Korea developed such a deep and competitive beauty industry rather than a single dominant brand. While precise market-share numbers shift year to year and are best avoided, the durable, structural fact is that a competitive landscape, led by Amorepacific and LG Household & Health Care, underpinned K-beauty's strength.

Key facts
  • LG Household & Health Care is part of the LG conglomerate
  • It became Amorepacific's main domestic cosmetics rival
  • The two firms anchor much of Korea's beauty market
  • Competition accelerated product innovation and category creation
