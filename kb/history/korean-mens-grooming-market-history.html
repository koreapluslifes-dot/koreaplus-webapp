How Korea Became a Global Leader in Men's Grooming

South Korea is frequently cited as one of the world's most developed markets for men's grooming and skincare. The country's comparatively high male engagement with cosmetics reflects shifting norms of masculinity, strong appearance culture, and the influence of pop media.

A standout market for male skincare

Korea has long been highlighted in industry coverage as a leader in per-capita male spending on grooming and skincare, with skincare and even color products such as BB creams and tinted bases finding a male audience. This stands out against many markets where male skincare adoption developed more slowly.

Cultural drivers

Several cultural factors are commonly cited: an appearance-conscious society, the visibility of well-groomed male celebrities and idols in K-pop and dramas, and broader shifts in how masculinity and self-care are understood. These influences are widely credited with helping normalize routine male skincare across generations.

Products and global influence

The market spans cleansers, moisturizers, sun care, and grooming basics, with dedicated men's lines from major Korean houses. As K-pop and Korean media gained global audiences, Korean male-grooming norms also became a reference point internationally, influencing perceptions of men's skincare abroad. (Note: cultural generalizations should be read as broad tendencies, not universal traits.)

Key facts
  • Korea is widely cited as a leading market for men's grooming and skincare.
  • Male engagement spans skincare and some tinted/base products.
  • K-pop and Korean media helped normalize male skincare.
  • Korean male-grooming norms became a global reference point.
