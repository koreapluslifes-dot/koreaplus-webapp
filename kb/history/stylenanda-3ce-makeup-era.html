Stylenanda, 3CE and K-Beauty Color Cosmetics

K-beauty is often associated with skincare, but brands like Stylenanda and 3CE highlighted its trend-led color-cosmetics side.

Fashion-driven beauty

Stylenanda began as a Korean fashion label and built a strong following, with its 3CE (3 Concept Eyes) line bringing trend-forward color cosmetics to a young, style-conscious audience. The brand's bold aesthetic and lifestyle branding showed that K-beauty was not only about skincare but also about fashionable makeup and identity.

Global recognition

Stylenanda and 3CE gained popularity well beyond Korea, including among international shoppers and tourists, and the brand drew significant industry attention. Its rise illustrated how Korean beauty success could come from blending fashion, retail experience and makeup, complementing the skincare-led image K-beauty was known for abroad.

Part of a bigger picture

The brand is frequently cited as evidence that global beauty companies took K-beauty seriously, with reports of major-company acquisition interest in Korean beauty assets during this era. While exact deal terms are best left unstated here, the durable point is that Stylenanda and 3CE exemplified the trend-driven, fashion-linked makeup side of the late-2010s K-beauty boom.

Key facts
  • Stylenanda started as a Korean fashion label
  • Its 3CE line brought trend-forward color cosmetics to young consumers
  • It gained strong popularity beyond Korea
  • It showed K-beauty's fashion-driven, makeup side
