How BHA (Salicylic) works BHA

⚡ Quick answer: How does BHA (Salicylic) work? BHA (salicylic acid) is oil-soluble, so it penetrates sebum-filled pores and exfoliates inside them, loosening debris to decongest blackheads and support clearer, less-congested skin; it is also mildly soothing. Typically 0.5–2% in cosmetics.
