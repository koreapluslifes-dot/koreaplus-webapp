How Panthenol (Pro-B5) works 판테놀

⚡ Quick answer: How does Panthenol (Pro-B5) work? Panthenol (pro-vitamin B5) converts in skin toward pantothenic acid and acts as a humectant that draws and holds water. It has a well-documented soothing, barrier-supporting profile and is broadly tolerated, making it useful in post-active and barrier-repair routine
