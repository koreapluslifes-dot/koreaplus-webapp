Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) is an antioxidant that neutralizes free radicals and is a cofactor for collagen-synthesizing enzymes; it also interferes with the tyrosinase step of pigment formation to help even tone. Pure L-ascorbic acid is effective around 10–20% at pH below 3.5; gentler derivatives suit sensitive skin but are generally less potent. Best in the AM under sunscreen.
