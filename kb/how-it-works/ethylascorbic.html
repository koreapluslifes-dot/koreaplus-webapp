3-O-Ethyl ascorbic acid is ascorbic acid with an ethyl group attached at a position that normally drives oxidation, which is why it stays stable in water-based formulas where pure vitamin C would not. Skin enzymes are thought to remove that group to release ascorbic acid, which then acts on the pigment-forming pathway and as an antioxidant. How completely that conversion happens is the open question, and it is the reason derivatives are usually slower than the real thing.
