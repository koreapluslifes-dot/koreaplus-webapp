Squalane is the stable, hydrogenated form of squalene, a lipid the skin naturally makes. As a skin-mimicking emollient it fills gaps between surface cells, softening skin and helping seal in moisture by slowing water loss, all with a lightweight, non-greasy, non-comedogenic feel. Usually plant-derived (e.g. olive, sugarcane).
