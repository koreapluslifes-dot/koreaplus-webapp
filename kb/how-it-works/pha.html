PHAs (e.g. gluconolactone) are larger polyhydroxy acids that exfoliate the surface like AHAs but penetrate more slowly, making them gentler and well-suited to sensitive skin new to acids; their multiple hydroxyl groups also bind water for added hydration and they carry mild antioxidant activity.
