⚡ Quick answer: How does Tranexamic Acid work? Tranexamic acid is a lysine derivative that helps interrupt the signaling between skin and pigment-producing cells (in part via the plasmin pathway), making it a favorite for the look of stubborn pigmentation and melasma, frequently paired with niacinamide. Tranexamic acid is a lysine derivative that helps interrupt the signaling between skin and pigment-producing cells (in part via the plasmin pathway), making it a favorite for the look of stubborn pigmentation and melasma, frequently paired with niacinamide.
