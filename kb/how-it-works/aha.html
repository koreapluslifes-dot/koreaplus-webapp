AHAs (glycolic, lactic) are water-soluble acids that weaken the bonds between surface dead skin cells, loosening and shedding them to reveal smoother, brighter skin and to even tone; lactic also adds humectant hydration. Glycolic (smallest) penetrates fastest and is stronger; lactic is gentler. OTC commonly 5–10%; SPF is essential afterward.
