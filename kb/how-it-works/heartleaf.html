Heartleaf (Houttuynia cordata) extract contains flavonoids (e.g. quercitrin) and other polyphenols linked to antioxidant and soothing activity in lab settings. In cosmetics it is used to calm the appearance of redness and support oily, breakout-prone skin. There is no standardized topical concentration, so cosmetic claims are best hedged.
