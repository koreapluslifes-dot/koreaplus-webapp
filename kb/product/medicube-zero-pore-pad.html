Medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Zero Pore Pad 2.0 is Medicube's toner pad, built around AHA (Glycolic/Lactic) and BHA (Salicylic) and PHA (Gluconolactone). Oily and combination skin with a genuinely congested T-zone that tolerates acids well.

Medicube's best-known pad and one of the biggest sellers in the Korean toner-pad category. It combines the three acid families in a soaked pad with a textured side, aimed at the visible pores and rough surface that come with oily skin. The 2.0 designation marks a reformulation of the original. Medicube's positioning is derma-tech and clinic-adjacent, which is branding rather than a regulatory category.

🏷️ Medicube ⬜ Toner pad

What it is built around

🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic)🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone)

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin with a genuinely congested T-zone that tolerates acids well. It is a strong pad by the standards of the format, so it suits people who have already used a leave-on BHA without trouble. Dry, sensitive and reactive skin should not start here — a single-acid liquid at a low frequency is a far more controllable place to begin.

🕳️ Pores & texture⚫ Blackheads & sebaceous filaments🛢️ Oil control🪨 Rough & uneven texture

How to use it

Evening, after cleansing, sweeping one pad over the oilier areas rather than the whole face. Start twice a week and increase only if your skin remains comfortable. Follow with a hydrating toner and moisturiser. Wear sunscreen daily. Do not use it on the same night as a retinoid or another leave-on acid.

⚠️ Worth knowing: "Zero pore" is not achievable. Pore size is largely genetic and can be influenced in appearance, not eliminated, and any product promising otherwise is overselling. Combining three acid types in an effortless pad is the fastest route to over-exfoliation in the whole category — the tightness, shine and stinging that follows takes weeks to settle. Frequency discipline matters more than the formula here.
