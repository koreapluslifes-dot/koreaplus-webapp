Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics SPF50+ PA++++

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics SPF50+ PA++++ is Beauty of Joseon's sunscreen, built around Rice Extract. Dry, normal and combination skin, and anyone whose objection to sunscreen has always been how it feels.

The sunscreen that introduced a generation to Korean SPF. An organic-filter formula — 유기자차 (yugi-jacha), the Korean term for chemical filters — with a serum-like slip, a dewy finish and no white cast on any tone. Rice extract and a probiotic ferment sit in the background; the reason to buy it is the texture, which is elegant enough that people actually reapply it. Rated SPF50+ PA++++ on the Korean system.

🏷️ Beauty of Joseon ☀️ Sunscreen 💸 $14-20

What it is built around

🍚 Rice Extract

Who it suits

Dry, normal and combination skin, and anyone whose objection to sunscreen has always been how it feels. It doubles as a glowy makeup base. Oily skin often finds the dewiness tips into shine by midday, so a matte or gel formula suits better. The organic filters can sting sensitive eyes, so very reactive people should patch test first.

☀️ Sun damage & photoageing⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)

How to use it

Last step of the morning routine, before makeup, roughly two fingers' length for face and neck — most people use a third of that and get a fraction of the rated protection. Give it a quarter of an hour before sun exposure. Reapply every couple of hours outdoors, and after swimming or heavy sweating.

⚠️ Worth knowing: It stings some people's eyes, particularly in heat, and that is by far the most common complaint. It is not water resistant in the way a beach sunscreen is. Packaging and formula have shifted across regions and years, so older reviews may describe a different tube. And the rice-and-probiotic angle is garnish; the filters do the work.
