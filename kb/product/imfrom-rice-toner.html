I'm From Rice Toner 라이스 토너

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Rice Toner is I'm From's toner / skin, built around Rice Extract and Niacinamide (B3). Dry, dull or textured skin that wants a comfortable hydration step with some glow to it.

A milky, slightly viscous toner built on rice extract with niacinamide alongside. I'm From's approach is single-origin ingredients at a high proportion, with the growing region named on the box, and this is the product that carried the brand abroad. It reads more like a thin essence than a toner and leaves skin looking soft and faintly luminous straight after application.

🏷️ I'm From 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $20-30

What it is built around

🍚 Rice Extract🧫 Niacinamide (B3)

Who it suits

Dry, dull or textured skin that wants a comfortable hydration step with some glow to it. Mature skin does well with it. Oily skin sometimes finds it too much for a first layer in summer. If your main concern is stubborn pigmentation, the niacinamide here is a supporting player rather than the main event — a dedicated serum will do more.

🌟 Dullness & glow🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🥵 Dryness & dehydration🪨 Rough & uneven texture

How to use it

After cleansing, AM and PM. Pour into the palm and press in; cotton pads waste it. One layer is usually enough given the texture, two on very dry days. Follow with a serum and cream. It pairs happily with vitamin C in the morning and with retinol at night.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The glow is hydration plus light reflection, and it fades as the layer dries down — no rice extract is remodelling your skin tone overnight. It carries a faint sweet scent that some people notice. Oily and congestion-prone skin should watch how the slightly heavier texture behaves under sunscreen before committing to it daily.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.
