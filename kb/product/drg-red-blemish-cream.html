Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Cream 레드 블레미쉬 클리어 수딩 크림

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Red Blemish Clear Soothing Cream is Dr.G's moisturizer / cream, built around Centella (Cica) and Madecassoside. Combination and oily skin with visible redness, breakout-prone skin that still needs a moisturiser, and barriers complaining after a stretch of acids.

A perennial Olive Young top-seller and, in Korea, the default answer to 'my face is red and I do not know why'. It is a light, cooling cica cream — centella plus its purified fractions — with a gel-like slip that suits skin running oily as well as reactive. Dr.G positions itself as dermatologist-adjacent, and the formula inside is more restrained than the packaging colour would suggest.

🏷️ Dr.G 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $22-32

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Combination and oily skin with visible redness, breakout-prone skin that still needs a moisturiser, and barriers complaining after a stretch of acids. Very dry skin will want more substance. Despite the name it is not a spot treatment and will not clear an active breakout — it makes irritated skin look and feel calmer while you address the cause elsewhere.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🔴 Acne & breakouts🧱 Damaged moisture barrier

How to use it

Morning and night as the last step, or under sunscreen. A thin layer is enough; it feels lighter than most cica creams and does not need pressing in. It layers cleanly under makeup. On a flare day, apply it after a plain hydrating toner and pause any exfoliating acids for a few days.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The 'blemish' framing sells it as acne care, which it is not — it is a soothing moisturiser, and treating real acne is a clinician's job with clinician's tools. The formula is lightly scented. And the cooling sensation on application comes from the texture and volatile components, not from anything measurable happening to the redness itself.
