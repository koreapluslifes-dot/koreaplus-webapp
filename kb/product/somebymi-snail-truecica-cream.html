Some By Mi Snail Truecica Miracle Repair Cream

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Snail Truecica Miracle Repair Cream is Some By Mi's moisturizer / cream, built around Snail Mucin and Centella (Cica) and Madecassoside. Combination and breakout-prone skin dealing with post-blemish marks and patchy redness, and dry skin in milder weather.

Some By Mi's attempt to put its two best-selling stories in one jar: black snail mucin for hydration and slip, and the house Truecica centella complex for the calming half. The texture is a medium, faintly bouncy cream that suits combination skin better than the richness implies. It shares shelf space with the brand's acid-led 30 Days line and functions as the gentle counterweight to it.

🏷️ Some By Mi 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $18-28

What it is built around

🐌 Snail Mucin🌿 Centella (Cica)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Combination and breakout-prone skin dealing with post-blemish marks and patchy redness, and dry skin in milder weather. It pairs sensibly with an exfoliating routine as the calming step. Very oily skin may find it a little much in summer. Anyone with a mollusc sensitivity should avoid snail formulas entirely, and it is not vegan or vegetarian.

🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🥵 Dryness & dehydration🪨 Rough & uneven texture

How to use it

Night, as the last step, over a serum. A fingertip for the face, pressed rather than rubbed. Fine in the morning under sunscreen for drier skin. If you also use the brand's AHA-BHA-PHA products, keep them to alternate nights and use this on the nights between rather than stacking everything together.

⚠️ Worth knowing: 'Miracle repair' is exactly the sort of naming this library distrusts. Snail mucin and centella can make marks and redness look better over weeks; they repair nothing, and true scarring is a clinic conversation. It is lightly fragranced, and the black-snail framing is a sourcing detail rather than a demonstrated upgrade over ordinary snail filtrate.

Is snail mucin safe for skin?

Snail mucin (snail secretion filtrate) is widely used in Korean skincare and generally well tolerated, including for sensitive skin. It's valued as a hydrating, comforting ingredient. As with any product, patch-test first, and those with known allergies to mollusks or related ingredients should be cautious.
