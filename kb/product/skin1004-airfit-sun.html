SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus SPF50+ PA++++

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Madagascar Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus SPF50+ PA++++ is SKIN1004's sunscreen, built around Centella (Cica). Combination and oily skin, and sensitive skin that gets on with organic filters and wants centella in the same layer.

An organic-filter sunscreen carrying SKIN1004's Madagascar-sourced centella in a light fluid-cream that dries down closer to fresh than dewy. The brand's entire positioning is single-source centella across every format, and this is the sun step of that idea. It absorbs quickly, leaves no cast, and behaves under makeup — the three things people actually judge a daily sunscreen on once the label is read.

🏷️ SKIN1004 ☀️ Sunscreen 💸 $18-28

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Combination and oily skin, and sensitive skin that gets on with organic filters and wants centella in the same layer. It is a reasonable option for skin that flushes in heat. Very dry skin will want something more emollient underneath it. As with any cica sunscreen, the centella is a supporting act — the filters are the part that protects you.

☀️ Sun damage & photoageing🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

Last morning step, two fingers' length for face and neck, spread evenly and left to set for a quarter of an hour. Under makeup it works best if you stop touching it once it is on. Reapply every couple of hours outdoors, using a stick or cushion if you are wearing base makeup.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The line carries several similarly named sun products and it is easy to order the wrong finish; check the exact name before buying. Centella in a sunscreen does not make it suitable for skin genuinely reacting to UV filters — if a formula stings, the filters are the likely cause, and no botanical in the bottle will fix that.
