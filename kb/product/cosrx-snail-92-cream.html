COSRX Advanced Snail 92 All In One Cream 어드밴스드 스네일 92 올인원 크림

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Advanced Snail 92 All In One Cream is COSRX's moisturizer / cream, built around Snail Mucin and Hyaluronic Acid. Best for combination, normal and mildly dry skin that wants hydration without weight, and for anyone chasing the plumped, dewy finish snail mucin is known for.

The cream half of COSRX's snail range: a high-percentage snail secretion filtrate base in a bouncy, faintly tacky gel-cream rather than a rich balm. It is the same idea as the famous 96 essence with enough emulsion behind it to seal a routine, so for many people it works as the only moisturising step. Nothing exotic sits alongside it — mostly humectants and a light slip. Long-running, widely stocked and cheap to re-buy.

🏷️ COSRX 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $15-25

What it is built around

🐌 Snail Mucin💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Who it suits

Best for combination, normal and mildly dry skin that wants hydration without weight, and for anyone chasing the plumped, dewy finish snail mucin is known for. Oily skin usually gets on with it in summer. Very dry skin in a cold climate will want something richer layered on top. Skip it if you react to molluscs, and skip it if animal-derived ingredients are a problem for you.

🥵 Dryness & dehydration🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)🪨 Rough & uneven texture

How to use it

Last step of the evening routine, or under sunscreen in the morning. A pea-sized amount on damp skin, pressed rather than rubbed — it grabs less and spreads further that way. The tackiness fades in a couple of minutes. If it stays sticky, you either used too much or applied it to skin that had already dried.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The tack is real and some people never make peace with it, particularly under makeup. Snail mucin is a cosmetic hydrator, not treatment for scarring — it can make post-blemish marks look softer, it will not level a pitted scar. And the number on the label refers to the filtrate content the brand declares, not to how well the finished cream performs.

Is snail mucin safe for skin?

Snail mucin (snail secretion filtrate) is widely used in Korean skincare and generally well tolerated, including for sensitive skin. It's valued as a hydrating, comforting ingredient. As with any product, patch-test first, and those with known allergies to mollusks or related ingredients should be cautious.

Do you really need all 10 steps of Korean skincare?
