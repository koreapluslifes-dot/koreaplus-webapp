Anua Azelaic Acid 10 Hyaluron Redness Soothing Serum

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Azelaic Acid 10 Hyaluron Redness Soothing Serum is Anua's serum, built around Azelaic Acid and Hyaluronic Acid. Sensitive, combination and blemish-prone skin dealing with the look of persistent redness and lingering marks.

One of the few Korean cosmetic serums built openly around azelaic acid, buffered with hyaluronic acid to take the edge off. Azelaic acid is unusual in this category: it has genuine clinical history at prescription strengths for redness and blemish-prone skin, and it does not increase sun sensitivity the way an AHA does. At the cosmetic level used here it is a gentler, slower version of that. The texture is a light lotion rather than a watery drop.

🏷️ Anua 🧪 Serum

What it is built around

🌾 Azelaic Acid💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Who it suits

Sensitive, combination and blemish-prone skin dealing with the look of persistent redness and lingering marks. It is a reasonable option for people who cannot tolerate acids or retinoids. Anyone with diagnosed rosacea should be having this conversation with a clinician, who can prescribe the higher strengths that the evidence actually rests on. Very dry skin may need extra moisturiser over the top.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🔴 Acne & breakouts🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

Evening to start, after toner, before moisturiser, every other night for the first fortnight. A pea-sized amount for the whole face. It can move to twice daily once tolerated. It does not conflict with sunscreen or niacinamide, and it is one of the few actives that plays reasonably well with a retinoid — though not on the same night at first.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Ten per cent sits well below the prescription strengths the clinical evidence is built on, so temper expectations accordingly. Azelaic acid has a real tendency to pill under sunscreen or makeup, and a mild tingling in the first weeks is common. It is also slow. Persistent facial redness has several possible causes, some of which need diagnosis rather than a serum.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is hyaluronic acid and how do you use it?

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant that draws water into the skin for a plumper, hydrated look. In Korean routines it's best applied to damp skin and sealed with a moisturizer on top, so the moisture it attracts stays locked in rather than evaporating.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
