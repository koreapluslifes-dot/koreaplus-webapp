Abib Gummy Sheet Mask Heartleaf Sticker 검 시트 마스크 어성초 스티커

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Gummy Sheet Mask Heartleaf Sticker is Abib's sheet mask, built around Heartleaf (Houttuynia) and Panthenol (Pro-B5). Reactive or flushed skin, and anyone who has given up on masks because they will not stay put — this one holds well enough to move around in.

Abib's sheet masks use a thicker, more elastic fabric than the usual thin cellulose, which is where the 'gummy' and 'sticker' naming comes from — it grips the face properly instead of sliding off halfway through. The heartleaf version carries a calming, watery essence. The sheet quality is the genuine differentiator; the essence is a competent, unremarkable soother.

🏷️ Abib 🎭 Sheet mask 💸 $3-5 per sheet

What it is built around

🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)

Who it suits

Reactive or flushed skin, and anyone who has given up on masks because they will not stay put — this one holds well enough to move around in. Oily and combination skin do well with it. Very dry skin may prefer a richer variant from the same range. Not a fix for anything, as with all sheet masks.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🔴 Acne & breakouts

How to use it

Cleansed skin, after toner, fifteen to twenty minutes. The thicker sheet stays wet longer, but do not leave it on until it dries. Press in the residue, then moisturise. Once or twice a week. The range includes rice and madecassoside versions if heartleaf is not the concern you are treating.

⚠️ Worth knowing: You are paying a premium over cheap cellulose masks for the sheet, not for the formula — worth knowing before buying a box. The essence, like most calming mask essences, works on the appearance of redness for a few hours. Single-use packaging waste is real, and Abib's individual wrappers are heavier than most.

What is heartleaf in Korean skincare?

Heartleaf, or Houttuynia cordata (어성초 in Korean), is a trending calming extract used in K-beauty to soothe and help balance oily, blemish-prone skin. It's found in lightweight toners and essences and pairs well with centella, niacinamide or BHA for troubled skin.
