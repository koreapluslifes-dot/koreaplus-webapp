Aestura Atobarrier 365 Lotion 아토베리어 365 로션

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Atobarrier 365 Lotion is Aestura's emulsion / lotion, built around Ceramides. Combination and normal skin that runs dry in patches, and dry skin during humid months.

The lighter sibling of the Atobarrier 365 cream — the same ceramide-led idea in an emulsion that pours rather than scoops. In Korean routine language this sits where a Western routine would use a lotion: after essence, before anything occlusive. It is meant for people who want the line's cushioned finish without the weight, and for warmer months when the cream feels like too much on the face.

🏷️ Aestura 🥛 Emulsion / Lotion

What it is built around

🧱 Ceramides

Who it suits

Combination and normal skin that runs dry in patches, and dry skin during humid months. It also suits anyone building a deliberately short routine, since it can be the only moisturising step. Very dry or visibly flaking skin will get more out of the cream. Reactive skin generally tolerates it well, but patch-test on the jaw for a few days like anything else.

🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

After toner or essence, morning and evening, on damp skin. Two or three pumps for the face and neck. In winter you can use it as the hydrating layer and put the cream on top; in summer it is usually enough on its own. Sunscreen goes over it in the morning, once it has settled.

⚠️ Worth knowing: People buy the lotion expecting cream-level richness and come away disappointed — it is genuinely lighter, and on very dry skin it will feel like it has evaporated by mid-afternoon. It is also a fairly plain formula: the appeal is reliability, not novel actives. If you want the barrier feel the line is known for, the cream is the product that delivers it.
