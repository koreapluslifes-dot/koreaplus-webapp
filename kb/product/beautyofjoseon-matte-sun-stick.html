Beauty of Joseon Matte Sun Stick: Mugwort + Camelia SPF50+ PA++++

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Matte Sun Stick: Mugwort + Camelia SPF50+ PA++++ is Beauty of Joseon's sunscreen, built around Mugwort (Artemisia) and Camellia (Dongbaek) Oil. Oily and combination skin, people who wear makeup and need a midday top-up, and anyone who gave up reapplying because it meant starting the whole face again.

A twist-up 선스틱 (seonseutik) — the sun stick format Korea popularised for reapplying over makeup — with a dry, powdery finish rather than the waxy slick most sticks leave behind. Mugwort and camellia sit behind organic filters. The appeal is practical: it lives in a bag, it does not leak, and it lets you top up nose and cheekbones at lunchtime without washing your face first.

🏷️ Beauty of Joseon ☀️ Sunscreen 💸 $14-20

What it is built around

🍵 Mugwort (Artemisia)🌺 Camellia (Dongbaek) Oil

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin, people who wear makeup and need a midday top-up, and anyone who gave up reapplying because it meant starting the whole face again. It is useful on hairlines, ears and the back of the neck. Dry skin will find the matte finish emphasises flaking. Not the format to rely on for your only application of the day.

☀️ Sun damage & photoageing🛢️ Oil control⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness

How to use it

Press and roll the stick over skin in overlapping passes, then pat with clean fingers to even it out — a single sweep across each cheek deposits far less than you think. Reapply over makeup every couple of hours outdoors. Use a cream or fluid for the morning application and keep this for top-ups.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Sticks are the hardest format to apply at the thickness lab testing assumes, so a quick swipe buys less protection than the rating implies — build it up in passes. The matte finish clings to flaking skin and can look powdery over dewy foundation. Mugwort and camellia are pleasant additions with no bearing on how well it protects.
