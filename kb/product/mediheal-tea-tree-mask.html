Mediheal Tea Tree Care Solution Essential Mask 티트리 케어 솔루션 에센셜 마스크

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Tea Tree Care Solution Essential Mask is Mediheal's sheet mask, built around Tea Tree and Panthenol (Pro-B5). Oily and breakout-prone skin wanting a calming twenty minutes, especially during a bad patch.

The blemish-focused sheet in Mediheal's core range: a soothing essence carrying tea tree leaf extract on the same thin cellulose sheet as the rest of the line. It is aimed at skin that is oily and irritated rather than dry, and the essence runs noticeably lighter than the hydrating versions. Tea tree appears here as an extract, not as the essential oil.

🏷️ Mediheal 🎭 Sheet mask 💸 $2-4 per sheet

What it is built around

🌱 Tea Tree🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)

Who it suits

Oily and breakout-prone skin wanting a calming twenty minutes, especially during a bad patch. It suits combination skin in summer. Dry skin will find it thin. It is genuinely not an acne treatment, and using it in place of an evidence-based active — a BHA, or something prescribed — is a poor trade.

🔴 Acne & breakouts🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🛢️ Oil control🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

On cleansed skin after toner, fifteen to twenty minutes, once or twice a week. Press in the remaining essence and follow with a light moisturiser. Avoid stacking it on the same night as a strong exfoliant; congested skin already being exfoliated does not need more stimulus on top.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Tea tree has some evidence at meaningful concentrations, mostly as the oil in leave-on products, and a sheet mask essence sits well below that. Expect calm, not clearing. Tea tree is also a known contact allergen for a minority, so patch-test. Persistent acne belongs with a clinician rather than with a box of masks.
