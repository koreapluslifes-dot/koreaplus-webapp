Dr.Jart+ Ceramidin Cream 세라마이딘 크림

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Ceramidin Cream is Dr.Jart+'s moisturizer / cream, built around Ceramides. Dry, dehydrated and winter-stressed skin, and skin that has been over-exfoliated to the point of stinging at everything.

The cream that made ceramides a mainstream K-beauty conversation. It is a thick, slightly waxy balm-cream built on a multi-ceramide complex, designed to sit on dry, tight, flaking skin and stay there. There is nothing clever going on beyond that: lipids, humectants and a firm occlusive finish. It has been reformulated more than once over the years, so tubs bought years apart can genuinely feel different.

🏷️ Dr.Jart+ 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $40-52

What it is built around

🧱 Ceramides

Who it suits

Dry, dehydrated and winter-stressed skin, and skin that has been over-exfoliated to the point of stinging at everything. It also works as a night cream over a retinoid. Oily and combination skin will find it heavy and may congest under it. If your main complaint is dullness or uneven tone rather than tightness, this is not the product for that job.

🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration🍂 Flaking & peeling🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

Night, as the last step, over serum. Warm a fingertip's worth between your palms first — straight from the tub it drags. In the morning use it only if your skin is genuinely dry, since it can make sunscreen slide. On very dry patches press a second thin layer on top rather than rubbing more in.

⚠️ Worth knowing: It is fragranced, which is an odd decision for a barrier cream aimed at sensitive skin, and reactive users notice. The texture is heavy enough that some people break out along the jaw and hairline. And ceramides support the comfort and look of the barrier — they do not resolve an underlying condition, so persistent flaking is a conversation for a dermatologist.
