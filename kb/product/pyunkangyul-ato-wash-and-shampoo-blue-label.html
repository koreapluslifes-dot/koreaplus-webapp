Pyunkang Yul ATO Wash & Shampoo Blue Label 아토 워시 앤 샴푸 블루라벨

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: ATO Wash & Shampoo Blue Label is Pyunkang Yul's water cleanser (gel / foam). Dry, tight, itchy-feeling skin that gets worse after showering, and households that want one bottle for everyone.

A combined body wash and shampoo from Pyunkang Yul's ATO line, formulated for atopy-prone skin and low-lathering by design. Korean atopy-branded washes avoid the aggressive surfactants that make conventional shower gels feel squeaky, on the reasoning that the shower is where a lot of dryness is manufactured in the first place. Fragrance-free, mild, and intended for the whole family including the 두피 (dupi), the scalp.

🏷️ Pyunkang Yul 🧼 Water cleanser (gel / foam)

Who it suits

Dry, tight, itchy-feeling skin that gets worse after showering, and households that want one bottle for everyone. It suits children's skin, again with a clinician's input if there is a diagnosis. People with oily scalps or heavy styling-product use will find it does not clean thoroughly enough, and anyone with a diagnosed scalp condition needs a medicated shampoo, not this.

🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🥵 Dryness & dehydration🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🍂 Flaking & peeling

How to use it

Use in a short, warm — not hot — shower. Work a small amount into wet hair at the scalp and let the run-off do the body, or apply directly to dry-feeling areas. Rinse well and pat dry, then moisturise within a few minutes while skin is still damp. That last step is where most of the benefit actually comes from.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Two-in-one products are a compromise and this is no exception: it is a gentle wash that happens to be usable on hair, not a good shampoo. Expect very little lather, which people misread as the product not working. And a mild wash does nothing for seborrhoeic dermatitis, psoriasis or persistent scalp itching — those need a diagnosis and, usually, a medicated product.
