Dr.Jart+ Ceramidin Liquid 세라마이딘 리퀴드

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Ceramidin Liquid is Dr.Jart+'s toner / skin, built around Ceramides. Dry and sensitive skin that finds conventional toners drying, and people who like the Korean layering habit.

The first step of the Ceramidin routine: a thin, milky liquid that behaves like a hydrating toner but carries the line's ceramide angle. Korean routines use a watery first step to prepare skin for everything after it, and this is Dr.Jart+'s version, slightly richer than a plain toner and slightly more slippery. It is best understood as a hydrating layer with a barrier-flavoured story attached.

🏷️ Dr.Jart+ 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $30-42

What it is built around

🧱 Ceramides

Who it suits

Dry and sensitive skin that finds conventional toners drying, and people who like the Korean layering habit. Combination skin will get on with it fine. Oily skin can use it, though cheaper hydrating toners do a comparable job. If your skin is currently comfortable, this is an optional addition rather than a missing step.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

Immediately after cleansing, on damp skin, morning and evening. Pour into the palms and press it in — two or three thin passes rather than one soaking. Follow with serum and the cream while skin still feels slightly tacky. Most people under-apply liquids like this; a properly covered face takes more than you think.

⚠️ Worth knowing: A ceramide in a rinse-adjacent watery step is doing much less than the same ingredient in a cream that stays put, so treat this as hydration first and barrier care a distant second. It is also expensive for a toner. If the budget is finite, put the money into the cream and use a plain hydrating toner underneath — you will not notice the difference.
