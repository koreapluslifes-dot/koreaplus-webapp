Abib Heartleaf Spot Pad Calming Touch

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Heartleaf Spot Pad Calming Touch is Abib's toner pad, built around Heartleaf (Houttuynia) and Panthenol (Pro-B5). Sensitive, flushed and blemish-prone skin that wants a calming step without acids, and anyone whose skin is currently unhappy from over-exfoliating.

A soothing rather than exfoliating toner pad, which makes it unusual in a format dominated by acids. The essence is built on 어성초 (eoseongcho), the heartleaf extract that runs through most of Abib's range, with panthenol alongside. The pads are large, thick and generously soaked, and they are as often used laid flat on a red patch for a few minutes as swept across the face. Abib's cotton pads are noticeably better quality than most.

🏷️ Abib ⬜ Toner pad 💸 $18-26

What it is built around

🌱 Heartleaf (Houttuynia)🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)

Who it suits

Sensitive, flushed and blemish-prone skin that wants a calming step without acids, and anyone whose skin is currently unhappy from over-exfoliating. It is one of the few pads safe to use daily. If your goal is blackheads or rough texture, this is the wrong product — it contains nothing that exfoliates and is not trying to.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🔴 Acne & breakouts🧱 Damaged moisture barrier

How to use it

After cleansing, sweep a pad across the face or lay it over a specific red area for three to five minutes as a mini-mask. Morning or evening, daily if you like. Pat in whatever essence remains rather than wiping it off, then continue with serum and moisturiser. In the morning, finish with sunscreen.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The word "spot" in the name implies treatment, and it is not one — this soothes the look of redness for a while, it does not clear a blemish, and a persistent or painful spot is a matter for a clinician. Cost per use is high compared with decanting a calming toner onto a plain cotton pad, which achieves much the same thing for less.

What is heartleaf in Korean skincare?
