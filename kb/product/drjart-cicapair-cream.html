Dr.Jart+ Cicapair Tiger Grass Cream 시카페어 크림

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Cicapair Tiger Grass Cream is Dr.Jart+'s moisturizer / cream, built around Centella (Cica) and Madecassoside. Skin that is red, reactive and slightly dry — post-shave, post-winter, post-too-many-acids.

The plain cream in Dr.Jart+'s cica range — 시카 (sika), the Korean shorthand for Centella asiatica — as opposed to the green colour-correcting treatment most people picture when the name comes up. It is a medium-weight cream carrying centella and its purified fractions, aimed at skin that flushes easily. Dense without being greasy, with a soft matte finish. The line is the most visible cica product in Western retail.

🏷️ Dr.Jart+ 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $45-60

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Skin that is red, reactive and slightly dry — post-shave, post-winter, post-too-many-acids. Normal and combination skin can use it year-round. Very oily skin may prefer a lighter cica gel. If your redness is persistent, patterned and comes with visible vessels, take that to a clinician rather than managing it with cosmetics; a cream addresses the look, not the cause.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🧱 Damaged moisture barrier

How to use it

Evenings as the last step, or mornings under sunscreen if your skin is not oily. A fingertip for the whole face. On a bad flushing day, apply to slightly damp skin and leave it alone rather than layering actives over the top. Give it a fortnight before judging — soothing products rarely announce themselves on day one.

⚠️ Worth knowing: It is fragranced, and a calming formula with fragrance is a contradiction reactive skin tends to notice. Buyers regularly order this expecting the green colour-correcting version and are disappointed, so check which one is in your basket. The price is well above what a comparable centella cream costs from a Korean drugstore brand doing the same job.
