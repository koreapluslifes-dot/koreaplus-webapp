KAHI Wrinkle Bounce Multi Balm 링클 바운스 멀티밤

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Wrinkle Bounce Multi Balm is KAHI's moisturizer / cream, built around Collagen (topical). Dry and normal skin that goes tight in air conditioning, offices and aeroplanes, and anyone who wants a glow on the high points of the face without redoing their base.

A twist-up balm stick that became a fixture of Korean handbags and, thanks to conspicuous placement in television dramas, of a great many baskets abroad. It is an occlusive stick — waxes, oils and a salmon-derived collagen story — swiped over dry patches, cheekbones and lips during the day. Not a moisturiser in the routine sense: a portable top-up that adds shine and stops tightness where you put it.

🏷️ KAHI 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $20-30

What it is built around

🧵 Collagen (topical)

Who it suits

Dry and normal skin that goes tight in air conditioning, offices and aeroplanes, and anyone who wants a glow on the high points of the face without redoing their base. Mature skin often likes it over makeup. Oily and congestion-prone skin should keep it off the mid-face — waxy sticks and clogged pores are an old and reliable pairing.

🥵 Dryness & dehydration🍂 Flaking & peeling🎈 Loss of firmness & elasticity

How to use it

Any time skin feels tight. Swipe once or twice over cheekbones, brow bones, lips and any flaking patch, then press with a fingertip to blend — dragging the stick repeatedly leaves streaks. Over makeup is fine. It is not a substitute for a night moisturiser and carries almost no hydration of its own.

⚠️ Worth knowing: 'Wrinkle' in the name is doing heavy lifting. It is an occlusive balm: it makes fine lines look softer while it sits there, because shine and surface hydration flatter texture. That effect leaves when the balm does. It is fragranced, it will shift makeup if you press too hard, and a shared stick is an unhygienic habit.
