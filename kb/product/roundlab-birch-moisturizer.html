Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Cream 자작나무 수분크림

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Birch Juice Moisturizing Cream is Round Lab's moisturizer / cream, built around Birch Sap and Panthenol (Pro-B5) and Hyaluronic Acid. Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and dry skin through warmer months.

Round Lab's water cream — 수분크림 (subun-keurim), the Korean category for a light, hydration-first moisturiser — built on birch sap rather than water, with panthenol behind it. It is cool, gel-like and quick to absorb, leaving skin padded rather than coated. The brand's whole identity is unfussy daily basics drawn from Korean water and sap, and this is the moisturiser end of that idea.

🏷️ Round Lab 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $20-30

What it is built around

🌳 Birch Sap🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Who it suits

Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and dry skin through warmer months. It is a good first Korean moisturiser because it is hard to dislike and unlikely to provoke anything. Genuinely dry skin in a cold, dry flat will want more lipids on top at night. Very oily skin can wear it alone; very sensitive skin generally tolerates it well.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🥵 Dryness & dehydration🧱 Damaged moisture barrier

How to use it

Morning and night as the last skincare step, or under sunscreen. Half a fingertip is enough — it spreads a long way. Press it into damp skin rather than rubbing. In a dry climate, layer it over a hydrating toner so the humectants have water to hold, and add an occlusive at night.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Lightly scented, which surprises people who assume a gentle basics brand means fragrance-free — check the version you are buying, as the line has shifted over time. The birch sap story is pleasant marketing; sap is mostly water with trace minerals and sugars, and the hydration you feel is ordinary humectants doing ordinary work.
