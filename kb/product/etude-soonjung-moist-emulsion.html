ETUDE SoonJung 10-Free Moist Emulsion 순정 10프리 모이스트 에멀젼

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: SoonJung 10-Free Moist Emulsion is ETUDE's emulsion / lotion, built around Panthenol (Pro-B5) and Madecassoside. Reactive or barrier-stressed skin, and anyone who finds creams too heavy but a serum not quite enough.

The lotion step in ETUDE's sensitive-skin line, formulated to the same minimum-irritant brief as the toner: panthenol and madecassoside, no added fragrance, no colourant, and the exclusion list the '10-free' name refers to. The texture is a genuine emulsion — thinner than a cream, thicker than an essence — which is a step Western routines tend to skip entirely.

🏷️ ETUDE 🥛 Emulsion / Lotion 💸 $14-20

What it is built around

🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Reactive or barrier-stressed skin, and anyone who finds creams too heavy but a serum not quite enough. It suits oily-but-sensitive skin particularly well and works year-round in humid climates. Very dry skin in winter will want a cream on top. There are no actives in it, which is the point rather than an oversight.

🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🥵 Dryness & dehydration🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌡️ Redness & sensitivity

How to use it

After your essence or serum, before sunscreen in the morning and before any final occlusive at night. A pea-sized amount covers the face. It makes a useful buffer on retinol or acid nights: emulsion, wait, active, then emulsion again over the top if your skin needs it.

⚠️ Worth knowing: '10-free' is a marketing label rather than a regulated standard — the specific exclusions vary by brand and tell you less than reading the list yourself. As a formula it is comfortable and unambitious; if your skin is calm and you already use a moisturiser, an emulsion adds a step without adding much. Dry skin will still need more.
