Nature Republic California Aloe Vera 92% Soothing Gel 캘리포니아 알로에 베라 92% 수딩젤

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: California Aloe Vera 92% Soothing Gel is Nature Republic's moisturizer / cream, built around Aloe Vera. Oily and combination skin in hot weather, and anyone wanting something cooling after a day outdoors.

The big blue tub of aloe gel that sits in a very large number of Korean bathrooms and guesthouse rooms. It is a cheap, watery gel with aloe leaf extract high in the list, a cooling finish and very little else. People use it as an after-sun layer, a light summer moisturiser, a hair and body soother and an emergency mask. It is not sophisticated and makes no attempt to be.

🏷️ Nature Republic 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream 💸 $8-15

What it is built around

🪴 Aloe Vera

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin in hot weather, and anyone wanting something cooling after a day outdoors. It is fine on the body. Dry skin gets nothing lasting from it — aloe gel evaporates and leaves skin no better sealed than before. Sensitive skin should patch test: aloe itself is mild, but the preservative and fragrance system here is not minimal.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity☀️ Sun damage & photoageing🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🛢️ Oil control

How to use it

A thin layer wherever skin feels hot, morning or night. On the face, use it in place of a light moisturiser in summer, or over one as a cooling top layer. Keep the tub in the fridge if you like — the cold is much of the appeal. Scoop with a spoon rather than dipping fingers in.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Sunburn is skin damage, and a cooling gel is comfort rather than care — a real burn needs a doctor's advice, not a tub of aloe. The '92%' refers to how much aloe leaf extract the brand declares in the formula, which tells you nothing about how it performs. It is fragranced and coloured, and it stings broken skin.
