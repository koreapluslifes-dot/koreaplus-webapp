Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Foam

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Red Blemish Clear Soothing Foam is Dr.G's water cleanser (gel / foam), built around Centella (Cica). Reactive, easily-flushed skin that finds standard foaming cleansers leave a tight, shiny feeling afterwards.

The cleanser in Dr.G's Red Blemish range, built to the same brief as the cream: a mild wash for skin that reacts to most foaming cleansers. It lathers modestly rather than squeakily, which is the point — the aim is to remove sunscreen and sweat without stripping the surface. As the second cleanse in a Korean double-cleansing routine it does exactly what it is supposed to and no more.

🏷️ Dr.G 🧼 Water cleanser (gel / foam)

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Reactive, easily-flushed skin that finds standard foaming cleansers leave a tight, shiny feeling afterwards. It works for combination and acne-prone skin too. Very dry skin may prefer a cream or milk cleanser. If your skin feels fine with whatever you already use, there is no argument for switching — cleansers are the step where upgrading changes the least.

🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🧱 Damaged moisture barrier

How to use it

Second step of an evening double cleanse, after an oil or balm has taken off sunscreen and makeup. A short pump's worth, emulsified in wet hands first, then thirty seconds on the face and a thorough rinse. Mornings, use it only if your skin feels congested on waking; water alone is often enough.

⚠️ Worth knowing: This is a rinse-off product, so the centella in it has seconds of contact time and does very little. Buy it because it is a gentle wash, not because of the calming claim on the front. The wider trap is the range name again — nothing here treats blemishes, and a cleanser has never been where acne is won or lost.
