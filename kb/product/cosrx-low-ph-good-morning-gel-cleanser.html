COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser 로우 pH 굿모닝 젤 클렌저

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser is COSRX's water cleanser (gel / foam), built around BHA (Salicylic) and Tea Tree. Oily and combination skin that wants a morning cleanse which does not strip, and anyone whose skin has been irritated by high-pH foaming washes.

The cleanser that taught a generation of readers what cleanser pH means. It is a mildly acidic gel that foams lightly, includes a small amount of BHA and tea tree oil, and rinses without the tight squeak of a traditional alkaline foaming wash. Its importance is historical as much as practical: it made low-pH cleansing a mainstream expectation. As a daily gel cleanser it remains competent, cheap and widely available.

🏷️ COSRX 🧼 Water cleanser (gel / foam) 💸 $10-15

What it is built around

🧽 BHA (Salicylic)🌱 Tea Tree

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin that wants a morning cleanse which does not strip, and anyone whose skin has been irritated by high-pH foaming washes. It is a reasonable second cleanse in the evening too. Dry and very sensitive skin may still find it slightly brisk and be happier with a milk or cream cleanser, particularly in winter.

🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🛢️ Oil control🕳️ Pores & texture🔴 Acne & breakouts

How to use it

A small amount on damp skin, massaged for around thirty seconds, then rinsed lukewarm. Fine morning and evening. In the evening use it after an oil or balm rather than instead of one. There is no benefit to leaving it sitting on the face — the low pH is about how it rinses, not about doing something while it waits.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The BHA content is far too low, and the contact time far too short, for this to affect breakouts — a lot of people bought it as an acne treatment because of the ingredient list and were disappointed. It is a good gentle cleanser, nothing more. It also contains tea tree oil, which a small number of people react to, so patch-test if essential oils have caused you trouble before.
