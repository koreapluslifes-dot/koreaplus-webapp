Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Cleansing Foam

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Cleansing Foam is Torriden's water cleanser (gel / foam), built around Hyaluronic Acid. Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and anyone using the rest of the DIVE-IN range who wants consistency across the routine.

The cleanser in Torriden's DIVE-IN range, the vegan hydration line built on low-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid that carries the brand internationally. It is a mild, low-foaming wash designed to leave skin feeling cushioned rather than clean-squeaky, with no fragrance in the formula. As with every cleanser in this bracket, the appeal is in what it avoids doing: it does not strip, and it does not leave a film.

🏷️ Torriden 🧼 Water cleanser (gel / foam) 💸 $12-18

What it is built around

💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Who it suits

Normal, combination and dehydrated skin, and anyone using the rest of the DIVE-IN range who wants consistency across the routine. It is gentle enough for most sensitive skin. Oily and congested skin will find it perfectly usable but underwhelming — there is no oil-control angle here, and nothing addressing blackheads.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🥵 Dryness & dehydration

How to use it

A short amount on damp skin, lathered lightly and massaged for around thirty seconds before rinsing with lukewarm water. Morning and evening. Follow immediately with a hydrating toner while the skin is still damp — that is where the hyaluronic acid in this routine actually earns its keep.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Hyaluronic acid in a rinse-off product is close to decorative. It is a humectant that works by staying on the skin and holding water there; wash it away and the benefit goes with it. The naming across the range invites you to think the cleanser hydrates in the way the serum does. It does not — it simply avoids drying you out, which is a lower and more honest bar.

What is hyaluronic acid and how do you use it?

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant that draws water into the skin for a plumper, hydrated look. In Korean routines it's best applied to damp skin and sealed with a moisturizer on top, so the moisture it attracts stays locked in rather than evaporating.

What is double cleansing?

Double cleansing is a two-step Korean method: first an oil-based cleanser dissolves makeup, sunscreen and sebum, then a water-based cleanser removes sweat and water-soluble debris. The pairing aims to clean thoroughly without the tightness or stripping that one harsh wash can cause.
