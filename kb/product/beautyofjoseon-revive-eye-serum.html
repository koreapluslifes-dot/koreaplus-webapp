Beauty of Joseon Revive Eye Serum: Ginseng + Retinal

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Revive Eye Serum: Ginseng + Retinal is Beauty of Joseon's eye cream, built around Ginseng and Retinaldehyde (Retinal). People who want a retinoid around the eyes but have found retinol creams too irritating there, and anyone whose complaint is fine lines and crepey texture rather than dark circles.

A rare thing: an eye product with an actual active in it. Beauty of Joseon pairs ginseng extract with retinal — the aldehyde form of vitamin A, one step closer to the active form than retinol — at a low, eye-area-appropriate level in a light serum texture. It is priced like a drugstore product and formulated like something more considered, which is most of the reason it sells the way it does.

🏷️ Beauty of Joseon 👁️ Eye cream 💸 $15-22

What it is built around

🫚 Ginseng🌙 Retinaldehyde (Retinal)

Who it suits

People who want a retinoid around the eyes but have found retinol creams too irritating there, and anyone whose complaint is fine lines and crepey texture rather than dark circles. Retinoid beginners should establish tolerance on the rest of the face first. Avoid during pregnancy and breastfeeding — retinoids are the standard exception, and that is a question for your doctor.

⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness🌑 Dark circles & eye area🪨 Rough & uneven texture

How to use it

PM only, a small amount tapped around the orbital bone, two or three nights a week to begin with and building slowly from there. Follow with a plain moisturiser. Sunscreen the next morning is non-negotiable with any retinoid. If the eye area flakes or stings, reduce the frequency rather than pushing through it.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Retinal is still a retinoid: expect an adjustment period, and do not layer it with acids or a second retinoid on the same night. The eye area is thin and unforgiving, and people routinely apply far too much. The ginseng contributes antioxidant character and scent; the anti-ageing work here is the retinal, and results are gradual.
