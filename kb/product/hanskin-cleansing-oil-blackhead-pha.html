Hanskin Cleansing Oil & Blackhead PHA

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Cleansing Oil & Blackhead PHA is Hanskin's oil cleanser / cleansing balm, built around PHA (Gluconolactone). Oily and combination skin dealing with visible blackheads around the nose and chin who want their first cleanse to feel light.

A cleansing oil with PHA (gluconolactone) folded into the formula, which is an interesting idea more than a proven one. The oil base itself is genuinely good: light, quick to emulsify, and effective on sunscreen and long-wear makeup without the residue heavier oils leave. The acid is best understood as an extra rather than the reason to buy. It is one of the longer-established Korean cleansing oils sold internationally.

🏷️ Hanskin 🫗 Oil cleanser / Cleansing balm 💸 $18-25

What it is built around

🫧 PHA (Gluconolactone)

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin dealing with visible blackheads around the nose and chin who want their first cleanse to feel light. It works well as a daily makeup-and-SPF remover for most skin types that are not actively dry. If your skin is dry or reactive, the acid content is not a problem in itself, but you will likely prefer a balm for comfort.

⚫ Blackheads & sebaceous filaments🕳️ Pores & texture🪨 Rough & uneven texture🛢️ Oil control

How to use it

Pump onto dry skin, massage for around a minute concentrating on congested areas, add water to emulsify, then rinse and follow with a water-based cleanser. PM only. There is no benefit to leaving it on longer in the hope the acid does more — it will not, and prolonged massage irritates more than it clears.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The PHA is the marketing hook and the weakest part of the pitch. Acids need contact time to do anything measurable, and a cleanser sits on your face for a minute before being rinsed away. Treat any smoothing you notice as a bonus, not a reason to skip a leave-on exfoliant. If texture is your primary concern, a BHA liquid is the product that actually addresses it.
