Goodal Green Tangerine Vita C Dark Spot Serum

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Green Tangerine Vita C Dark Spot Serum is Goodal's serum, built around Vitamin C. Normal, combination, oily and dry skin looking for the look of a more even tone without the sting and instability of pure vitamin C.

A tone-focused serum built on green tangerine extract from Jeju — the unripe fruit, which the brand markets as the more concentrated stage — rather than on a high dose of pure ascorbic acid. That makes it a gentler, more stable proposition than a traditional vitamin C serum, and a considerably slower one. The texture is light and non-sticky with a clear citrus scent, and it has been a fixture of Korean drugstore brightening for years.

🏷️ Goodal 🧪 Serum

What it is built around

🍊 Vitamin C

Who it suits

Normal, combination, oily and dry skin looking for the look of a more even tone without the sting and instability of pure vitamin C. A sensible option for people who tried ascorbic acid and gave up. Anyone with established pigmentation who wants visible change will need more than this, plus rigorous sun protection, and probably a clinician's input if it is melasma.

🟤 Dark spots & tone🌟 Dullness & glow🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)☀️ Sun damage & photoageing

How to use it

Morning or evening after toner, before moisturiser. A few drops over the whole face rather than dabbing at individual marks. Morning use goes under sunscreen, which is the part of any tone routine that actually decides the outcome. Give it two to three months of daily use before forming a view.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Extract-led vitamin C is not the same proposition as ascorbic acid, and the label leans on the vitamin C association harder than the formula earns. Expect subtle change at best. The citrus scent is noticeable, so check the current ingredient list if fragrance is an issue for you. Dark spot in a product name is a cosmetic tone claim, not a promise of removal.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
