Dear, Klairs Supple Preparation Facial Toner 서플 프레퍼레이션 페이셜 토너

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Supple Preparation Facial Toner is Dear, Klairs's toner / skin, built around Hyaluronic Acid and Centella (Cica). Dry and normal skin that wants a comforting, slightly cushioned first layer.

One of the toners that introduced Western buyers to the idea that a Korean toner hydrates rather than strips. It is a slightly viscous, mildly acidic layer of humectants with centella and other plant extracts, positioned as preparation for whatever follows. Klairs also sells an unscented version, which matters more than usual here, because the original is perfumed with essential oils.

🏷️ Dear, Klairs 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $18-25

What it is built around

💧 Hyaluronic Acid🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Dry and normal skin that wants a comforting, slightly cushioned first layer. Sensitive skin should go straight to the unscented version rather than testing the original. Oily skin sometimes finds the scented one a touch heavy under sunscreen. It is a general hydrator, not a treatment — it will not move the needle on pigmentation or breakouts.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🥵 Dryness & dehydration

How to use it

After cleansing, AM and PM, patted into damp skin. Two layers if your skin is thirsty. It sits comfortably under acids or retinol as a buffer layer. Give it half a minute to absorb before the next step, or it can pill underneath thicker creams and silicone-containing sunscreens.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The original formula contains lavender and other essential oils. They smell pleasant and are a well-documented trigger for reactive and eczema-prone skin — if you flush or itch easily, buy the unscented version. The two are otherwise close enough in performance that there is little reason to choose the scented one on merit.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.

What is a Korean toner pad?
