Abib Quick Sunstick Protection Bar SPF50+ PA++++

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Quick Sunstick Protection Bar SPF50+ PA++++ is Abib's sunscreen, built around Glycerin. Anyone who needs to reapply during the day without dismantling their makeup, people with short hair who burn along the parting, and parents applying sunscreen to a moving child.

Abib's twist-up sun stick, and one of the most widely exported examples of the format. It is a firm bar with a fairly dry finish that presses a layer of organic filters onto skin or over makeup in a few seconds. Abib built its name on calming pads and sheet masks; this is the practical corner of the range, sold on convenience rather than on any ingredient story.

🏷️ Abib ☀️ Sunscreen 💸 $18-26

What it is built around

💧 Glycerin

Who it suits

Anyone who needs to reapply during the day without dismantling their makeup, people with short hair who burn along the parting, and parents applying sunscreen to a moving child. Oily and combination skin suit the finish best. Dry skin may find it emphasises flaking. It is a top-up tool, not the format to rely on for a full morning application.

☀️ Sun damage & photoageing🛢️ Oil control

How to use it

Press and roll it over the skin in overlapping passes rather than one sweep, then pat with a clean finger to even the layer out. Reapply every couple of hours outdoors. Use a cream or fluid sunscreen for the first application of the day and keep this in a bag for everything after that.

⚠️ Worth knowing: A shared stick collects whatever was on the last face it touched, which is worth a thought if you lend it around. The waxy base can congest oily skin over weeks of daily use. And reapplying a stick over an already-sunscreened face disturbs makeup more than a cushion does — the format has real limits alongside its convenience.
