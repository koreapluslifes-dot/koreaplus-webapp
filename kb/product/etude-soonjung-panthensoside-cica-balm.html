ETUDE SoonJung Panthensoside Cica Balm

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: SoonJung Panthensoside Cica Balm is ETUDE's moisturizer / cream, built around Panthenol (Pro-B5) and Madecassoside and Centella (Cica). Skin having a bad week: post-exfoliation tightness, wind-burnt cheeks, the flat patches left after a spot has gone.

The comfort layer of the SoonJung line, combining the panthenol the range is built on with centella derivatives. ETUDE calls the blend Panthensoside, which is a brand name rather than an ingredient category. Despite "balm" in the title it is closer to a rich cream than an ointment — it presses in and disappears rather than sitting on the surface like a salve, and it is fragrance-free like the rest of the line.

🏷️ ETUDE 🧴 Moisturizer / Cream

What it is built around

🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Skin having a bad week: post-exfoliation tightness, wind-burnt cheeks, the flat patches left after a spot has gone. It suits sensitive and dry skin, and combination skin can use it in targeted patches rather than all over. Oily skin will find full-face use heavy. It is a soothing cosmetic, so anything genuinely inflamed or persistent goes to a clinician, not to a balm.

🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration

How to use it

As the last step at night on the areas that need it, over serum, or all over the face if your skin is dry. It also works as a targeted patch treatment on rough spots during the day. Use less than you think — it looks thick in the tub and spreads a long way once warmed between the fingers.

⚠️ Worth knowing: "Balm" sets the wrong expectation. If you are after a genuinely occlusive ointment for cracked, weeping or badly compromised skin, this is not dense enough and you should be asking a pharmacist or doctor instead. It is also easy to over-apply into a tacky film. And the Panthensoside name is proprietary — do not go looking for it on other brands' ingredient lists.
