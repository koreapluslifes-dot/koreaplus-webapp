Aestura Atobarrier 365 Hydro Essence 아토베리어 365 하이드로 에센스

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Atobarrier 365 Hydro Essence is Aestura's essence, built around Ceramides and Hyaluronic Acid. Dehydrated skin of any type, including oily skin that feels tight after cleansing.

A water-gel essence from the Atobarrier line, positioned as the hydrating step before the lotion or cream. It is the lightest thing the range makes: a bouncy gel that sinks in quickly and leaves almost no film. The pitch is that skin marketed as barrier-compromised still wants water, not just lipids, and that a humectant layer under an occlusive one works better than either alone.

🏷️ Aestura 🌿 Essence

What it is built around

🧱 Ceramides💧 Hyaluronic Acid

Who it suits

Dehydrated skin of any type, including oily skin that feels tight after cleansing. It is a sensible entry point to the line if you have never used a ceramide product and are unsure how your skin will respond. Skin that is properly dry rather than dehydrated will need something over the top — used alone in a cold, dry room this will not hold.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin

How to use it

After cleansing and toner, morning and evening. A coin-sized amount, pressed in with the palms rather than dragged with fingertips. Follow with the lotion or cream while your skin is still slightly tacky, which is when the occlusive step does the most good. It layers under sunscreen without pilling if you give it thirty seconds.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Humectant-heavy gels can feel drying in genuinely arid conditions if you leave them uncovered — the water goes somewhere, and in a dehumidified office it is not into your skin. Always put something over it. The name also invites the assumption that an essence is doing more than a toner; in this range the difference is texture and price, not a different order of ingredient.

What is essence in Korean skincare?

Essence is a lightweight, watery Korean skincare layer applied after toner and before serum. It's designed to hydrate and prep the skin so later products absorb better. Often fermented or ingredient-rich, essence is sometimes called the heart of a Korean routine.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is a first essence and do I need one?

A first essence is a watery, high-percentage ferment applied straight after cleansing, before anything else. It is a hydration and conditioning step. Pleasant and popular, but genuinely optional.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
