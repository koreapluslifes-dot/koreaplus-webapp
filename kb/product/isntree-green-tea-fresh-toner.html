Isntree Green Tea Fresh Toner

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Green Tea Fresh Toner is Isntree's toner / skin, built around Green Tea and Panthenol (Pro-B5). Oily and combination skin, particularly in summer or a humid climate, and breakout-prone skin already using an exfoliant elsewhere that does not want a second one.

The oily-skin counterpart to Isntree's hyaluronic toner: a thin, fast-drying water layer based on green tea with soothing humectants behind it. There are no acids, which is unusual for a toner aimed at oily skin — the idea is hydration and calm rather than decongestion, on the reasonable premise that stripping oily skin tends to make it oilier.

🏷️ Isntree 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $18-25

What it is built around

🍃 Green Tea🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)

Who it suits

Oily and combination skin, particularly in summer or a humid climate, and breakout-prone skin already using an exfoliant elsewhere that does not want a second one. Dry or mature skin will find it too slight. Anyone hoping a green tea toner will hold sebum in check across a whole day should temper expectations considerably.

🛢️ Oil control🔴 Acne & breakouts🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🕳️ Pores & texture

How to use it

After cleansing, morning and night, two thin layers on damp skin. It disappears quickly, which makes it an easy AM step under sunscreen. Follow with a light gel moisturiser rather than skipping the seal — oily skin still needs one, and skipping it is a common reason oily routines stall.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Green tea polyphenols are genuinely antioxidant in a lab setting, but how much survives in a water toner and how long it stays on skin make any meaningful effect hard to claim. Treat this as a light hydrator that happens to smell faintly of tea. It will not shrink pores — nothing topical does.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.

What is a Korean toner pad?
