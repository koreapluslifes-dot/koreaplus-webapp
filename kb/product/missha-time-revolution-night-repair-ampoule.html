MISSHA Time Revolution Night Repair Ampoule

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Time Revolution Night Repair Ampoule is MISSHA's ampoule, built around Bifida Ferment Lysate and Niacinamide (B3). Normal, dry, combination and mature skin wanting a comfortable evening ampoule with a hydrating, smoothing finish.

A ferment-led night ampoule and one of the longest-running products in Korean mass skincare, sold from the beginning as an accessible answer to a famous Western night serum. Bifida ferment lysate is the headline: a bacterial ferment filtrate used across the category for the look of resilience and smoothness. The texture is a light amber fluid that sinks in fast. It has been reformulated and renamed across several generations, each marked on the box.

🏷️ MISSHA 💉 Ampoule

What it is built around

🧫 Bifida Ferment Lysate🧫 Niacinamide (B3)

Who it suits

Normal, dry, combination and mature skin wanting a comfortable evening ampoule with a hydrating, smoothing finish. It is an easy product to tolerate and layers under anything. It is not an active in the sense a retinoid or an acid is, so anyone hoping to change texture or pigment will need something else in the routine. Fragrance-sensitive skin should check the current pack.

⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌟 Dullness & glow🎈 Loss of firmness & elasticity

How to use it

Evening, after toner, before moisturiser or a sleeping mask. A few drops pressed over face and neck. It suits the nights you are not using a retinoid, and it also works as a buffering layer under one if your skin is sensitive. Give it a season rather than a fortnight before deciding.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The comparison to a famous imported night serum has sold this ampoule for years, and the two formulas are not the same product at a different price — treat the dupe framing as marketing. The line has also changed formula and numbering repeatedly, so older reviews may describe something quite different. It is fragranced, and ferment-heavy formulas do not suit every reactive skin.

What is an ampoule in K-beauty?

An ampoule is a highly concentrated Korean skincare treatment, stronger than a serum, used to target specific concerns like dullness, dryness or fine lines. Applied after essence and before moisturizer, it's typically used in short bursts or when skin needs an extra boost.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
