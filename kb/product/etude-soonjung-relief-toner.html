ETUDE SoonJung pH 5.5 Relief Toner 순정 pH 5.5 릴리프 토너

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: SoonJung pH 5.5 Relief Toner is ETUDE's toner / skin, built around Panthenol (Pro-B5) and Madecassoside. Reactive, flushed or barrier-compromised skin, and anyone recovering from over-exfoliation.

The toner from ETUDE's SoonJung range — 순정 (sunjeong, roughly 'pure and gentle') — formulated around panthenol and madecassoside at a mildly acidic pH close to skin's own. It is fragrance-free, colourant-free and built to the minimum-irritant brief Korean brands apply to sensitive-skin lines. Cheap, widely stocked, and one of the least eventful things you can put on your face.

🏷️ ETUDE 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $12-18

What it is built around

🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Reactive, flushed or barrier-compromised skin, and anyone recovering from over-exfoliation. It is also a sound beginner toner for teenage skin that is oily but easily irritated. Very dry skin will find it thin on its own. There is nothing in it for pigmentation, lines or pores, and it makes no pretence otherwise.

🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)

How to use it

First step after cleansing, twice a day. Pat in with hands on damp skin and layer twice if needed. Useful on retinol or acid nights as a calming buffer applied before and after the active. Follow with a cream, particularly if your skin runs dry or the weather has turned.

⚠️ Worth knowing: 'Relief' is doing a lot of work in that name. It calms the look of irritation and adds water; it does not fix an underlying condition. Redness, stinging or flaking that persists for weeks is a reason to see a dermatologist rather than buy another gentle toner. Gentleness also has a ceiling — a thin toner rarely does the heavy lifting on a damaged barrier.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.
