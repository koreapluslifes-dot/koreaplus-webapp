Round Lab 1025 Dokdo Toner 1025 독도 토너

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: 1025 Dokdo Toner is Round Lab's toner / skin, built around Panthenol (Pro-B5) and Glycerin. A safe default for sensitive, barrier-stressed or newly reactive skin, and a sensible first Korean toner for anyone who has been burnt by fragranced products.

Round Lab's flagship, named for the coordinates of the Dokdo islets whose deep-sea mineral water forms its base. The formula is deliberately unremarkable: mineral water, humectants, panthenol, a little betaine, no fragrance and no essential oils. It belongs to the category of toners designed to be boring on purpose — a plain water-and-humectant layer you can use twice a day for years without your skin objecting.

🏷️ Round Lab 💦 Toner / Skin 💸 $15-22

What it is built around

🩹 Panthenol (Pro-B5)💧 Glycerin

Who it suits

A safe default for sensitive, barrier-stressed or newly reactive skin, and a sensible first Korean toner for anyone who has been burnt by fragranced products. Dry skin will need something richer layered over it. If you want visible brightening or exfoliation from a toner this is the wrong bottle — it does hydration and nothing else, by design.

🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🥵 Dryness & dehydration

How to use it

Straight after cleansing, AM and PM, patted in with hands. Two thin layers on damp skin do more than one heavy one. Fine underneath any active, and useful as a buffer on nights you use retinol or an exfoliant. A cotton pad works but wastes a good deal of product.

⚠️ Worth knowing: The deep-sea mineral water story is doing more work than the ingredient does — mineral content at these levels is not a demonstrated skincare benefit. Judge it as a well-made, fragrance-free humectant toner, which it is, rather than as something special from Ulleungdo. A few people find the slight slip takes a moment to settle under sunscreen.

What is a Korean toner and how is it different?

In Korea, toner (called 'skin' or 'skin toner') is usually a gentle, hydrating step that preps and balances skin after cleansing, rather than a harsh astringent. It's meant to add moisture and help following layers absorb, not to strip or tighten the skin.

What is a Korean toner pad?

A pre-soaked cotton or embossed pad delivering a measured dose of toner, or of a mild exfoliating or soothing treatment. It removes the guesswork about how much product to use.
