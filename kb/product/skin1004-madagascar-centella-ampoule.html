⚡ Quick answer: Madagascar Centella Ampoule is SKIN1004's ampoule, built around Centella (Cica) and Madecassoside. Sensitive and easily-flushed skin, oily skin that reacts to richer soothing products, and anyone running a routine with acids or retinoids who needs a neutral layer on the off nights.

A deliberately minimal ampoule: centella asiatica extract from Madagascar and very little else, sold in a large dropper bottle at a price that makes people use it generously. Centella is the most-studied soothing botanical in Korean skincare, and its isolated fractions have decent support for the appearance of redness and irritation. The formula's whole argument is subtraction — no fragrance, no essential oils, nothing extra for reactive skin to catch on.

🏷️ SKIN1004 💉 Ampoule 💸 $15-22

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)🍀 Madecassoside

Who it suits

Sensitive and easily-flushed skin, oily skin that reacts to richer soothing products, and anyone running a routine with acids or retinoids who needs a neutral layer on the off nights. Normal skin can use it as a light hydrating step. Dry skin should not expect it to moisturise — there is almost nothing occlusive in the bottle, and a cream on top is mandatory.

🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🔴 Acne & breakouts

How to use it

After cleansing and toner, before moisturiser, morning and evening if you like. Several drops pressed into the face, which is easy given the bottle size. It absorbs almost instantly and layers under sunscreen without pilling. Useful as a first-aid step: a generous layer under a plain moisturiser on a day when your skin has had enough.

⚠️ Worth knowing: This is centella in water, and its ceiling is correspondingly low — it makes an irritated face look calmer and does not do anything else. The large bottle encourages using far more than any layer needs. Centella is well tolerated but not universally: a small number of people react to it, and being a plant extract does not exempt it. Patch-test if you are reactive.

What is cica or centella in skincare?

Cica is short for Centella asiatica, a plant also called tiger grass and widely used in Korean skincare to soothe the look of redness and support a comfortable, resilient barrier. It's a go-to for sensitive, stressed or breakout-prone skin in calming creams and serums.

What is an ampoule in K-beauty?

An ampoule is a highly concentrated Korean skincare treatment, stronger than a serum, used to target specific concerns like dullness, dryness or fine lines. Applied after essence and before moisturizer, it's typically used in short bursts or when skin needs an extra boost.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
