d'Alba White Truffle First Spray Serum 화이트 트러플 퍼스트 스프레이 세럼

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: White Truffle First Spray Serum is d'Alba's face mist. Dry, dull or mature skin that wants a fast one-step glow layer, and travellers who want a serum that survives a carry-on liquids bag.

A two-phase spray that shakes into an emulsion of white truffle extract and plant oils, applied as the first step after cleansing. It is the product that made d'Alba a duty-free fixture, and the format is genuinely unusual — an oil-containing serum delivered as a mist, leaving a soft, faintly luminous finish rather than a wet one you have to wait out.

🏷️ d'Alba 💨 Face mist 💸 $35-55

Who it suits

Dry, dull or mature skin that wants a fast one-step glow layer, and travellers who want a serum that survives a carry-on liquids bag. Oily and acne-prone skin should be cautious — the oil phase does not suit everyone. It is a comfort and finish product rather than a treatment for any particular concern.

🥵 Dryness & dehydration🌟 Dullness & glow🥤 Dehydration (water-poor skin)

How to use it

Shake hard until the phases combine, mist onto cleansed skin from a short distance, then press in. Two or three sprays, AM and PM, before the rest of your steps. It can also be misted over makeup for a dewier finish, though it will move foundation if you overdo it.

⚠️ Worth knowing: White truffle is an expensive-sounding ingredient with essentially no independent evidence behind it in skincare — the premium buys a story. It is fragranced. The spray format also means a fair amount of product ends up in the air rather than on your face, which makes the real cost per use higher than the bottle size suggests.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is a first essence and do I need one?

A first essence is a watery, high-percentage ferment applied straight after cleansing, before anything else. It is a hydration and conditioning step. Pleasant and popular, but genuinely optional.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
