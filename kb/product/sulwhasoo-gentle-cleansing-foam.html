⚡ Quick answer: Gentle Cleansing Foam is Sulwhasoo's water cleanser (gel / foam). Dry and mature skin that treats cleansing as part of the pleasure of a routine and wants the scent and texture to match the rest of a Sulwhasoo shelf.

The cleanser from Sulwhasoo's 순행 (Sunhaeng) line, the entry point into a brand otherwise built on ginseng-led prestige serums and creams. It is a dense, creamy foam with the hanbang herbal scent the house is known for, and it rinses without any tightness. As a piece of formulation it is well made and pleasant. As a purchase it is a luxury-tier price for a step that lasts under a minute.

🏷️ Sulwhasoo 🧼 Water cleanser (gel / foam)

Who it suits

Dry and mature skin that treats cleansing as part of the pleasure of a routine and wants the scent and texture to match the rest of a Sulwhasoo shelf. It is genuinely comfortable and does not strip. If you are choosing between spending on this or on a treatment step, the treatment step is where the money does more.

🥵 Dryness & dehydration🌾 Mature skin care🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌟 Dullness & glow

How to use it

Lather a small amount in wet hands, massage over damp skin for around thirty seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water. Evening as the second cleanse after an oil or balm; morning if your skin tolerates a foam on waking. Follow with the first essence or toner while skin is still damp.

⚠️ Worth knowing: This is the clearest example in the category of paying for a rinse-off. No cleanser, at any price, stays on the skin long enough for expensive botanicals to matter; the herbal scent is real and the formula is comfortable, but so are cleansers at a fifth of the cost. The fragrance is also pronounced, which rules it out for anyone whose skin flares at scent.
