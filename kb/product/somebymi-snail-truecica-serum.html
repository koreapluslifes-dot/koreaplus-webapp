Some By Mi Snail Truecica Miracle Repair Serum

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Snail Truecica Miracle Repair Serum is Some By Mi's serum, built around Snail Mucin and Madecassoside and Centella (Cica). Dry, normal and combination skin recovering the look of comfort after a period of over-exfoliation, and sensitive skin that gets on with snail mucin.

The gentler half of the brand's range, pairing snail secretion filtrate with the centella fraction it markets as Truecica. Snail mucin is a well-liked humectant that leaves skin looking plump and dewy; the centella side is there for the appearance of redness. Together they make a comfortable, slightly tacky serum aimed at people whose skin has been through an acid-heavy phase and now looks tired rather than congested.

🏷️ Some By Mi 🧪 Serum

What it is built around

🐌 Snail Mucin🍀 Madecassoside🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Dry, normal and combination skin recovering the look of comfort after a period of over-exfoliation, and sensitive skin that gets on with snail mucin. Good alongside, not instead of, sun protection when you are watching post-blemish marks fade. It is mollusc-derived, so not for vegans. Very oily skin often finds the film heavier than it wants in summer.

🩹 Post-acne marks (PIE & PIH)🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🥵 Dryness & dehydration

How to use it

Evening after toner, before moisturiser, or morning if you like the dewy finish under makeup. A few drops on slightly damp skin, pressed in and allowed a moment to set. It layers well over a soothing ampoule and under a cream. Nothing in it conflicts with actives used on other nights.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Repair and miracle are cosmetic marketing, not descriptions of what happens — snail mucin hydrates and comforts, and the evidence for anything beyond that is thin. Post-blemish marks fade largely on their own with time and diligent sunscreen, which is unglamorous but true. The texture is genuinely sticky, and mollusc-derived ingredients are worth patch-testing if you react to shellfish.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

Is snail mucin safe for skin?

Snail mucin (snail secretion filtrate) is widely used in Korean skincare and generally well tolerated, including for sensitive skin. It's valued as a hydrating, comforting ingredient. As with any product, patch-test first, and those with known allergies to mollusks or related ingredients should be cautious.
