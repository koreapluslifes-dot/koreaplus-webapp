VT Cosmetics Reedle Shot 100

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Reedle Shot 100 is VT Cosmetics's ampoule, built around Centella (Cica). Normal, combination and oily skin that is otherwise healthy, curious about the sensation and willing to build up slowly.

The product that made spicule ampoules a mainstream category. Spicules are microscopic mineral-like needles derived from freshwater sponge, suspended in a centella-based fluid; they sit on the skin and create very shallow micro-channels, which is why the brand talks about needles at home. The numbering — 100, 300, 700 — marks increasing spicule intensity rather than a percentage of anything, and 100 is the entry point for a reason.

🏷️ VT Cosmetics 💉 Ampoule

What it is built around

🌿 Centella (Cica)

Who it suits

Normal, combination and oily skin that is otherwise healthy, curious about the sensation and willing to build up slowly. It suits people chasing the look of smoother texture. It is emphatically not for sensitive, reactive or barrier-compromised skin, not for anyone with active inflamed breakouts or broken skin, and not for anyone already running a retinoid or acids more than occasionally.

🪨 Rough & uneven texture🕳️ Pores & texture🌟 Dullness & glow⏳ Anti-ageing & firmness

How to use it

Evening, on clean dry skin, starting once or twice a week. A small amount patted — never rubbed hard — then left a few minutes before a plain moisturiser. Skip everything else active that night. Sunscreen the next day matters, since freshly abraded skin is more vulnerable. Stop entirely if stinging persists past the first minutes.

⚠️ Worth knowing: This is a physical abrasive dressed as a technology. Heat and prickling are described as normal, which makes it hard for users to tell irritation from the intended sensation — a poor situation for any product. The at-home needling framing implies clinical microneedling results that a topical cannot deliver. Anyone considering it for scarring should see a clinician about the actual procedure instead.
