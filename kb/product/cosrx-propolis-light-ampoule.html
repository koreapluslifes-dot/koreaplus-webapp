COSRX Full Fit Propolis Light Ampoule

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Full Fit Propolis Light Ampoule is COSRX's ampoule, built around Propolis. Dry and normal skin that wants dewiness without an occlusive layer, and combination skin in winter.

A propolis extract ampoule with a honeyed, faintly amber look and a texture to match — light in name, decidedly sticky in practice. Propolis is the resinous material bees collect and process; in skincare it is used as a comforting, mildly antioxidant humectant and it is one of the ingredients most associated with the glowing, wet-looking finish people buy Korean skincare for. The extract content is high enough that the product smells and behaves like what it is.

🏷️ COSRX 💉 Ampoule

What it is built around

🍯 Propolis

Who it suits

Dry and normal skin that wants dewiness without an occlusive layer, and combination skin in winter. It flatters dull, tired-looking skin more than almost anything else at this price. Anyone with a known reaction to bee products — propolis, honey, royal jelly — should leave it alone, and very oily skin will find the tack unpleasant by mid-afternoon.

🌟 Dullness & glow🥵 Dryness & dehydration🧱 Damaged moisture barrier🌡️ Redness & sensitivity

How to use it

Evening suits it best, after toner and before moisturiser, though a thin layer in the morning under a light sunscreen works if you like the finish. Two or three drops pressed rather than rubbed in. Give it a minute to set before anything goes on top, or the stickiness drags.

⚠️ Worth knowing: Propolis is a reasonably common contact allergen, so patch-test properly rather than going straight to the face — anyone who reacts to bee stings or honey should be especially careful. The finish is genuinely tacky and can pill under certain sunscreens. And propolis products vary wildly in extract quality between brands, so reviews of one are not evidence about another.

What is an ampoule in K-beauty?

An ampoule is a highly concentrated Korean skincare treatment, stronger than a serum, used to target specific concerns like dullness, dryness or fine lines. Applied after essence and before moisturizer, it's typically used in short bursts or when skin needs an extra boost.

What is propolis in skincare?

Propolis is a bee-derived, honey-like extract used in Korean skincare for a comforting, nourishing glow and antioxidant support. It's popular in soothing essences and ampoules for dry or stressed skin. Anyone with a bee-product allergy should avoid it and patch-test before regular use.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
