Dr.G Green Mild Up Sun+

⏱ 2 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Green Mild Up Sun+ is Dr.G's sunscreen. Sensitive and normal skin that wants daily protection it will not resent applying.

Dr.G's sunscreen for reactive skin, sold under the brand's green dermocosmetic positioning and carrying a high SPF with PA++++ for UVA. It is the sun step most people meet Dr.G through after the soothing cream. The formula is pitched at skin that stings with conventional sunscreens, and it wears more like a light moisturiser than a beach product, which is the whole reason Korean sunscreens travel so well.

🏷️ Dr.G ☀️ Sunscreen

Who it suits

Sensitive and normal skin that wants daily protection it will not resent applying. It suits redness-prone faces well and sits reasonably under makeup. Very oily skin may want something drier-finishing; very dry skin may want a cream underneath. Anyone who has reacted to a specific UV filter before should read the current filter list on the pack rather than trusting the word "mild".

☀️ Sun damage & photoageing🌡️ Reactive & easily-stung skin🌡️ Redness & sensitivity🟤 Dark spots & tone

How to use it

Final step of the morning routine, after moisturiser, before makeup. The standard measurement guidance is roughly two finger-lengths for face and neck, and most people apply far less than that. Reapply every couple of hours outdoors. Give it two or three minutes to set before anything goes over the top.

⚠️ Worth knowing: "Mild" is a marketing word with no regulatory meaning, and plenty of people with reactive skin still find sunscreens uncomfortable around the eyes. Formulas and filter lists also change between production runs and export markets, so the tube you buy abroad may not match the review you read. Apply enough or the SPF number on the front is fiction.
