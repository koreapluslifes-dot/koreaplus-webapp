🙋 People also ask

What is glass skin?

Glass skin is a Korean beauty ideal describing a complexion so smooth, even and hydrated it looks translucent and reflective, like a pane of glass. It isn't a single product but the result of diligent hydration, gentle exfoliation and sun protection layered over a healthy skin barrier.

How do you get glass skin?

Hydration layered on damp skin, a healthy barrier, gentle regular exfoliation for smoothness, and daily sunscreen. Glass skin is a lighting effect produced by a smooth, well-hydrated surface — not something a single product delivers.
