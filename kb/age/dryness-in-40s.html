Dryness & dehydration in your 40s: the Korean approach

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Dryness & dehydration in your 40s: Dryness, pigment and loss of firmness tend to arrive together, and cleansing that worked for twenty years can start to feel stripping. The Korean approach leans on Hyaluronic Acid, Ceramides, Panthenol (Pro-B5), introduced one at a time and always finished with daily sunscreen.

What dryness & dehydration looks like here

Ingredients to lean on
