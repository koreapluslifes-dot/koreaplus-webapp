Dullness & glow in your 20s: the Korean approach

What is different about this stage

What dullness & glow looks like here

Ingredients to lean on

🍊 Vitamin C🍋 AHA (Glycolic/Lactic)🧫 Niacinamide (B3)🫚 Ginseng🍶 Galactomyces Ferment
⚠️ Be careful with: Build-up from skipping exfoliation
