Loss of firmness & elasticity in your 30s: the Korean approach

What is different about this stage

Turnover slows and the first cumulative sun damage becomes visible, often alongside breakouts that never fully left.

What loss of firmness & elasticity looks like here

Skin that has lost its snap-back — softening along the jaw and cheeks rather than lines on the surface.

Ingredients to lean on

🔗 Peptides🇰🇷 Adenosine🌙 Retinaldehyde (Retinal)🔷 Copper Peptide (GHK-Cu)🐟 PDRN (Salmon DNA)
⚠️ Be careful with: Smoking and unprotected sun, which drive elastin breakdown
💡 Tip: Be honest about what skincare can reach. Topicals work on the surface and its immediate support; structural laxity is a clinic conversation, and pretending otherwise wastes years.
