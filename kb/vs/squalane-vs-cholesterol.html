Squalane vs Cholesterol: which Korean skincare ingredient?

⚡ Quick answer: Squalane vs Cholesterol: Squalane leans toward dryness & dehydration, while Cholesterol targets damaged moisture barrier. Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

Squalane vs Cholesterol: Squalane leans toward dryness & dehydration, while Cholesterol targets damaged moisture barrier. Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

SqualaneCholesterol
What it isA skin-mimicking emollient (usually plant-derived) that softens without heaviness.Skin's barrier is roughly ceramides, cholesterol and free fatty acids together. That is why barrier creams list all three — supplying only one of the trio is a bit like restocking a pantry with nothing but flour.
Best forDryness & dehydrationDamaged moisture barrier, Dryness & dehydration, Reactive & easily-stung skin
When to useBOTHBOTH

Which should you choose?

Choose Squalane if your priority is Dryness & dehydration; choose Cholesterol for Damaged moisture barrier. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
