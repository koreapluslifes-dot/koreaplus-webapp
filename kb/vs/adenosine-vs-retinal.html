What it isIf you have ever wondered why so many Korean anti-wrinkle creams list the same quiet ingredient, this is it. Adenosine is one of the actives Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety recognises for wrinkle-improvement claims, which is why Korean brands lean on it far more than Western ones.The middle rung of the retinoid ladder. Because it needs fewer conversion steps than retinol, it is generally considered more potent at the same percentage — start low, go slow, and always pair it with sunscreen the next morning. Retinoids are also the one category conventionally avoided altogether in pregnancy and breastfeeding: here, 'caution' means skip it until after.
