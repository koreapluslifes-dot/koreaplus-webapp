Polymethylsilsesquioxane vs Witch Hazel (Hamamelis): which Korean skincare ingredient?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Polymethylsilsesquioxane vs Witch Hazel (Hamamelis): Polymethylsilsesquioxane leans toward pores & texture, while Witch Hazel (Hamamelis) targets oil control. Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

What it isThe 'pore filter' effect in a jar, and the reason a Korean primer can make your nose look airbrushed in about four seconds. These are tiny hard spheres that sit on the surface and bounce light in all directions. Nothing is being treated. Wash it off and the pores are exactly as they were.The tightening sensation is real and almost entirely temporary. Witch hazel's tannins constrict the skin surface for a while, which reads as smaller pores in a mirror. The bigger question is the distillate: many versions carry alcohol, and on already-reactive skin that combination gives you redness dressed up as freshness.

Which should you choose?

Choose Polymethylsilsesquioxane if your priority is Pores & texture; choose Witch Hazel (Hamamelis) for Oil control. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
