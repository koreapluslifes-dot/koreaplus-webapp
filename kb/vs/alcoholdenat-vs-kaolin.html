Alcohol Denat. vs Kaolin Clay: which Korean skincare ingredient?

Alcohol Denat.Kaolin Clay
What it isThe single most argued-about line on a Korean sunscreen label. Denatured alcohol is why that sunscreen feels like nothing and vanishes under makeup — it flashes off and leaves the filters behind. It genuinely stings compromised or sensitised skin. The claim that it damages a healthy barrier at cosmetic levels is far shakier than the internet suggests.The gentlest of the common cosmetic clays, and the one Korean wash-off masks lean on when they want oil control without a stripped face. Kaolin soaks up sebum sitting on the surface. It does not reach into a pore, dissolve a blackhead or shrink anything — those are jobs for a BHA, if they are jobs at all.
Best forOil controlOil control, Pores & texture, Blackheads & sebaceous filaments
When to useBOTHPM

Which should you choose?

Choose Alcohol Denat. if your priority is Oil control; choose Kaolin Clay for Oil control. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
