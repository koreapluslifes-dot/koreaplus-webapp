In skincare it is used as a protective humectant, and it has become a fixture in the recent wave of Korean barrier serums.

Which should you choose?

Choose Centella (Cica) if your priority is Redness & sensitivity; choose Ectoin for Reactive & easily-stung skin. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
