Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride vs Meadowfoam Seed Oil: which Korean skincare ingredient?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride vs Meadowfoam Seed Oil: Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride leans toward dryness & dehydration, while Meadowfoam Seed Oil targets dryness & dehydration. Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

Caprylic/Capric TriglycerideMeadowfoam Seed Oil
What it isCoconut-derived, fractionated, and nothing like coconut oil in behaviour. It is one of the most-used emollients in cosmetics because it is light, spreads beautifully and stays fresh for years. If a Korean cleansing oil or ampoule feels silky rather than greasy, this ester is often the quiet reason.Chemically closer to jojoba than to a typical seed oil, which is why it lasts so well in a formula. Meadowfoam gives a light cushioning finish without the weight of a butter. Nothing about it is corrective — it is a good emollient chosen by formulators who want an oil that will not turn on them.
Best forDryness & dehydration, Damaged moisture barrierDryness & dehydration, Damaged moisture barrier

Which should you choose?

Choose Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride if your priority is Dryness & dehydration; choose Meadowfoam Seed Oil for Dryness & dehydration. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
