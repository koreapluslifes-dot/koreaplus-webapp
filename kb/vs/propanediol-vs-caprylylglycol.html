Propanediol vs Caprylyl Glycol: which Korean skincare ingredient?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Propanediol vs Caprylyl Glycol: Propanediol leans toward dehydration (water-poor skin), while Caprylyl Glycol targets dehydration (water-poor skin). Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

PropanediolCaprylyl Glycol
What it isThe corn-derived glycol that Korean brands reached for once 'glycol-free' became a selling point. Functionally it does what butylene glycol does — a bit of hydration, a lot of solvency — with a slightly softer skin feel. Choosing between them is a formulator's decision about texture and sourcing, not a decision about how well your serum works.A diol that hydrates a little, feels pleasant, and makes preservatives more effective — which is why it appears in the same breath as 1,2-hexanediol on so many Korean labels. Nobody buys a product for caprylyl glycol. It is there so the product can be gentle and still survive six months in a warm bathroom.
Best forDehydration (water-poor skin), Dryness & dehydrationDehydration (water-poor skin)
When to useBOTHBOTH

Which should you choose?

Choose Propanediol if your priority is Dehydration (water-poor skin); choose Caprylyl Glycol for Dehydration (water-poor skin). Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
