Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone) vs Omija (Schisandra): which Korean skincare ingredient?

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone) vs Omija (Schisandra): Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone) leans toward anti-ageing & firmness, while Omija (Schisandra) targets dullness & glow. Can you use both? Yes — they layer well together.

Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone)Omija (Schisandra)
What it isOne of the older anti-ageing actives and still a sensible one. It is oil-soluble and yellow, so it lives in creams and oils rather than watery essences.오미자 (omija) means five-flavour berry, and it has been a 한방 (hanbang) tonic ingredient for centuries. In cosmetics it is a competent botanical antioxidant with a good phenolic profile. Most of the research sits in test tubes rather than on faces, so treat it as supporting cast alongside a vitamin C or niacinamide.
Best forAnti-ageing & firmness, Loss of firmness & elasticity, Dullness & glowDullness & glow, Sun damage & photoageing, Anti-ageing & firmness
When to useBOTHBOTH

Which should you choose?

Choose Coenzyme Q10 (Ubiquinone) if your priority is Anti-ageing & firmness; choose Omija (Schisandra) for Dullness & glow. Many Korean routines use both — yes — they layer well together.
