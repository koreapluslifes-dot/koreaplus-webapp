Cleansing agents (surfactants) in skincare — what they do

Surfactants lift away oil, sunscreen and grime. K-beauty favours mild amino-acid and amphoteric surfactants that clean without stripping.

  • Cocamide DEA — A foam-stabilizing and thickening helper that supports a creamier lather in wash-off products.
  • Cocamide MEA — A coconut-derived helper that supports thicker foam and a richer feel in cleansers.
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine — A gentle coconut-derived cleanser that helps create foam and softens the harshness of stronger surfactants.
