Solvents in skincare — what they do

⚡ Quick answer: Solvents dissolve and carry other ingredients. Some lightweight alcohols also give a fast-absorbing finish (a sensitivity point for some skin).

Solvents dissolve and carry other ingredients. Some lightweight alcohols also give a fast-absorbing finish (a sensitivity point for some skin).

5 solvents in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Denatured Alcohol — A fast-evaporating alcohol that helps products feel light but can be drying with frequent use.
  • Ethanol — A simple alcohol that helps thin formulas and speed drying but can dry out skin over time.
  • SD Alcohol 40-B — A specially denatured alcohol that helps products feel weightless but can be drying for some skin.
  • Water — The base most products are built on; carries and dissolves other ingredients.
  • Water (Aqua) — The base most products are built on; carries and dissolves other ingredients.
