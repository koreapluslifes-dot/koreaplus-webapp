Exfoliants (AHA/BHA/PHA) in skincare — what they do

Chemical exfoliants loosen dead surface cells for smoother, clearer-looking skin. K-beauty tends to favour low, gentle, gradual percentages.

8 exfoliants (aha/bha/pha) in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Citric Acid — A common acid that helps lightly exfoliate and is widely used to set a product's pH.
  • Gluconolactone — A polyhydroxy acid that helps gently smooth skin while also drawing in moisture.
  • Glycolic Acid — A small alpha-hydroxy acid that helps loosen dead surface cells so skin looks smoother and brighter.
  • Lactobionic Acid — A large polyhydroxy acid that helps gently exfoliate and supports the skin's moisture barrier.
  • Malic Acid — A fruit-derived alpha-hydroxy acid that helps loosen dead cells for a smoother surface.
  • Mandelic Acid — A large, slow-acting alpha-hydroxy acid that helps gently exfoliate and is often kinder to sensitive skin.
  • Salicylic Acid — An oil-soluble acid that helps clear inside pores and supports a smoother, clearer complexion.
  • Tartaric Acid — A grape-derived acid that helps with gentle exfoliation and supports a balanced product pH.
