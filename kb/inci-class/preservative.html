Preservatives in skincare — what they do

Preservatives keep water-based formulas safe from microbial growth. Modern, well-tolerated systems are why your essence stays safe for months.

18 preservatives in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Benzoic Acid — A preservative that helps prevent the growth of mold and bacteria to keep a product safe.
  • Benzyl Alcohol — A naturally occurring preservative and solvent that helps protect a product from spoilage.
  • Butylparaben — A preservative that helps guard a formula against bacteria and mold over its shelf life.
  • Chlorphenesin — A preservative that helps protect a product from microbial growth so it keeps fresh through use.
  • Dehydroacetic Acid — A preservative that helps keep a product free of microbes so it stays fresh and safe.
  • Diazolidinyl Urea — A preservative that helps keep a product free of microbes to maintain freshness.
  • DMDM Hydantoin — A preservative that helps prevent bacterial growth so a product stays safe to use.
  • Ethylhexylglycerin — A gentle preservative booster that supports other preservatives while also softening and conditioning the skin.
  • Ethylparaben — A preservative that helps keep a product stable and safe by limiting bacterial growth.
  • Imidazolidinyl Urea — A preservative that helps protect water-based products from bacterial contamination.
  • Methylchloroisothiazolinone — A potent preservative (used with methylisothiazolinone) effective at very low levels; restricted to rinse-off products due to allergy risk.
  • Methylisothiazolinone — A potent preservative used in very small amounts, mainly in rinse-off products; a frequent contact allergen now restricted in leave-on products.
  • Methylparaben — A long-established preservative that helps keep a product free from bacteria and fungus.
  • Phenoxyethanol — A widely used preservative that helps keep a product free of bacteria and mold so it stays safe to use over time.
  • Potassium Sorbate — A mild food-grade preservative that helps stop yeast and mold from growing in a product.
  • Propylparaben — A preservative from the paraben family that helps protect a formula from microbial spoilage.
  • Sodium Benzoate — A gentle preservative that helps keep a formula stable and free from bacteria during its shelf life.
  • Sorbic Acid — A gentle preservative that helps protect a formula against yeast and mold.
