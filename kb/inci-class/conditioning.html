Skin-conditioning agents in skincare — what they do

⚡ Quick answer: Skin-conditioning agents improve how skin looks and feels — versatile multitaskers found across toners, essences and creams.

Skin-conditioning agents improve how skin looks and feels — versatile multitaskers found across toners, essences and creams.

5 skin-conditioning agents in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Bee Venom — A bee-derived ingredient used to give skin a temporarily firmer, smoother-looking appearance; can irritate sensitive skin.
  • Polynucleotides (PN) — A nucleotide-based active that helps support a smoother, more supple, revitalized look.
  • Rice Bran Water — A water-light rice ingredient that hydrates and helps soften the look of the skin.
  • Royal Jelly Extract — A nutrient-rich bee secretion that helps nourish and condition skin for a supple feel.
  • Saccharomyces Ferment Filtrate — A yeast ferment that hydrates and helps skin look more even, soft, and conditioned.
