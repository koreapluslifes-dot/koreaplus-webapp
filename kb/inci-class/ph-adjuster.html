pH adjusters in skincare — what they do

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: pH adjusters fine-tune a formula to skin-friendly acidity, which keeps both the product and your skin barrier happy.

pH adjusters fine-tune a formula to skin-friendly acidity, which keeps both the product and your skin barrier happy.

6 ph adjusters in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Aminomethyl Propanol — A buffering agent that helps set a formula's pH and supports gel thickeners.
  • Potassium Hydroxide — A pH adjuster that helps bring a formula to the right acidity for comfortable use.
  • Sodium Citrate — A buffering salt that helps hold a formula at a steady, skin-friendly pH.
  • Sodium Hydroxide — A pH adjuster used in tiny amounts to help balance a formula to a skin-friendly level.
  • Triethanolamine — A pH adjuster that helps balance acidity and supports a smooth, even texture.
  • Tromethamine — A buffering agent that helps neutralize and stabilize the pH of a gel or lotion.
