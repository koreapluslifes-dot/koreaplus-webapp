Chelators in skincare — what they do

⚡ Quick answer: Chelators bind stray metal ions so formulas stay stable and effective for longer.

Chelators bind stray metal ions so formulas stay stable and effective for longer.

4 chelators in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Disodium EDTA — A chelator that grabs onto stray metal ions to help a product stay stable and work better.
  • Sodium Phytate — A gentle plant-based chelator that helps keep a formula stable by binding stray metal ions.
  • Tetrasodium EDTA — A chelator that binds trace metals in the water to help keep a formula stable and effective.
  • Trisodium Ethylenediamine Disuccinate — A biodegradable chelator that ties up metal ions to help support a formula's stability.
