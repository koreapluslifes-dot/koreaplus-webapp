Brightening actives in skincare — what they do

⚡ Quick answer: Brightening actives target the look of dark spots and uneven tone for a more radiant, even finish.

Brightening actives target the look of dark spots and uneven tone for a more radiant, even finish.

8 brightening actives in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Alpha-Arbutin — A gentle brightening ingredient that helps reduce the look of dark spots for a more even tone.
  • Arbutin — A plant-derived brightener that helps support a more even, radiant-looking complexion.
  • Galactomyces Ferment Filtrate — A yeast ferment that hydrates and helps refine the look of pores, tone, and radiance.
  • Kojic Acid — A fungus-derived ingredient that helps support a brighter, more even-looking tone.
  • Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) — A versatile vitamin B3 that helps brighten, support the moisture barrier, and minimize the look of pores.
  • Rice Ferment Filtrate — A fermented rice liquid that hydrates and helps skin look more even and luminous.
  • Saccharomyces / Rice Ferment Filtrate — A fermented rice-yeast liquid that hydrates and helps support a brighter, smoother look.
  • Tranexamic Acid — An ingredient included to support a more even-looking tone and reduce the look of dark spots.
