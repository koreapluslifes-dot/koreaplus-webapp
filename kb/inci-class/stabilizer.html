Emulsifiers & stabilizers in skincare — what they do

⚡ Quick answer: Emulsifiers and stabilizers hold oil and water together so a cream stays smooth and uniform from first pump to last.

Emulsifiers and stabilizers hold oil and water together so a cream stays smooth and uniform from first pump to last.

6 emulsifiers & stabilizers in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Acrylates/C10-30 Alkyl Acrylate Crosspolymer — A thickening and stabilizing polymer that helps keep oils and water blended into a smooth gel or cream.
  • Carbomer — A gel-forming thickener that helps create a smooth, lightweight texture and stabilizes the formula.
  • Hydroxyethylcellulose — A plant-derived thickener that helps create a smooth gel texture and keeps ingredients suspended.
  • Sodium Metabisulfite — An antioxidant stabilizer that helps keep sensitive ingredients from breaking down in the bottle.
  • Sodium Polyacrylate — A polymer thickener that helps give a formula body and a silky, even feel.
  • Xanthan Gum — A natural thickener that helps give a product a smooth, even texture and keeps ingredients evenly mixed.
