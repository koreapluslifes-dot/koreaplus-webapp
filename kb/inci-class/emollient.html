Emollients & occlusives in skincare — what they do

Emollients soften and smooth the skin surface; occlusives form a light seal that slows water loss — the "lock it in" step after watery layers.

41 emollients & occlusives in the K-beauty INCI decoder

  • Argan Oil — A vitamin-rich oil that conditions and softens skin while helping it feel supple.
  • Avocado Oil — A deeply nourishing oil that softens skin and helps replenish a dry, depleted barrier.
  • Beeswax — A natural wax that thickens products and forms a protective layer to help skin retain moisture.
  • Behenyl Alcohol — A waxy fatty alcohol that thickens creams and leaves skin feeling soft and smooth.
  • Camellia (Tsubaki) Seed Oil — A lightweight nourishing oil that softens skin and helps lock in a smooth, supple feel.
  • Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride — A silky coconut-derived oil that smooths and conditions skin while helping other ingredients spread evenly.
  • Ceramide AP — A barrier-supporting ceramide that helps skin stay resilient and hold onto hydration.
  • Ceramide EOP — A structural ceramide that helps seal the barrier and support long-lasting moisture.
  • Ceramide NP — A skin-identical lipid that helps rebuild and strengthen the moisture barrier for smoother, calmer skin.
  • Cetearyl Alcohol — A creamy fatty alcohol that conditions skin and keeps oil and water blended in a lotion.
  • Cetyl Alcohol — A nourishing fatty alcohol that softens skin and gives creams a smooth, stable texture.
  • Cholesterol — A natural barrier lipid that works with ceramides to keep skin supple and well-protected.
  • Cocoa Butter — A rich plant butter that forms a softening, protective layer to comfort very dry skin.
  • Coconut Alkane / C13-15 Alkane — A light, silicone-free emollient that gives a silky slip and softens skin.
  • Coconut Oil — A rich tropical oil that deeply softens skin and helps slow moisture loss.
  • Cyclomethicone — A fast-spreading silicone blend that adds a smooth, weightless feel and helps products glide on.
  • Cyclopentasiloxane (D5) — A light silicone that gives a silky slip and then evaporates, leaving skin feeling soft and smooth.
  • Dimethicone — A breathable silicone that forms a smooth protective layer to help skin hold onto moisture.
  • Dimethicone Crosspolymer — A bouncy silicone gel that blurs the look of pores and gives a soft, velvety finish.
  • Glyceryl Stearate — A gentle emulsifier that blends oil and water while softening and smoothing the skin.
  • Glyceryl Stearate SE — A self-emulsifying version of glyceryl stearate that creates rich, stable creams and conditions skin.
  • Hydrogenated Polyisobutene — A clean-feeling synthetic oil that adds slip and helps lock in moisture without a greasy finish.
  • Isopropyl Myristate — A fast-absorbing ester that adds slip and a non-greasy softness to lotions and serums.
  • Isopropyl Palmitate — A smooth ester that conditions skin and gives products a light, easily spreadable feel.
  • Jojoba Oil — A wax-like oil very close to skin's natural sebum that conditions and balances without heaviness.
  • Lanolin — A wool-derived wax that closely mimics skin lipids to soften and protect very dry areas.
  • Mineral Oil — A gentle, non-irritating oil that forms a barrier to slow water loss and keep skin soft.
  • Olive Oil — A rich, antioxidant-containing oil that softens and helps protect dry skin from moisture loss.
  • PEG-7 Glyceryl Cocoate — A mild cleansing helper that supports softer foam and helps leave a conditioned feel on skin.
  • Petrolatum — A classic protective ointment that seals in water to help very dry or chapped skin stay hydrated.
  • Phytosphingosine — A barrier lipid that helps support ceramide production and keeps skin feeling calm and balanced.
  • Rosehip Oil — A fatty-acid-rich oil that softens skin and helps support a smooth, even-looking complexion.
  • Sea Buckthorn Oil — A nourishing orange oil that helps soften skin and support a comfortable, conditioned feel.
  • Shea Butter — A rich plant butter that deeply softens and supports the skin barrier, ideal for dry, tight skin.
  • Squalane — A lightweight oil that mimics skin's own lipids to soften and lock in moisture without feeling greasy.
  • Stearic Acid — A fatty acid that helps emulsify creams and leaves a soft, conditioned feel on skin.
  • Stearyl Alcohol — A softening fatty alcohol that thickens formulas and helps leave skin feeling smooth.
  • Sunflower Oil — A light, antioxidant-rich oil that softens skin and helps reinforce its moisture barrier.
  • Sweet Almond Oil — A mild, nourishing oil that softens and soothes skin while supporting its moisture barrier.
  • Vitamin E — A skin-loving antioxidant that helps protect oils from going off while softening and conditioning skin.
  • Vitamin E Acetate — A stable form of vitamin E that conditions skin and helps defend against everyday environmental stress.
