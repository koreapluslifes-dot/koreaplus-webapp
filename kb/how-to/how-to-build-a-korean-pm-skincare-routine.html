How to Build a Korean Evening Skincare Routine

The evening routine is where Korean skincare focuses on cleansing away the day and supporting overnight recovery. Without the constraint of sunscreen, the PM routine is a good time for treatment-focused steps.

Cleanse Thoroughly

Evening is when double cleansing earns its place: an oil-based cleanser to dissolve sunscreen and makeup, then a water-based cleanser to finish. This leaves a clean base so the products that follow can work without sitting on residue.

Treat and Hydrate

Apply a hydrating toner or essence, then your treatment step. Evening is a common time for actives such as retinoids or exfoliating acids, used on alternating nights rather than together, to reduce irritation. Layer a serum or ampoule for targeted concerns, then a moisturizer.

Seal It In

Finish with a richer night cream, or on dry nights a sleeping pack or a thin occlusive layer to help reduce overnight moisture loss. Introduce any new active gradually and patch-test first. Keep potent actives separated across different nights so your barrier has time to recover, and listen to your skin if it feels irritated.

Key facts
  • Double cleanse at night to remove sunscreen and makeup
  • Evening suits treatment steps like retinoids or acids
  • Use potent actives on alternating nights, not together
  • Seal with a night cream or, on dry nights, a sleeping pack
