How to Do the Korean Double Cleanse Step by Step

⚡ Quick answer: Master the two-step Korean double cleanse: an oil-based cleanser followed by a water-based cleanser, with tips on technique and frequency.

The double cleanse is a two-step method popularized in Korean skincare: first an oil-based cleanser to dissolve oil-soluble debris, then a water-based cleanser to remove the rest. It is especially useful for removing sunscreen and makeup.

Step One: Oil-Based Cleanser

Start on dry skin with dry hands. Massage a cleansing oil or balm over your face for around a minute so it can dissolve oil-based grime such as sunscreen, makeup, and sebum. Add a little water to emulsify it into a milky texture, then rinse. This first step is what makes the second cleanse more effective.

Step Two: Water-Based Cleanser

Follow with a gentle water-based (foaming or gel) cleanser to lift away sweat, dirt, and any remaining residue. Use lukewarm water rather than hot, and avoid scrubbing harshly. The goal is clean, comfortable skin, not a tight or squeaky feeling, which can signal over-cleansing.

How Often and Who Benefits

Double cleansing is most worthwhile in the evening, particularly if you wear sunscreen or makeup. In the morning, a single gentle cleanse (or just water) is often enough. If your skin feels dry or irritated, you may be cleansing too aggressively; scale back. Patch-test new cleansers and choose a fragrance-free option if you are sensitive.

Key facts
  • Step one is an oil-based cleanser on dry skin to dissolve oil-soluble debris
  • Step two is a water-based cleanser to remove the rest
  • Most useful at night, especially after sunscreen or makeup
  • Use lukewarm water and avoid harsh scrubbing
