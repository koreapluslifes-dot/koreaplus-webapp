In what order? Vitamin C + Hyaluronic Acid + Ceramides

⚡ Quick answer: The optimal order for Vitamin C, Hyaluronic Acid, Ceramides is: Vitamin C → Hyaluronic Acid → Ceramides. Apply thinnest to thickest, low-pH actives first, oils last — the Korean barrier-friendly way.

1

3

⏳ Wait ~15–20 minutes after the low-pH step (acid or vitamin C) so it can work before the next layer.
☀️ Finish every morning with SPF, whatever you layered.
