Purging or just breaking out? How to tell the difference

⏱ 1 min read
⚡ Quick answer: Purging is real but narrower than the internet suggests. The mechanism, and its limits

Ingredients that accelerate turnover bring already-forming congestion to the surface sooner than it would have arrived. That is purging. It requires an ingredient that actually does this — retinoids and acids. A moisturiser cannot cause purging, and a brand claiming otherwise is explaining away a reaction.

The two tests

Location and time. Purging tends to appear where you normally congest, because that is where the existing blockages are — a useful rule of thumb rather than a test that settles it, since blockages you cannot yet see also count. And it should resolve within a turnover cycle or two: commonly four to six weeks for acids, sometimes eight or more for a retinoid. New locations, or trouble still building past that, points to irritation or a reaction.

What to do while it happens

Reduce frequency rather than stopping outright, keep the rest of the routine simple and barrier-supporting, and be patient. Pushing through with daily use at full strength usually makes it worse and longer.

When to stop entirely

Stinging, swelling, spreading redness or hives are not purging under any definition. Stop, simplify, and see a professional if it does not settle.

What is the difference between essence and serum?

In Korean skincare, essence is typically a thinner, hydration-focused layer that preps skin, while serum is more concentrated with targeted actives like vitamin C or niacinamide. Essence usually comes first; serum follows. The line between them blurs, and not every routine needs both.

What is the difference between AHA and BHA?

AHAs like glycolic and lactic acid are water-soluble and work on the skin's surface to brighten and smooth, while BHA like salicylic acid is oil-soluble and gets into pores. In Korean skincare both are used sparingly, a few times weekly, always with daily sun protection.

What is the difference between essence, serum and ampoule?

Essence is the lightest and most hydration-focused, serum is more concentrated and targeted, and ampoule is the most concentrated of the three. The boundaries are marketing convention, not regulated definitions.
