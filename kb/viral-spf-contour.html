Sunscreen “contouring”: viral claim vs the evidence

💨 More hype than proof

Viral claim: "Skip SPF on some areas to “contour” with a tan."

A genuinely harmful trend — uneven sunscreen means uneven UV damage and pigmentation, the opposite of healthy skin. Always apply SPF evenly to the whole face.
