Korean Beauty's Presence at Global Trade Shows

International beauty trade shows are a key B2B channel where Korean brands, manufacturers, and ingredient suppliers meet global buyers, distributors, and potential ODM clients.

Why trade shows matter

Beauty is a relationship-driven B2B business behind the consumer brands. Trade shows let Korean exporters showcase finished products, contract-manufacturing (ODM/OEM) capabilities, and ingredients to international retailers, distributors, and brand owners in one place. For smaller Korean firms, a show booth can be a cost-effective way to access overseas buyers without a foreign sales office.

Major show types

Globally recognized events include Cosmoprof (a large international beauty trade fair held in cities such as Bologna and Las Vegas) and in-cosmetics (focused on cosmetic ingredients and formulation). Korea also hosts its own beauty expos. Korean national trade-promotion bodies have historically supported group pavilions to help domestic companies exhibit abroad—an approach common to many exporting nations.

What gets transacted

At these events, deals range from distribution agreements and private-label/ODM contracts to ingredient sourcing and brand partnerships. Trade shows also serve as trend-scouting venues, where buyers spot the next textures, formats, or actives. They are part of the unglamorous B2B infrastructure that underpins K-beauty's visible consumer success.

Key facts
  • Trade shows are a key B2B channel for Korean beauty exporters.
  • Cosmoprof and in-cosmetics are major international events.
  • Shows connect brands with buyers, distributors, and ODM clients.
  • National trade bodies often support group pavilions abroad.
  • Deals include distribution, private-label/ODM, and ingredient sourcing.
