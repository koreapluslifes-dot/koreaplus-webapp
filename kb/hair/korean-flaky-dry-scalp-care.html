Soothing a Flaky, Dry Scalp the Korean Way 건성·각질 두피

⚡ Quick answer: Korean care for a flaky, dry scalp pairs gentle low-pH cleansing with hydrating scalp tonics rich in soothing botanicals like centella (cica) and heartleaf. Hydration over harsh scrubbing

A dry, flaky scalp needs moisture and gentleness, which is exactly where Korean scalp care excels. Rather than scrubbing aggressively, the Korean approach leans on mild, low-pH shampoos and hydrating leave-on tonics. Soothing botanicals beloved in Korea — centella asiatica (cica), heartleaf, and panthenol — are used to support a calm, comfortable scalp and reduce the appearance of dryness and visible flakes.

Build a moisture barrier-friendly routine

Choose sulfate-light, fragrance-conscious shampoos and avoid very hot water, which can leave the scalp feeling tighter and drier. After washing, apply a hydrating scalp serum or ampoule to a towel-dried scalp to lock in comfort. Ingredients like hyaluronic acid, ceramides, and madecassoside (from centella) are common in Korean scalp lines and help support a hydrated, supple-feeling scalp.

Gentle weekly exfoliation

Some flaking comes from dead-skin buildup, so a very gentle weekly scalp scaler can help — but only the soothing, hydrating kind, not abrasive scrubs. Korean enzyme or mild PHA scalers loosen flakes while staying kind to a dry scalp. Always follow with hydration. This balanced rhythm supports a comfortable scalp and a cleaner, healthier-looking part line.
